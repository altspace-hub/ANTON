# Investment & Asset Management — Area Context

## Domain Overview

This area covers investment analysis, portfolio management, fund compliance, and regulatory advisory for investment managers, asset managers, wealth managers, and their clients. The investment management sector is subject to extensive regulation — MiFID II, AIFMD, UCITS, SFDR, EMIR — and is undergoing rapid change from sustainable finance requirements, digitalisation, and evolving client expectations.

## Regulatory Framework

### MiFID II / MiFIR (Markets in Financial Instruments Directive/Regulation)
The foundational regulation for investment firms and markets:
- **Authorisation and organisational requirements** — Capital, systems, governance, compliance function
- **Client classification** — Retail, Professional, Eligible Counterparty (different protection levels)
- **Conduct obligations** — Suitability, appropriateness, best execution, conflicts of interest, inducements
- **Product governance** — Target market identification, distribution chain obligations (POG)
- **Reporting** — Transaction reporting (to NCA), transparency (pre/post-trade), record-keeping (5 years)
- **Research** — Unbundling (MIFID II), partially re-bundled (MiFID III discussions)

### UCITS — Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities
Framework for retail investment funds. Key requirements:
- Eligible assets, diversification rules (5/10/40 rule, issuer concentration limits)
- Liquidity management — minimum weekly redemption
- KIID/KID — Key Information Document for investor disclosure
- Depositary function — safekeeping and oversight

### AIFMD — Alternative Investment Fund Managers Directive
Framework for alternative fund managers (hedge funds, private equity, real estate funds):
- Authorisation thresholds and requirements
- Leverage limits and reporting
- Investor disclosure and transparency
- Depositary requirements
- NPPR (National Private Placement Regime) for marketing outside home state

### SFDR — Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation
Investment product sustainability classification:
- **Article 6** — No sustainability integration claim; risk disclosure only
- **Article 8** — Promotes environmental/social characteristics ("light green")
- **Article 9** — Has sustainable investment as objective ("dark green")
- **PAI indicators** — Principal Adverse Impact on sustainability factors; mandatory for large managers
- **Entity-level** — Policy on integration of sustainability risks (Article 3)

### EMIR — European Market Infrastructure Regulation
For derivatives users:
- **Clearing obligation** — Standardised OTC derivatives must be centrally cleared via CCP
- **Margin requirements** — Variation margin (daily), initial margin (for non-cleared)
- **Trade reporting** — All derivatives reported to Trade Repository
- **Risk mitigation** — Timely confirmation, portfolio reconciliation, dispute resolution

### PRIIPs / KID — Packaged Retail and Insurance-based Investment Products
Standardised disclosure document for retail packaged products:
- 3-page maximum
- Performance scenarios, risk indicator, costs (RIY)
- Retail investor protection focus

## Investment Analysis Frameworks

### Fundamental Analysis
- **Quantitative** — Financial ratios, valuation models (DCF, comparables, precedent transactions)
- **Qualitative** — Management quality, competitive moat, industry dynamics, ESG factors
- **Macro** — Interest rate sensitivity, currency exposure, geopolitical risk

### Ratio Analysis (Key Metrics)
| Metric | Formula | What it shows |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| P/E Ratio | Price / EPS | Market's earnings multiple |
| EV/EBITDA | Enterprise Value / EBITDA | Unlevered valuation multiple |
| ROE | Net Income / Equity | Return on equity |
| Leverage (Net Debt/EBITDA) | Net Debt / EBITDA | Debt burden relative to earnings |
| Interest Coverage | EBIT / Interest Expense | Ability to service debt |
| FCF Yield | FCF per share / Price | Cash generation relative to price |

### Portfolio Analysis
- **Risk-adjusted returns** — Sharpe ratio (return / volatility), Sortino ratio (return / downside volatility)
- **Correlation analysis** — How assets move relative to each other and the market
- **Factor exposure** — Value, Growth, Momentum, Quality, Size (Fama-French factors)
- **Scenario analysis** — Portfolio performance under different market conditions

## Key Compliance Issues in Asset Management

1. **Suitability** — Does the recommended portfolio match the client's risk profile, objectives, and knowledge?
2. **Appropriateness** — For execution-only, does the client understand the product?
3. **Conflicts of interest** — Related party transactions, soft commissions, employee personal account dealing
4. **Product governance** — Is the product distributed to its intended target market?
5. **Market abuse** — Insider dealing, market manipulation, front-running controls
6. **AML/KYC** — CDD on investors; source of funds verification; beneficial ownership
7. **SFDR classification integrity** — Is Article 8/9 claim sustainable under regulatory scrutiny?
