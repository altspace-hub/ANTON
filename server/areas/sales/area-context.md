# B2B Sales Strategy & Execution Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Sales Strategy & Execution area covers the full B2B sales cycle — from lead qualification and pipeline management through deal structuring, proposal writing, pricing strategy, competitive analysis, and customer success. It serves sales directors, enterprise account executives, sales managers, revenue operations professionals, and customer success teams in B2B environments selling complex, high-value solutions. The domain is grounded in rigorous deal qualification, evidence-based forecasting, and the commercial mechanics of winning and retaining large accounts.

## Core Sales Domains

**Pipeline Management and Forecasting:**
- Sales funnel architecture: stage definitions, entry and exit criteria, conversion rates
- Opportunity scoring: weighted pipeline, commit vs. best case vs. pipeline categories
- CRM hygiene and data quality: what good looks like by stage
- Sales forecasting methodologies: bottom-up call, run-rate analysis, regression-based models
- Pipeline review cadences: weekly deal reviews, monthly forecasts, quarterly business reviews (QBR)

**Deal Qualification:**
- MEDDIC framework: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion
- MEDDPICC: adds Paper Process and Competition for enterprise complexity
- SPIN Selling (Neil Rackham): Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff questioning framework
- Challenger Sale model: teach, tailor, take control — challenging customer thinking to create differentiated value
- Budget qualification: confirmed vs. assumed budget, budget cycle timing, competing priorities
- Economic buyer access: distinguishing champions from economic buyers, multi-threading strategy

**Proposal Writing and Value Selling:**
- Executive summary structure: business problem → proposed solution → expected outcomes → investment
- ROI/business case development: quantifying value using customer's own metrics
- Competitive differentiation: why us vs. alternatives vs. doing nothing
- Proposal customisation: mirroring customer language, referencing discovery insights
- Proposal review process: internal red team, champion review, executive alignment

**Pricing Strategy:**
- Value-based pricing: anchoring price to quantified customer value, not cost-plus
- Pricing architecture: land-and-expand, platform plus modules, per-seat vs. usage, enterprise licences
- Discount strategy: discount authority levels, discount tracking, protecting margin
- Multi-year deal structures: annual vs. multi-year commitments, price escalation provisions
- Competitive pricing response: when to match and when to hold on price

**Competitive Intelligence:**
- Win/loss analysis: structured debrief methodology, pattern identification across deals
- Competitive battlecards: objection handling, differentiation points, attack and defend strategies
- Market positioning: where you play, where you choose not to play
- Analysing competitor movements: pricing changes, product updates, sales team activity

**Customer Success and Renewals:**
- Customer health scoring: product usage, support tickets, executive engagement, NPS/CSAT
- Renewal risk assessment: early warning signals — low adoption, champion departure, budget pressure
- Expansion strategy: land-and-expand, cross-sell and upsell frameworks
- Executive Business Reviews (EBR): structure, success metrics, forward-looking agenda
- Churn prevention: root cause analysis, save playbooks, retention economics

## Key Frameworks and Standards

- **MEDDIC/MEDDPICC** — Qualification framework for complex B2B enterprise sales
- **SPIN Selling (Rackham)** — Needs-based questioning methodology
- **Challenger Sale (Dixon & Adamson)** — Teaching, tailoring, taking control to disrupt customer thinking
- **Command of the Message (Force Management)** — Required capabilities, positive business outcomes, metrics
- **Value Selling Framework** — Anchoring price and positioning to quantified customer business outcomes
- **Customer Success Platform Methodology (Gainsight/Totango)** — Health scoring, playbooks, lifecycle management

## Major Challenges

- Accurate pipeline forecasting when rep self-reporting is optimistic
- Reaching economic buyers through gatekeeping champions
- Differentiating in commoditised or highly competitive markets
- Protecting price in negotiations without losing deals unnecessarily
- Scaling win/loss learning across large, distributed sales organisations
- Identifying renewal risk early enough to remediate before the 90-day renewal window

## Analytical Principles

1. **Qualification is kindness** — Pursuing unwinnable deals wastes resource on both sides; early disqualification creates capacity for winnable ones
2. **Economic buyer access is mandatory** — Deals without economic buyer engagement rarely close at full value
3. **Value before price** — Quantify ROI before discussing investment; price without value context invites negotiation on price alone
4. **Losing well is learning** — Rigorous win/loss analysis compounds into competitive advantage over time
5. **Renewal is sold at implementation** — Customer success outcomes must be delivered and measured from Day 1, not rescued at renewal
