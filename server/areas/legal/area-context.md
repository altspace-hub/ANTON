# Legal & Regulatory Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Legal & Regulatory area covers the full spectrum of legal analysis, regulatory interpretation, and compliance framework design that professionals in financial services, corporate, and advisory contexts require. This includes reading and applying legislation, reviewing contracts, building compliance programmes, and navigating the intersection of law and business operations.

## Regulatory Framework

**EU Financial Services Legislation:**
- AMLR 2024/1624, AMLD6, AMLA framework
- MiFID II / MiFIR, PSD2 / PSD3, PSR
- DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) 2022/2554
- MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation) 2023/1114
- CRR3 / CRD6, Solvency II, PRIIPS
- GDPR (Regulation 2016/679), ePrivacy Directive

**Regulatory Hierarchy:**
- EU Regulation (directly applicable) → EU Directive (national transposition required) → Delegated Acts → RTS/ITS (Regulatory/Implementing Technical Standards) → EBA/ESMA/EIOPA Guidelines (comply-or-explain) → National supervisory guidance (Q&A, Dear CEO letters)

**Nordic Regulatory Environment:**
- Sweden: Finansinspektionen (FI), FFFS regulatory codes
- Finland: Finanssivalvonta (FIN-FSA)
- Denmark: Finanstilsynet
- Norway: Finanstilsynet
- National implementations diverge on transposition choices — always verify national law

**Contract Law:**
- Nordic countries use civil law tradition with specific national contract acts
- EBA Guidelines on Outsourcing and ICT Risk impose contractual obligations on financial institutions
- DORA Articles 30+ specify minimum ICT contract requirements

## Key Terminology

- **RTS/ITS** — Regulatory/Implementing Technical Standards (binding delegated legislation under ESA mandates)
- **ESA** — European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, ESMA, EIOPA)
- **Comply-or-explain** — Guidelines that require compliance or documented reasons for non-compliance
- **Proportionality** — Regulatory principle that obligations scale with entity size, complexity, and risk profile
- **Lex specialis** — More specific law overrides general law where they conflict
- **Purposive interpretation** — Interpreting law based on legislative intent, not just literal text

## Analytical Principles

When working in the Legal & Regulatory domain:
1. **Distinguish obligation types** — "shall" (binding) vs. "should" (best practice) vs. "may" (permissive)
2. **Check the regulatory hierarchy** — A guideline cannot override a regulation; national law cannot override directly applicable EU law
3. **Read recitals** — Recitals in EU legislation explain legislative intent and are critical for interpretation
4. **Verify current status** — Legislation is amended frequently; always confirm version and effective date
5. **This is analysis, not legal advice** — Flag when qualified legal counsel should be consulted
6. **Nordic context matters** — National supervisory expectations in the Nordics often exceed the EU minimum
