# Insurance & Reinsurance Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Insurance & Reinsurance area covers general insurance, life and health insurance, reinsurance, Islamic insurance (Takaful), and the regulatory frameworks that govern them — including Solvency II, IFRS 17, the Insurance Distribution Directive, and IFSB standards. It serves insurance executives, actuaries, compliance officers, underwriters, risk managers, claims professionals, and reinsurance structuring teams across European, UK, and international markets.

## Core Insurance Domains

**Solvency II:**
- Three Pillars: Pillar 1 (quantitative requirements), Pillar 2 (governance/ORSA), Pillar 3 (reporting/disclosure)
- Solvency Capital Requirement (SCR): standard formula and internal model approaches
- Minimum Capital Requirement (MCR)
- Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA): forward-looking, stress-tested capital planning
- Supervisory Review Process and EIOPA guidelines
- UK Solvency UK (post-Brexit reforms): matching adjustment, risk margin, internal model approvals

**IFRS 17 Implementation:**
- Three measurement models: General Measurement Model (GMM/BBA), Premium Allocation Approach (PAA), Variable Fee Approach (VFA)
- Contractual Service Margin (CSM): recognition and release patterns
- Loss Component recognition and onerous contract testing
- Transition methods: full retrospective, modified retrospective, fair value approach
- Interaction with IFRS 9 (financial assets) and IFRS 4 Phase II legacy challenges
- Disclosure requirements and investor communication

**Product Governance (IDD):**
- Insurance Distribution Directive (IDD) product oversight and governance (POG) requirements
- Product Approval Process (PAP): target market identification, distribution strategy
- Product review triggers and annual review obligations
- Value for money assessment and fair customer outcomes (aligned to Consumer Duty)

**Actuarial Communications:**
- Translating actuarial analysis for boards, regulators, and non-technical audiences
- Reserve adequacy opinions and actuarial reports
- Pricing basis documentation and technical pricing memoranda
- Stress and scenario testing results communication

**Claims Analysis:**
- Large loss review and reserving adequacy assessment
- Claims trends analysis: frequency, severity, inflation
- Litigation and settlement strategy
- Fraud detection patterns and referral frameworks

**Reinsurance:**
- Treaty structures: proportional (quota share, surplus) and non-proportional (XL, stop loss)
- Reinsurance programme design: retention optimisation, capital efficiency, catastrophe protection
- Reinsurer security assessment and panel management
- Commutation analysis and run-off strategy

**Takaful (Islamic Insurance):**
- Takaful principles: mutual guarantee (Ta'awun), risk-sharing, prohibition of gharar and maysir
- Takaful models: Wakala, Mudaraba, hybrid Wakala-Waqf
- IFSB-8: Guiding principles on governance of Takaful undertakings
- AAOIFI FAS 12: Takaful financial reporting
- Regulatory frameworks: DFSA, BNM (Malaysia), Saudi Arabia SAMA/AMSB

## Key Frameworks and Standards

- **Solvency II (Directive 2009/138/EC)** — EU insurance capital and governance regime
- **IFRS 17** — Insurance contracts accounting standard (effective January 2023)
- **IDD (Directive 2016/97/EU)** — Insurance distribution and conduct regulation
- **IFSB-8** — Takaful governance and risk management standards
- **Lloyd's Market Bulletins** — Delegated authority, coverholder management, performance management

## Major Challenges

- IFRS 17 system implementation complexity and CSM modelling accuracy
- Solvency II internal model validation and regulatory approval maintenance
- Product governance documentation at scale across diverse distribution channels
- Communicating SCR volatility and balance sheet sensitivity to boards
- Structuring reinsurance programmes that provide genuine capital relief under Solvency II
- Takaful surplus distribution policies balancing participant equity and Operator economics

## How the Modules Help

**Solvency-ii** supports ORSA narrative preparation, SCR analysis, and supervisory communication. **Ifrs17-implementation** guides measurement model selection, CSM recognition, and transition approach documentation. **Product-governance** structures POG frameworks and target market definitions. **Actuarial-comms** translates complex actuarial outputs for non-specialist audiences. **Claims-analysis** structures large loss investigations and reserving communications. **Reinsurance-program-review** evaluates treaty structures and capital efficiency. **Idd-compliance** ensures distribution and conduct obligations are met. **Takaful-product-design** and **takaful-regulatory** support Shari'a-compliant product development and regulatory compliance.

## Analytical Principles

1. **Capital adequacy is not a target — it is a floor** — Sound risk management maintains meaningful buffers above regulatory minimums
2. **Policyholder protection is the ultimate purpose** — Regulatory requirements exist to protect customers; compliance should reflect this intent
3. **Actuarial independence** — Actuarial opinions must be objective; commercial pressure does not change technical reality
4. **Transparency with supervisors** — Proactive, open engagement with regulators builds the trust necessary for efficient supervision
5. **Takaful distinctiveness** — Takaful structures must be genuinely Shari'a-compliant, not conventional insurance with Islamic branding
