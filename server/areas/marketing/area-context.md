# Marketing & Digital Marketing Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Marketing & Digital Marketing area covers the full spectrum of modern marketing practice: from overarching brand and go-to-market strategy through to granular digital execution, performance measurement, and customer lifecycle management. It serves marketing managers, digital strategists, growth teams, brand owners, and business leaders who need to connect commercial objectives to audience behaviour.

## Core Frameworks

**Strategic Planning Frameworks:**
- SOSTAC (Situation, Objectives, Strategy, Tactics, Actions, Control) — Dave Chaffey's structured planning model
- RACE Framework (Reach, Act, Convert, Engage) — lifecycle-oriented digital marketing planning
- ANSOFF Matrix — market penetration, product development, market development, diversification
- Porter's Five Forces — competitive positioning and market attractiveness
- Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) — understanding what customers are genuinely trying to accomplish

**Customer-Centric Approaches:**
- Customer Journey Mapping — touchpoint analysis across Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Retention, Advocacy
- Persona Development — behavioural, psychographic, and demographic audience modelling
- Voice of the Customer (VoC) — qualitative and quantitative feedback integration
- Net Promoter Score (NPS), Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), Customer Effort Score (CES)

**Performance and Attribution:**
- Multi-touch attribution models: first-touch, last-touch, linear, time-decay, data-driven
- Marketing Mix Modelling (MMM) — econometric measurement of channel contribution
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV/LTV) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) — the foundational growth equation
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Cost per Acquisition (CPA), Cost per Lead (CPL)
- Incrementality testing — holdout experiments to measure true causal lift

## Key Terminology

- **Top-of-funnel (TOFU) / Mid-funnel (MOFU) / Bottom-of-funnel (BOFU)** — awareness, consideration, and conversion stages
- **Owned / Earned / Paid media** — content assets, organic reach, and paid placements
- **CAC:LTV ratio** — the fundamental unit economics test (healthy SaaS: LTV > 3× CAC)
- **Demand generation vs. lead generation** — creating market awareness vs. capturing intent
- **Account-Based Marketing (ABM)** — B2B approach targeting specific high-value accounts
- **Programmatic advertising** — automated, real-time media buying via DSP/SSP ecosystems
- **First-party data** — customer data owned directly by the business (essential in a cookieless era)

## Analytical Principles

When working in the Marketing domain:
1. **Audience first** — Every strategic decision begins with a clear, evidence-based understanding of who you are trying to reach and what they need
2. **Objectives drive channel selection** — Channel tactics must follow objectives, not the reverse; never lead with "we need to be on TikTok"
3. **Test, measure, iterate** — Marketing is inherently empirical; hypotheses must be testable and results must feed back into strategy
4. **Attribution is imperfect** — No single attribution model captures the full truth; triangulate across models and methods
5. **B2B and B2C differ fundamentally** — Sales cycles, buying committee dynamics, content needs, and KPIs diverge sharply between sectors
6. **Budget follows performance data** — Resource allocation should be evidence-driven and regularly rebalanced, not locked into annual plans
7. **Brand and performance are complementary** — Long-term brand investment and short-term performance marketing reinforce each other (Binet & Field: 60/40 principle)
