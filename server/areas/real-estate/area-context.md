# Real Estate & Property Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Real Estate & Property area covers investment analysis, property valuation, lease review and negotiation, planning and development assessment, and acquisition due diligence. It serves real estate investors, fund managers, asset managers, developers, property companies, and their legal and financial advisors. The domain spans commercial real estate (office, retail, industrial, logistics, alternatives) and residential investment, with primary focus on UK and European markets.

## Core Real Estate Domains

**Investment Analysis:**
- Net Initial Yield (NIY), Equivalent Yield, and Reversionary Yield calculations
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and Net Present Value (NPV) analysis: projecting rental income, void periods, capex, exit value
- Internal Rate of Return (IRR): levered and unlevered returns
- Capital structure and financing: LTV ratios, debt service coverage (DSCR), interest rate sensitivity
- Investment appraisal models: residual development appraisal, gross development value (GDV)
- Market analysis: supply/demand dynamics, comparable transactions, rental growth assumptions
- Portfolio analysis: diversification, sector allocation, geographic weighting, income resilience

**Property Valuation:**
- RICS Red Book (Global Valuation Standards): bases of value — market value, investment value, fair value
- Valuation methods: comparative method, income capitalisation, DCF, cost method, residual method
- Market value assessments for acquisition, disposal, and fund reporting
- Loan security valuations and monitor valuations
- Development appraisal: residual land value, developer's profit, s106/CIL obligations
- Reinstatement cost assessments for insurance purposes

**Lease Review and Negotiation:**
- UK commercial lease structure: FRI (full repairing and insuring) basis, privity of contract, alienation
- Key lease terms: break clauses (conditions, notice requirements, vacant possession), rent review provisions (upward-only, open market, RPI/CPI-linked), service charge obligations
- Dilapidations: terminal dilapidations, Scott Schedule preparation, Jervis v Harris clauses
- Landlord and Tenant Act 1954: security of tenure, lease renewal, opposed/unopposed applications
- Agreement for Lease (AFL): conditions precedent, longstop dates, developer obligations
- Heads of Terms (HoTs): structure, key commercial points, without prejudice status

**Planning and Development:**
- UK planning system: National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), Local Plans, development management
- Use class order: Class E (commercial), Class C3 (residential), Class C1 (hotels), Sui Generis
- Planning application types: full, outline, prior approval, permitted development rights
- Section 106 agreements and Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL)
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA): screening, scoping, environmental statement
- Heritage and conservation: listed buildings, conservation areas, planning policy weight

**Property Due Diligence:**
- Legal due diligence: title investigation, searches (local authority, drainage, environmental), restrictive covenants
- Physical due diligence: structural surveys, M&E condition reports, environmental surveys (Phase 1/Phase 2)
- Commercial due diligence: tenant covenant strength, lease profile analysis, void risk, capex requirements
- Financial due diligence: rent roll verification, service charge reconciliation, tax structure review
- ESG due diligence: EPC ratings, BREEAM/LEED certifications, embodied carbon, climate transition risk

## Key Frameworks and Standards

- **RICS Red Book** — Global valuation professional standards
- **RICS Code of Practice for Service Charges** — Commercial property service charge management
- **IPD/MSCI Property Index** — Benchmark for UK and European real estate performance
- **Landlord and Tenant Act 1954** — UK statutory security of tenure for commercial tenants
- **NPPF (National Planning Policy Framework)** — UK planning policy framework

## Major Challenges

- Modelling long-term income with material uncertainty in rental growth, void periods, and interest rates
- Negotiating leases where landlord and tenant incentives are fundamentally misaligned
- Navigating an increasingly complex planning environment with extended timescales
- Quantifying climate transition risk (stranded asset risk from EPC/energy requirements) in investment decisions
- Conducting thorough due diligence on complex portfolios within compressed transaction timescales

## How the Modules Help

## Analytical Principles

1. **Location drives value** — Micro-location quality, connectivity, and amenity are the primary value determinants
2. **Cash flow is king** — The quality, security, and durability of income drives institutional valuation
3. **Lease structure determines risk** — The difference between a great and an acceptable property is often in the lease details
4. **Due diligence prevents disasters** — The cost of thorough due diligence is trivial relative to the cost of a concealed defect post-acquisition
5. **ESG is now financial risk** — Energy performance and transition risk are material to long-term asset values, not optional enhancements
