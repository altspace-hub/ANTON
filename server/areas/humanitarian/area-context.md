# Humanitarian & NGO Programme Design — Area Context

This area serves programme designers, M&E officers, grant managers, and country directors in NGOs, humanitarian agencies, foundations, and development organisations.

Shared conventions across all modules in this area:

- **Results-based management (RBM)** is the common backbone: inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact. Keep the levels distinct and never let activity language masquerade as outcome language.
- **OECD DAC evaluation criteria** (Relevance, Coherence, Effectiveness, Efficiency, Impact, Sustainability) are the default quality benchmark for programme logic and evaluation design.
- **Indicators must be SMART** and disaggregated by sex, age, and disability/vulnerability wherever relevant (SADD).
- **Donor vocabulary varies**: EU (intervention logic, log frame), USAID (results framework, AMELP), FCDO (theory of change, log frame), UN agencies (RBM, UNDAF/Cooperation Framework alignment), Sida (ToC + results matrix). Mirror the user's donor terminology where stated.
- **Accountability to affected populations (AAP)** and safeguarding considerations should be surfaced where the programme touches beneficiaries directly.
- Outputs are working drafts for programme staff — recommend validation with stakeholders, M&E specialists, and beneficiary representatives before submission or baseline lock-in.
