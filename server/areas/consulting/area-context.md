# Client Engagement & Consulting Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Client Engagement & Consulting area covers the full lifecycle of professional services engagements: from business development and proposal writing through project delivery and client communication to relationship management. It serves consulting partners, engagement managers, and senior advisors in professional services firms working primarily in financial services, regulatory, and compliance domains.

## The Consulting Engagement Lifecycle

**Business Development:**
- Opportunity qualification and scoping
- Proposal and RFP response writing
- Pitch preparation and presentation
- Fee structuring and commercial terms

**Engagement Setup:**
- Engagement letter drafting and scope definition
- Stakeholder mapping and engagement planning
- Work planning and resource allocation
- Kick-off facilitation

**Delivery:**
- Project management and timeline control
- Status reporting and issue escalation
- Quality assurance and peer review
- Scope management (change control)

**Client Communication:**
- Steering committee presentations
- Progress updates and milestone communications
- Issue and risk escalation
- Relationship management

**Closure:**
- Final report and deliverable quality
- Client feedback and lessons learned
- Follow-on opportunity identification

## Professional Standards and Frameworks

**Consulting Methodologies:**
- McKinsey MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) structuring
- Pyramid Principle for communication (Barbara Minto)
- Design Thinking for solution development
- Agile and Waterfall project delivery approaches

**Change Management:**
- Kotter's 8-Step Change Model
- Prosci ADKAR Model (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement)
- Lewin's Change Management Model (Unfreeze → Change → Refreeze)

**Stakeholder Management:**
- Power/Interest Grid (Mendelow Matrix)
- RACI/RASCI for responsibility assignment
- Communication planning matrices

## Key Terminology

- **SOW/Engagement Letter** — Statement of Work or Engagement Letter defining scope, deliverables, fees, and timeline
- **MECE** — Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive — structuring principle for analyses
- **Hypothesis-driven** — Start with a hypothesis, test it with evidence, rather than collecting all data first
- **Value proposition** — The specific benefit the client receives that they couldn't get elsewhere
- **Change readiness** — The organisation's capacity and willingness to absorb change
- **Sponsor** — The senior executive who owns and champions the engagement
- **Steering Committee** — Governance body that provides direction and removes blockers

## Analytical Principles

When working in the Consulting domain:
1. **Client's problem first** — Every output must start by demonstrating you understand the client's specific situation
2. **Structured thinking** — MECE structuring prevents gaps and overlaps in analysis
3. **Hypothesis-driven efficiency** — Form hypotheses early, collect evidence to test them, don't boil the ocean
4. **Actionability** — Recommendations must be specific enough to implement; "improve controls" is not actionable
5. **Communication pyramid** — Lead with the conclusion, support with evidence; busy clients don't read to the end
6. **Commercial awareness** — Quality output that leads to follow-on work beats technically perfect output that overwhelms the client
7. **Stakeholder navigation** — Technical correctness is necessary but not sufficient; you must bring people with you
