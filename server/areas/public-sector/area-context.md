# Public Sector & Government Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Public Sector & Government area covers policy analysis and development, public procurement, grant writing, public consultation, and regulatory impact assessment within the UK and European public sector context. It serves civil servants, policy advisors, local government officers, NGO professionals, public sector consultants, and third-sector organisations working within formal public administration frameworks. This area is distinct from broader global government work: it focuses on the specific methodologies, standards, and institutional frameworks of UK central and local government and EU institutions.

## Core Public Sector Domains

**Policy Analysis and Development:**
- Policy cycle: agenda-setting, formulation, decision-making, implementation, evaluation
- Evidence-based policy making: systematic evidence review, research synthesis, expert consultation
- Logic models and theories of change: inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact
- Ministerial briefings and policy papers: structure, tone, and information hierarchy for different audiences (ministers, select committees, public)
- Comparative policy analysis: benchmarking against international equivalents
- Policy implementation planning: delivery design, governance, risk management

**Public Procurement:**
- UK Procurement Act 2023 (replacing PCR 2015/Utilities Contracts Regulations): open framework agreements, transparency notices, dynamic markets
- Find a Tender Service (FTS) and Contracts Finder
- EU Directives 2014/24/EU (public), 2014/25/EU (utilities), 2014/23/EU (concessions)
- OJEU thresholds and procurement procedures: open, restricted, competitive with negotiation, competitive dialogue, innovation partnership
- Government Commercial Function (GCF) standards and commercial capability framework
- Social value in procurement: Social Value Act 2012, PPN 06/20, net zero and levelling up considerations
- Business case development: HM Treasury Five Case Model (strategic, economic, commercial, financial, management)

**Grant Writing:**
- Grant landscape: UKRI/Innovate UK, ESRC, NIHR, Horizon Europe, Shared Prosperity Fund, Levelling Up
- Bid structure: problem statement, theory of change, deliverables, evaluation plan, value for money
- Consortium building and partnership documentation
- Budget narratives and full economic costing (fEC) for research grants
- Compliance with funder eligibility criteria and assessment rubrics

**Public Consultation:**
- Legal requirements for consultation: Gunning Principles (four-stage test: consult on proposals; at a formative stage; adequate information; genuine consideration of responses)
- Consultation design: response formats, accessibility, reach, and representativeness
- Equalities impact assessment (EIA) in consultation design
- Consultation analysis and response reporting
- Participation methods: digital engagement, citizen assemblies, deliberative democracy

**Regulatory Impact Assessment:**
- UK: Regulatory Policy Committee (RPC) impact assessment requirements; Better Regulation Framework
- HM Treasury Green Book and Magenta Book for appraisal and evaluation
- Cost-benefit analysis and monetisation of non-market impacts
- Post-implementation review (PIR) requirements
- EU: European Commission Better Regulation Guidelines and impact assessment toolbox
- OIOO (One In, One Out) / OITO (One In, Three Out) business impact targets

## Key Frameworks and Standards

- **HM Treasury Green Book** — UK government appraisal and evaluation guidance
- **HM Treasury Five Case Model** — Business case development for public spending
- **Gunning Principles** — Legal test for lawful public consultation
- **Magenta Book** — UK government evaluation methodology guidance
- **EU Better Regulation Guidelines** — European Commission impact assessment framework
- **Social Value Act 2012 / PPN 06/20** — Social value in public procurement

## Major Challenges

- Writing evidence-based policy under political pressure and compressed timescales
- Navigating procurement regulations without inadvertently creating legal challenge exposure
- Designing grant applications that stand out in highly competitive funding rounds
- Ensuring consultation processes are legally robust and genuinely inclusive
- Quantifying non-market benefits (health, wellbeing, social cohesion) in cost-benefit analysis

## How the Modules Help

**Policy-analysis** structures evidence reviews and policy option assessments. **Procurement-review** evaluates procurement strategies against current regulatory requirements. **Grant-writing** develops compelling bid narratives with robust theories of change and evaluation plans. **Public-consultation** designs legally sound and effective consultation processes. **Regulatory-impact** builds HM Treasury-compliant impact assessments with cost-benefit analysis.

## Analytical Principles

1. **Evidence quality determines policy quality** — Policy recommendations must be grounded in reliable, current evidence
2. **Proportionality in regulation** — Regulatory burden must be proportionate to the problem being solved
3. **Procedural fairness is legally essential** — Flawed process can invalidate technically sound policy decisions
4. **Value for money is a public duty** — Every pound of public expenditure must be justified; rigour in economic appraisal is non-negotiable
5. **Transparency builds legitimacy** — Open, well-documented decision-making sustains public trust in institutions
