# Banking & Financial Services Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Banking & Financial Services area covers the technical and regulatory dimensions of banking operations, credit risk, payment services, financial statement analysis, and prudential regulation. It serves banking professionals, credit analysts, compliance officers, and advisors working across credit institutions, payment institutions, and investment firms.

## Regulatory Framework

**Prudential Regulation:**
- CRR3 (Capital Requirements Regulation 3, Regulation (EU) 2024/1623) — Basel IV implementation in the EU; applies from 1 January 2025
- CRD6 (Capital Requirements Directive 6) — national law implementation
- Basel III/IV standards (BIS) — minimum capital, liquidity, leverage requirements
- SREP (Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process) — supervisory capital add-ons (Pillar 2)
- ICAAP/ILAAP — Internal Capital/Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Processes

**Payment Services:**
- PSD2 (Payment Services Directive 2) — current framework
- PSD3 / PSR (Payment Services Regulation) — the replacement package, agreed but not yet law: the co-legislators' compromise texts were approved by the European Parliament's ECON committee on 5 May 2026 and both files await the Council's first-reading position. Neither has been adopted or published in the Official Journal, so neither is in force, neither has an adopted instrument number, and no application date exists yet. PSD2 remains the binding framework.
- EMD2 (E-Money Directive 2) / EMR (forthcoming E-Money Regulation)
- Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) under RTS 2018/389
- Open Banking / Open Finance framework

**Accounting Standards:**
- IFRS 9 — Financial Instruments (expected credit loss, classification, measurement)
- IFRS 17 — Insurance Contracts
- IAS 39 — predecessor to IFRS 9 (some entities still relevant)
- FINREP/COREP — EBA reporting frameworks for financial and prudential data

**Consumer Protection:**
- Consumer Credit Directive (CCD2)
- Mortgage Credit Directive (MCD)
- PRIIPs Regulation
- MiFID II suitability and appropriateness requirements

## Key Terminology

- **PD/LGD/EAD** — Probability of Default / Loss Given Default / Exposure at Default (credit risk parameters)
- **RWA** — Risk-Weighted Assets (basis for capital requirement calculation)
- **NPL/NPE** — Non-Performing Loan / Non-Performing Exposure
- **NIM** — Net Interest Margin (core banking profitability measure)
- **LCR/NSFR** — Liquidity Coverage Ratio / Net Stable Funding Ratio (liquidity requirements)
- **DSCR** — Debt Service Coverage Ratio (credit underwriting measure)
- **Stage 1/2/3** — IFRS 9 credit impairment stages
- **SCA** — Strong Customer Authentication (payment security requirement)
- **TPSP** — Third Party Service Provider (payment services context)

## Analytical Principles

When working in the Banking & Financial Services domain:
1. **Credit judgement over model output** — Models are decision support tools, not decision makers
2. **Through-the-cycle thinking** — Assess creditworthiness across economic cycles, not just current conditions
3. **Regulatory capital awareness** — Every product and portfolio decision has capital implications
4. **Customer protection** — Regulatory requirements in consumer banking exist to prevent real customer harm
5. **Payment infrastructure interdependency** — Payment regulation intersects with AML, data protection, and operational resilience
6. **IFRS 9 forward-looking** — Provisioning requires forward-looking economic scenarios, not just historical data
7. **Proportionality** — Regulatory requirements scale with entity size, complexity, and interconnectedness
