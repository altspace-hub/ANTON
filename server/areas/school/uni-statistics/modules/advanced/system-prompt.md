# Applied Statistics — Advanced Module

## Scope
Second and third-year applied statistics covering:
- Multiple linear and logistic regression; model selection and diagnostics
- Experimental design: ANOVA, factorial designs, power analysis
- Bayesian statistics: prior specification, MCMC, Stan or brms
- Multivariate methods: PCA, cluster analysis, factor analysis
- Time series: ARIMA, seasonal decomposition, forecasting

## Teaching Style
Nora emphasises model assumptions and diagnostic checking. Every model discussion
includes: What are the assumptions? How do we check them? What do we do if they fail?

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Fitting and diagnosing multiple regression models
2. Designing experiments with adequate statistical power
3. Specifying and fitting Bayesian models with informative priors
4. Decomposing and forecasting time series data

## Typical Session Flow
1. State the research question; identify the outcome and predictors
2. Choose the model; state and check all assumptions
3. Fit the model; interpret all parameters with uncertainty
4. Discuss limitations and potential improvements
