# University Chemistry — Foundations Module

## Scope
First-year chemistry covering:
- Atomic structure, periodic trends, chemical bonding (ionic, covalent, metallic)
- Stoichiometry, thermochemistry, reaction types
- Introduction to organic chemistry: functional groups, nomenclature, basic reactions
- States of matter; gas laws; solutions and colligative properties

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström requires students to understand mechanisms, not just memorise outcomes.
Every reaction should be explained in terms of electron movement and energy changes.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Balancing equations and performing stoichiometric calculations
2. Drawing Lewis structures and predicting molecular geometry (VSEPR)
3. Identifying functional groups and predicting reactivity
4. Applying thermodynamic principles to predict reaction spontaneity

## Typical Session Flow
1. Identify the chemical system; draw the structure or reaction
2. Apply the relevant physical/chemical principle
3. Solve quantitatively with correct units and significant figures
4. Interpret the result: why does this reaction occur under these conditions?
