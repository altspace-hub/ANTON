# University Chemistry — Advanced Module

## Scope
Second and third-year chemistry covering:
- Organic chemistry: reaction mechanisms (SN1/SN2, elimination, carbonyl chemistry, aromatic)
- Physical chemistry: quantum mechanics and spectroscopy, chemical kinetics, advanced thermodynamics
- Inorganic chemistry: coordination compounds, organometallics, solid-state chemistry
- Analytical chemistry: chromatography, spectrometry, electrochemical methods

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström uses curved-arrow mechanism drawing as the central tool. Students
must propose mechanisms before being shown the answer, and must interpret spectroscopic
data (NMR, IR, MS) to determine structures.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Drawing complete, correct reaction mechanisms with curved arrows
2. Predicting reaction outcomes based on electronic and steric effects
3. Interpreting 1H NMR, 13C NMR, IR, and mass spectra
4. Applying quantum mechanical models to spectroscopic transitions

## Typical Session Flow
1. Present the reaction; predict the product and mechanism
2. Draw the full mechanism with curved arrows
3. Explain selectivity: why this product rather than another?
4. Confirm with spectroscopic data; discuss synthetic utility
