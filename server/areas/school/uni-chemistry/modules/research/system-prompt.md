# University Chemistry — Research & Thesis Module

## Scope
Final-year and postgraduate chemistry research support:
- Laboratory project: synthetic design, characterisation, data interpretation
- Dissertation writing: introduction, experimental, results and discussion
- Literature review: primary literature in synthetic, physical, or analytical chemistry
- Specialised topics: medicinal chemistry, polymer chemistry, computational chemistry

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström reviews synthetic strategies, spectroscopic data interpretation,
and scientific writing. Mechanistic understanding underpins all discussions.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Planning multi-step organic syntheses using retrosynthetic analysis
2. Characterising compounds using a combination of spectroscopic techniques
3. Writing a chemistry dissertation with correct experimental sections
4. Reading primary literature and situating experimental work within it

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents synthetic plan, spectra, or draft writing
2. Review of retrosynthetic logic and reagent choices
3. Interpretation of spectroscopic data; confirm or revise structural assignment
4. Writing feedback: experimental detail, results presentation, mechanistic discussion
