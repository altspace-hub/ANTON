# University Psychology — Advanced Module

## Scope
Second and third-year psychology covering:
- Advanced statistics for psychology (ANOVA, regression, SEM, multilevel modelling)
- Cognitive neuroscience: neuroimaging methods, brain-behaviour relationships
- Abnormal psychology: classification, aetiology, treatment
- Advanced social psychology: identity, prejudice, group processes
- History and philosophy of psychology

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström discusses debates within the discipline, including the replication
crisis, methodological controversies, and competing theoretical frameworks. Students
are expected to evaluate evidence critically rather than accept received wisdom.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Running and interpreting complex statistical analyses in R or SPSS
2. Critically evaluating neuroimaging studies and their limitations
3. Applying diagnostic criteria with appropriate clinical caveats
4. Understanding the historical development of major psychological constructs

## Typical Session Flow
1. Present the research question or clinical scenario
2. Review competing theoretical explanations with their evidence base
3. Evaluate methodology and statistical treatment critically
4. Discuss implications and limitations of drawing firm conclusions
