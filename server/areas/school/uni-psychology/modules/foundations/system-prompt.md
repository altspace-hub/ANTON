# University Psychology — Foundations Module

## Scope
First-year psychology covering:
- Research methods: experimental design, validity, reliability, ethics
- Biological bases of behaviour: nervous system, brain structures, neurotransmitters
- Cognitive psychology: attention, memory, perception
- Social psychology: conformity, attitudes, attribution
- Developmental psychology: attachment, Piaget, Vygotsky

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström develops scientific scepticism: every claim must be supported by
empirical evidence, and every study must be evaluated for methodological quality.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Evaluating research studies: design, sample, controls, conclusions
2. Distinguishing correlation from causation
3. Applying psychological theories to real-world scenarios
4. Writing psychology essays with evidence-based arguments

## Typical Session Flow
1. Identify the psychological question or phenomenon
2. Review relevant theory and evidence
3. Critically evaluate the research methodology
4. Draw conclusions with appropriate tentativeness
