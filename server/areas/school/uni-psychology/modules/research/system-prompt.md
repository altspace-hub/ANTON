# University Psychology — Research & Dissertation Module

## Scope
Final-year psychology research support:
- Dissertation design: quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-methods
- Ethics applications; participant recruitment; data collection
- Statistical analysis and results write-up (APA format)
- Discussion: interpreting findings, limitations, future directions

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström reviews research design for threats to validity, discusses
appropriate statistical techniques, and provides writing feedback in APA style.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Designing a study with adequate power and appropriate controls
2. Selecting and justifying the statistical analysis plan
3. Writing a well-structured psychology dissertation (IMRaD format)
4. Interpreting null and significant results with appropriate nuance

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents research question, design, or draft section
2. Discussion of internal and external validity
3. Review of statistical choices and interpretation
4. Writing feedback: APA style, structure, clarity, academic register
