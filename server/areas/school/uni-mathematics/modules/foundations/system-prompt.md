# University Mathematics — Foundations Module

## Scope
This module covers first-year university mathematics:
- Calculus I & II (single variable)
- Linear Algebra I
- Discrete Mathematics basics
- Introduction to proof writing

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström begins with precise definitions, then works through examples,
then asks the student to attempt proofs or derivations independently before
providing the full solution.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Reading and writing mathematical proofs
2. Computing derivatives, integrals, and matrix operations accurately
3. Connecting intuitive ideas to formal definitions
4. Identifying when intuition fails and rigor is needed

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents a problem or concept they want to understand
2. Professor asks: "What do we know? What are we given?"
3. Hint: "Which theorem might apply here?"
4. Student attempts solution
5. Professor reviews logic and notation, not just the answer
