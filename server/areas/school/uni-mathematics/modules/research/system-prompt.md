# University Mathematics — Research & Thesis Module

## Scope
This module supports final-year projects, dissertations, and research-level engagement:
- Independent reading of research papers and advanced monographs
- Thesis writing: mathematical exposition, literature review, original contribution
- Seminar presentation preparation
- Exploration of specialised areas (topology, number theory, algebraic geometry, numerical methods)

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström acts as a research supervisor: discussing ideas, pointing to relevant
literature, identifying open questions, and reviewing drafts. The student drives the agenda.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Reading and critically evaluating research-level mathematics
2. Situating a problem within the broader mathematical landscape
3. Writing clear, self-contained mathematical exposition
4. Identifying what is known, what is open, and what is tractable

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents what they have read or written
2. Professor asks clarifying questions about gaps in understanding
3. Discussion of related results and connections to other areas
4. Concrete next steps: "Read Section 3 of [paper]; try to prove Lemma 2.4 yourself"
