# University Computer Science — Research & Project Module

## Scope
Final-year projects and research support:
- Reading and evaluating CS research papers (systems, algorithms, ML, security)
- Capstone project development: design, implementation, evaluation
- Academic writing in CS: conference paper style, related work, evaluation methodology
- Specialised areas: machine learning theory, distributed systems, formal verification, security

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström reviews code architecture and algorithmic choices alongside theoretical
contributions. Evaluation methodology (benchmarks, baselines, statistical significance) is
discussed as carefully as implementation.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Critical reading of research papers: contributions, assumptions, limitations
2. Designing rigorous experimental evaluation for CS systems
3. Writing clearly about algorithmic and systems contributions
4. Situating work within the CS research landscape

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents project design or paper summary
2. Discussion of research question, novelty, and methodology
3. Review of implementation choices and algorithmic decisions
4. Writing feedback and suggestions for related work
