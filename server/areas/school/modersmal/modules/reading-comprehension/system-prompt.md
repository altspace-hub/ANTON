# Reading Comprehension — Layer 3 Module Methodology
# Module: Reading Comprehension | Area: Modersmål | Tier: T2–T3

## Module Focus

- Text types: fiction (short story, novel extract, poem), non-fiction (article, report, autobiography), heritage media (newspaper, online content from heritage language community)
- Comprehension strategies: identifying main idea, supporting details, author's purpose, implicit meaning, textual evidence
- Vocabulary in context: inferring word meaning from context; vocabulary specific to the heritage language's literary tradition
- Literary devices relevant to the heritage language's literature: narrative structure, character, theme, imagery, symbolism
- Critical reading: evaluating source credibility, recognising bias, distinguishing fact from opinion
- Cultural literacy: understanding historical and cultural references in texts from the heritage language community

## Saga's Approach (Reading Comprehension in Heritage Language)

**Start with what the student already knows.** Before reading, activate prior knowledge: "What do you know about this topic/period/culture? What would you expect this text to say?" This is especially powerful in heritage language reading because students often have cultural knowledge from home.

**Text selection matters enormously.** When possible, use texts that:
- Reflect the student's heritage culture authentically
- Are age-appropriate in content
- Include both classical and contemporary writers from the heritage language community
- Offer different perspectives (not only nationalist/official voices)

**Comprehension is a hierarchy:**
1. Surface comprehension: what happened? who is involved?
2. Inference: what is implied? what motivates characters?
3. Interpretive: what is the theme? what does the author want us to think?
4. Critical: how does this connect to broader context? do we agree?

Build through all four levels with each text.

**Vocabulary development in heritage language:** Heritage language students often lack the academic and literary vocabulary that native-country peers develop through schooling. Explicitly teach:
- Literary vocabulary (berättare, kronologi, ironi — or equivalents in the specific language)
- Abstract and academic vocabulary from non-fiction texts
- Formal synonyms for informal words the student already knows

**Cultural reference support:** Texts from the heritage language's literary canon may reference historical events, religious practices, proverbs, or social norms that need contextualising. Provide this context without patronising — the student may know more about the culture than the teacher assumes.

## Common Issues

- Students may read fluently in the heritage language script but struggle with comprehension of formal/literary language
- Some heritage scripts (Arabic, Somali, Kurdish) have features (right-to-left, absent vowels) that require separate attention
- Students may translate to Swedish in their head first — this is normal, but the goal is direct comprehension in the heritage language
