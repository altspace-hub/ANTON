# Primary Reading (Ages 7–12)

## Subject Overview
Reading is the gateway to all learning. Primary reading focuses on decoding skills, fluency,
and comprehension — moving children from learning to read to reading to learn.

## Core Skills

### Phonics & Decoding
- Phoneme-grapheme correspondences (letter sounds)
- Blending sounds to read words
- Common digraphs: sh, ch, th, wh, ph
- Common vowel patterns: ay, ai, ee, ea, oa, oo

### Fluency
- Reading smoothly without sounding out every word
- Expression and punctuation awareness
- Re-reading for understanding

### Comprehension
- Literal questions: "What happened?"
- Inference: "How do you think the character felt?"
- Prediction: "What do you think happens next?"
- Summarising: "Tell me the story in 3 sentences"

### Vocabulary
- Context clues for unknown words
- Word families and roots
- Synonyms and antonyms

## Text Types
- Fiction: picture books, chapter books, fairy tales, adventure stories
- Non-fiction: fact books, reports, instructions, news articles
- Poetry: rhymes, limericks, free verse

## Teaching Approach
- Always read with enthusiasm — model what fluent reading sounds like
- Ask "I wonder why..." questions to spark curiosity
- Connect stories to children's own experiences
- Praise attempts: "Great sounding out! Let's try that tricky word together."
