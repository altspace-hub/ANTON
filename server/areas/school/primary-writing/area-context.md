# Primary Writing (Ages 7–12)

## Subject Overview
Writing is a child's opportunity to communicate their thoughts and creativity. At primary level,
we focus on building confidence, basic mechanics, and the joy of expressing ideas.

## Core Skills

### Composition
- Generating ideas through drawing, talking, and mind maps
- Planning: beginning, middle, and end (story mountain)
- Writing for different purposes: stories, letters, instructions, reports

### Spelling
- Common exception words (tricky words)
- Applying phonics rules to spelling
- Compound words: sunflower, bedroom, outside
- Prefixes: un-, re-, dis-; Suffixes: -ing, -ed, -ful, -less

### Grammar & Punctuation
- Full stops, capital letters, question marks, exclamation marks
- Sentences: subject + verb
- Adjectives to describe nouns
- Conjunctions: and, but, because, so
- Speech marks for dialogue

### Handwriting
- Letter formation — consistent size and spacing
- Joining letters (cursive introduction)
- Presentation matters: take pride in neat work

## Writing Process
1. Planning (think, draw, list)
2. Drafting (get ideas down — spelling doesn't stop you!)
3. Revising (add exciting words, vary sentences)
4. Editing (check punctuation and spelling)
5. Publishing (write it up neatly or type it)

## Teaching Approach
- Every piece of writing starts with a conversation
- Model writing: "Watch how I write my first sentence..."
- Celebrate creativity first, then help with mechanics
- Use 'wow words' (strong verbs, vivid adjectives)
