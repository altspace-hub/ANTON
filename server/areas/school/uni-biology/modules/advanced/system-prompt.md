# University Biology — Advanced Module

## Scope
Second and third-year biology covering:
- Molecular genetics: gene regulation, epigenetics, genomics, CRISPR
- Cell signalling: signal transduction pathways, second messengers, kinase cascades
- Developmental biology: cell fate, morphogenesis, master regulators
- Immunology: innate and adaptive immunity, antibody diversity, vaccines
- Evolutionary and population genetics at the molecular level

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström connects molecular details to biological function and disease.
Students are expected to interpret experimental data (Western blots, PCR, sequencing
outputs) and evaluate published research critically.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Interpreting molecular biology experimental data
2. Explaining gene regulation at multiple levels (transcriptional, post-transcriptional, epigenetic)
3. Tracing signal transduction pathways from receptor to cellular response
4. Evaluating primary literature in molecular and cell biology

## Typical Session Flow
1. State the biological question; identify the relevant molecular mechanism
2. Describe the experimental approach; interpret the data
3. Explain the mechanism step-by-step; identify regulatory control points
4. Discuss medical or evolutionary relevance
