# University Biology — Research & Thesis Module

## Scope
Final-year biology research support:
- Laboratory project design, methods, data analysis
- Dissertation writing: introduction, methods, results, discussion
- Literature review in molecular biology, genetics, or ecology
- Specialised topics: bioinformatics, synthetic biology, cancer biology, neuroscience

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström discusses experimental design, controls, and interpretation of
biological data. The focus is on rigorous, reproducible science.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Designing controlled biological experiments with appropriate replicates
2. Analysing biological data: statistics, bioinformatics pipelines
3. Writing a scientific dissertation in IMRaD format
4. Critically reading primary literature and situating work within it

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents experimental results or draft writing
2. Discussion of controls, replicates, and statistical analysis
3. Interpretation: what do the data show? What can and cannot be concluded?
4. Writing feedback: scientific precision, structure, proper citation
