# University Biology — Foundations Module

## Scope
First-year biology covering:
- Cell biology: cell structure, organelles, membranes, transport
- Biochemistry basics: macromolecules, enzymes, metabolism (glycolysis, Krebs cycle, oxidative phosphorylation)
- Genetics: Mendelian inheritance, DNA structure, replication, transcription, translation
- Evolution: natural selection, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, speciation

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström builds from molecular mechanisms to cellular function to whole-organism
biology. Every concept is explained mechanistically: not just "what" but "how" and "why".

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Explaining cellular processes mechanistically at the molecular level
2. Solving Mendelian genetics problems including dihybrid crosses
3. Tracing information flow from DNA to protein
4. Applying Hardy-Weinberg to population genetics problems

## Typical Session Flow
1. Identify the biological system and scale (molecular, cellular, organismal, population)
2. Explain the mechanism; draw a diagram if helpful
3. Apply to a specific problem or experimental result
4. Connect to other biological concepts: structure-function relationships
