# School Vocabulary — Layer 3 Module Methodology
# Module: School Vocabulary | Area: SFI Bridge | Tier: T1–T3

## Module Focus

- Academic Swedish vocabulary across school subjects: mathematics, science, social studies, history, Swedish language arts
- Instruction vocabulary: task words (beräkna, jämför, beskriv, analysera, motivera, redogör för) and their meanings
- Classroom language: what teachers say in Swedish classrooms; what students need to say/write
- Text type vocabulary: words that signal argument (å ena sidan, å andra sidan, dock, emellertid), cause-effect (eftersom, därför, leder till), sequence (först, sedan, slutligen)
- Compound words: Swedish compound formation (miljökonsekvenser = miljö + konsekvenser; samhällsvetenskap = samhälle + vetenskap)
- False cognates and near-cognates with the student's home language

## Teaching Approach — School Vocabulary

**Thematic vocabulary teaching — not isolated words:**
Teach vocabulary in clusters around a school topic, not as individual words:
- Mathematics cluster: siffra, tal, beräkna, summa, skillnad, produkt, kvot, likhet, olikhet, lösning
- Science cluster: organism, cell, ämne, reaktion, energi, kraft, rörelse, förändring
- History cluster: orsak, konsekvens, händelse, period, revolution, krig, fred, makt, samhälle

**Teach task words explicitly — they determine what the student must do:**
| Swedish instruction word | English equivalent | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| Beräkna | Calculate | Show working, give numerical answer |
| Beskriv | Describe | Give characteristics, how it looks/works |
| Förklara | Explain | Give reasons, causes, mechanisms |
| Jämför | Compare | Both similarities AND differences |
| Analysera | Analyse | Break down + explain connections |
| Motivera | Justify | Give reasons with evidence |
| Redogör för | Account for | Systematic description with detail |

**Compound word decoding strategy:**
"Swedish puts words together to make new words. If you see a long unfamiliar word, try to break it into smaller parts: [miljö][konsekvenser] = environment + consequences = environmental consequences."

**Contextualised practice:** After introducing vocabulary, use it in a sentence from a school subject context immediately. "The word 'orsak' means cause. In history you might read: 'En av orsakerna till första världskriget var...' — One of the causes of World War One was..."

**Visual vocabulary organisers:** Describe semantic maps: "Imagine a web. In the centre: 'rörelse' (movement). Connected words: hastighet (speed), acceleration, riktning (direction), kraft (force), friktion (friction). These words all appear together in physics."

## Common Issues

- Students may know the concept in their home language but not the Swedish academic vocabulary — teach the word, not the concept
- Polysemy: many Swedish academic words have different meanings in everyday vs academic use (e.g., "lösning" = both "solution" to a problem and "solution" in chemistry — a liquid mixture)
- Instruction words are critical: misreading "jämför" as "beskriv" completely changes what answer is expected; many lower grades come from misreading the task
