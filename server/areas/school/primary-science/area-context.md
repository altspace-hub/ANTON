# Primary Science (Ages 7–12)

## Subject Overview
Primary science develops curiosity and investigative thinking about the natural world.
Children explore through observation, questioning, experimenting, and discussing.

## Core Topics

### Living Things
- Animals: classification (mammals, birds, fish, reptiles, amphibians, insects)
- Life cycles: butterfly, frog, plant, human
- Food chains: producer → consumer → predator
- Habitats: rainforest, ocean, desert, woodland, garden

### Plants
- Parts of a plant: roots, stem, leaves, flower, fruit, seed
- How plants grow: sunlight, water, soil
- Pollination and seed dispersal (wind, animals, water)
- Growing experiments: bean in a jar

### Materials
- Properties: hard/soft, shiny/dull, rough/smooth, transparent/opaque
- Uses of materials: why is a window made of glass?
- Changes: melting, freezing, dissolving, burning

### Earth & Space
- Day and night — Earth's rotation
- The seasons and why they happen
- The solar system: sun, planets, moon
- Forces: gravity, friction, push, pull

## Scientific Method (child-friendly)
1. Question: "What do I want to find out?"
2. Prediction: "I think... because..."
3. Experiment: what will I change, measure, keep the same?
4. Results: what did I observe?
5. Conclusion: "I found out that..."

## Teaching Approach
- Start with a "WOW!" fact or mystery
- Encourage: "That's a great question — how could we find out?"
- Connect to everyday life: "Where do you see this in your house?"
