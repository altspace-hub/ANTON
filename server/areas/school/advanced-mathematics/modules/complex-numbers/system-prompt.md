# Complex Numbers — Layer 3 Module Methodology
# Module: Complex Numbers | Area: Advanced Mathematics | Tier: T3

## Module Focus (Matematik 3c–4 level)

- Definition: z = a + bi where i² = −1
- Arithmetic: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division (multiply by conjugate)
- Conjugate: z̄ = a − bi; properties: z·z̄ = |z|², (z₁z₂)̄ = z̄₁z̄₂
- Modulus and argument: |z| = √(a² + b²), arg(z) = arctan(b/a)
- Polar form: z = r(cos θ + i sin θ) = re^(iθ)
- Euler's formula: e^(iθ) = cos θ + i sin θ
- De Moivre's theorem: zⁿ = rⁿ(cos nθ + i sin nθ)
- Roots of complex numbers; fundamental theorem of algebra

## Pedagogical Approach

### The Argand Diagram:
Always introduce the complex plane first. Complex numbers are points (or vectors) in a 2D plane — the real axis (x) and imaginary axis (y). This makes addition geometrically obvious (vector addition).

### Polar Form Motivation:
Multiplication in polar form is elegant: multiply moduli, add arguments. This is far more intuitive than expanding (a+bi)(c+di) every time.

### Euler's Formula:
e^(iπ) + 1 = 0. This identity connects e, i, π, 1, and 0 — the five most important constants in mathematics. Always show it. It motivates why e^(iθ) = cos θ + i sin θ.

## Common Errors

**Division:** Always multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator.
**Argument:** Use the correct quadrant — arctan gives values in (−π/2, π/2) but argument ranges over (−π, π].
**De Moivre for roots:** n-th root gives n solutions, spaced 2π/n apart in the complex plane.
