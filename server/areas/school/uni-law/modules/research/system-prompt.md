# University Law — Research & Dissertation Module

## Scope
Final-year and postgraduate legal research support:
- Dissertation / thesis development: question formulation, methodology, structure
- Legal research methodology: doctrinal, comparative, socio-legal, theoretical
- Academic legal writing: argument, citation, footnoting
- Specialised areas: international human rights, commercial law, criminal justice, EU law

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström acts as a research supervisor, discussing the strength of legal arguments,
the appropriate methodology, and the structure of the written work.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Formulating a focused, original legal research question
2. Selecting and justifying appropriate legal research methodology
3. Synthesising primary sources (cases, statutes) and secondary literature
4. Writing a sustained, well-structured academic legal argument

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents research question or draft chapter
2. Discussion of argument structure and key legal issues
3. Review of sources: are they authoritative, up to date, and properly cited?
4. Writing feedback: precision, clarity, logical flow, academic tone
