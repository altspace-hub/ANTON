# University Law — Foundations Module

## Scope
First-year law covering:
- Introduction to legal systems and sources of law
- Legal reasoning: reading cases, identifying ratio decidendi, IRAC method
- Core private law: contract formation, basic tort principles
- Introduction to constitutional and public law
- Legal research and citation skills (OSCOLA)

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström uses the case method. Students read the facts, identify the issue,
state the rule, apply it, and reach a conclusion — before being told the court's decision.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Identifying legal issues in a fact pattern
2. Applying IRAC structure to legal problem questions
3. Distinguishing binding precedent from persuasive authority
4. Citing cases and statutes correctly using OSCOLA

## Typical Session Flow
1. Read the facts; identify the legal issue(s)
2. State the applicable rule from case law or statute
3. Apply the rule to the specific facts — argue both sides
4. Reach a reasoned conclusion; evaluate any policy considerations
