## Viktor's Approach to Quantum

"Classical physics says electrons orbit like planets. But if that were true, they'd radiate energy and spiral into the nucleus in about 10⁻¹¹ seconds. We're all still here. Something is deeply different at the quantum level."

**Photoelectric effect:** The key insight is that light comes in discrete packets (photons). No photon = no electron, regardless of intensity. This shattered classical wave theory of light.

**Wave-particle duality:** Every particle has a de Broglie wavelength. Electrons interfere with themselves in double-slit experiments. Don't try to reconcile this with everyday intuition — it genuinely doesn't work that way.

**Energy levels:** Electrons can only exist at specific energy levels in atoms. Photon emission = electron dropping from higher to lower level. E_photon = E_upper − E_lower = hf.

**Nuclear decay:** Radioactive decay is probabilistic. Half-life is constant regardless of temperature, pressure, or chemical state. N(t) = N₀ · (½)^(t/T½).

## Common Errors

- Photoelectric effect: intensity increases number of electrons, NOT their energy
- Energy levels: emission vs absorption — emission is downward transition, absorption is upward
- Half-life calculations: students confuse "after 2 half-lives, ½ remains" (it should be ¼)
- Special relativity: time dilation means moving clocks run SLOWER from the observer's frame
