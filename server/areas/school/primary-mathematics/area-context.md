# Primary Mathematics (Ages 7–12)

## Subject Overview
Primary mathematics builds the foundation for all future mathematical thinking. At this age,
learning should be hands-on, visual, and connected to real-world experiences children recognise.

## Core Topics

### Number & Arithmetic
- Number bonds to 10, 20, and 100
- Times tables (2, 5, 10 first; then 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9)
- Written methods: column addition, subtraction, multiplication
- Fractions (halves, quarters, thirds) — use pizza and chocolate bar examples
- Place value: ones, tens, hundreds, thousands

### Measurement
- Length, mass, volume — practical activities with rulers and scales
- Time: reading clocks (analogue and digital), days/weeks/months
- Money: counting coins, giving change

### Geometry
- 2D shapes (triangle, square, rectangle, circle, pentagon, hexagon)
- 3D shapes (cube, cuboid, sphere, cylinder, cone)
- Symmetry: fold-and-cut activities

### Data
- Tally charts, pictograms, bar charts
- Simple surveys: favourite animals, sports, colours

## Teaching Approach (Child-Friendly)
- Start EVERY topic with a game or real-world example
- Use rhymes and songs for times tables
- Draw pictures before writing numbers
- Celebrate small wins with enthusiastic praise
- If stuck: "Let's try with our fingers first!"

## Common Misconceptions
- Place value confusion (e.g. writing 23 as 203)
- Subtraction direction (subtracting the larger from the smaller)
- Fractions as parts of a whole vs. parts of a set
- Zero as a placeholder

## Example Activities
- Times table bingo
- Real shop scenarios with toy money
- Measuring classroom objects with a ruler
- Creating bar charts from class surveys
