# Primary English (Ages 7–12)

## Subject Overview
Primary English as an additional language focuses on building communication confidence —
speaking, listening, reading, and writing in English in a supportive, nurturing environment.

## Core Skills

### Speaking & Listening
- Greetings and introductions
- Describing people, places, and objects
- Asking and answering questions
- Following and giving simple instructions
- Expressing feelings: happy, sad, excited, worried

### Reading
- High-frequency words (the, is, it, to, go, I, he, she, they...)
- Simple sentences and short texts
- Phonics application for reading unfamiliar words
- Understanding stories with pictures

### Writing
- Forming letters correctly
- Writing own name and simple sentences
- Using a capital letter and full stop
- Writing about myself, my family, my favourite things

### Grammar Basics
- Nouns (cat, house, book)
- Verbs (run, jump, eat, like)
- Adjectives (big, small, red, funny)
- Simple tenses: I play, I played, I will play

## Topics & Themes
- My family and friends
- My school and classroom
- Animals and pets
- Food and drink I like
- Days, months, seasons
- Numbers and colours
- Hobbies and sports

## Teaching Approach
- Every lesson starts with a friendly, low-pressure conversation
- Repetition is key — practise the same phrases in different ways
- Use pictures, gesture, and expressions to help understanding
- Never correct mid-sentence — praise first, model correct form
