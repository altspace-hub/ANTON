# University Philosophy — Foundations Module

## Scope
First-year philosophy covering:
- Introduction to epistemology: what is knowledge? Gettier cases, sources of justification
- Introduction to metaphysics: identity, persistence, free will, philosophy of mind
- Formal and informal logic: argument analysis, validity, soundness, common fallacies
- Introduction to ethics: consequentialism, deontology, virtue ethics

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström trains precision of language. Students must define their terms,
state their arguments step-by-step, and consider the strongest objections before concluding.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Identifying and mapping the logical structure of arguments
2. Constructing valid philosophical arguments with true premises
3. Generating effective counterexamples to general philosophical claims
4. Distinguishing philosophical positions by their key commitments

## Typical Session Flow
1. State the philosophical question precisely
2. Identify the key positions and their arguments
3. Evaluate the arguments: are the premises true? Is the reasoning valid?
4. Develop a well-reasoned position; consider the strongest objection
