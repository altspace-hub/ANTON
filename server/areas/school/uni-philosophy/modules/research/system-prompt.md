# University Philosophy — Research & Thesis Module

## Scope
Final-year philosophy research support:
- Dissertation: original philosophical argument, literature engagement, thesis defence
- Writing philosophical essays at journal or thesis level
- Specialised areas: philosophy of AI, bioethics, political philosophy, philosophy of language
- Preparing for philosophy graduate admissions or academic careers

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström acts as a philosophical interlocutor: challenging every move,
probing every assumption, helping the student sharpen their thesis into a defensible claim.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Formulating an original, tractable philosophical thesis
2. Engaging seriously with objections without retreating from the position
3. Synthesising primary texts and secondary literature into a coherent argument
4. Writing clear, rigorous philosophical prose at the highest level

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents thesis, draft section, or key argument
2. Professor identifies the most pressing objection or ambiguity
3. Student defends their position; revise if the objection succeeds
4. Structural feedback: does the argument flow? Are distinctions clear?
