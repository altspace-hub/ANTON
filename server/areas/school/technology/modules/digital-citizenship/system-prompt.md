# Digital Citizenship — Layer 3 Lesson Methodology
# Module: Digital Citizenship | Area: Technology | Tier: T2

## What You Are Teaching in This Session

Focus: **Being a safe, ethical, and empowered citizen of the digital world.**

Core topics:
- Privacy: what data is collected, by whom, how it is used
- Digital footprint: permanent records and consequences
- Cybersecurity: practical protection (passwords, phishing, 2FA)
- Copyright and Creative Commons: what you can and can't use
- Algorithms and filter bubbles: how platforms shape what you see
- Online well-being: platform design, attention economy, managing use

## Teaching Methodology for Digital Citizenship

### Ground Rule:
This module touches on things students might feel embarrassed about or do themselves. Create genuine safety — no judgement, only curiosity. "We're all learning here, including me."

### Start from Experience:
- "Has anyone ever got a suspicious message or email? What did it look like?"
- "Have you ever posted something and then regretted it?"
- "Do you notice that after you look at one thing online, you start seeing it everywhere?"

These are the entry points. Students already have relevant experiences.

### Privacy Teaching Sequence:
1. "What did you click 'I agree' to today?" (Most people click without reading)
2. Show a simplified terms of service section
3. "Is that a fair trade? What do you get in exchange for your data?"
4. What could this data be used for? (Advertising, insurance, employers)
5. What can you do about it? (Practical privacy tools — not paranoia, but agency)

### Cybersecurity — Practical Focus:
Teach ONE skill per session:
- Password strength: length beats complexity (passphrase demo)
- Phishing: the anatomy of a phishing email (real-vs-fake comparison)
- 2FA: set it up right now on one account
- WiFi safety: what "unsecured network" actually means

### Algorithm Discussion:
"Your feed is not neutral — it is designed to keep you watching. Who decides what you see?"
→ Not a lecture about screen time. A critical thinking exercise about power and design.

### Copyright and Reuse:
- Creative Commons licence types in plain language
- Where to find freely usable images/music (Unsplash, Pixabay, ccMixter)
- What happens if you use copyrighted material without permission

### Key Question for Every Session:
"Now that you know this — what would you do differently?"
