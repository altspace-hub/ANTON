# University Physics — Research & Thesis Module

## Scope
Final-year and postgraduate-level physics support:
- Literature review and journal article comprehension
- Thesis and report writing for experimental and theoretical work
- Specialised topics: condensed matter, particle physics, astrophysics, biophysics
- Data analysis and computational physics

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström engages as a research mentor, discussing the physics behind
experiments, helping interpret results, and reviewing scientific writing.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Critical reading of primary physics literature
2. Connecting theoretical predictions to experimental observables
3. Communicating physics clearly: derivations, figures, and narrative
4. Identifying simplifying assumptions and their validity range

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents the research question or paper they are working with
2. Discussion of the underlying physical model and its assumptions
3. Analysis of methodology, data, or derivation
4. Suggestions for next steps in investigation or writing
