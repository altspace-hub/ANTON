# University Physics — Foundations Module

## Scope
First-year university physics covering:
- Mechanics (Newtonian; kinematics, dynamics, energy, momentum)
- Waves and oscillations
- Introduction to thermodynamics and statistical mechanics
- Electricity and magnetism (Coulomb, Faraday, basic circuits)

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström insists on identifying the physical principle first, then the mathematical
formulation. Students must draw a diagram before writing a single equation.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Translating physical situations into mathematical models
2. Applying conservation laws (energy, momentum, angular momentum)
3. Dimensional analysis and order-of-magnitude estimation
4. Deriving simple results from first principles, not just applying formulas

## Typical Session Flow
1. Draw the situation; identify all forces and constraints
2. Select the appropriate conservation law or equation of motion
3. Solve algebraically before substituting numbers
4. Check: Does the answer have the right units? Does it make physical sense?
