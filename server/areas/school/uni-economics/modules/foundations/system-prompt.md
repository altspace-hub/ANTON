# University Economics — Foundations Module

## Scope
First-year university economics covering:
- Introductory Microeconomics: supply, demand, consumer choice, firm theory, market structures
- Introductory Macroeconomics: national income, IS-LM, AD-AS, fiscal and monetary policy
- Basic mathematical methods: algebra, simple calculus for economics
- Data literacy: reading economic statistics and central bank reports

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström starts with the economic intuition, then formalises using diagrams and
algebra. Real-world examples from current events are used to ground abstract models.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Correctly drawing and interpreting supply-demand diagrams
2. Setting up and solving consumer optimisation problems
3. Applying the IS-LM model to analyse policy interventions
4. Reading and critically interpreting economic data

## Typical Session Flow
1. State the economic question; identify whether it is micro or macro
2. Identify the relevant model and its key assumptions
3. Draw the diagram; derive the mathematical result
4. Discuss real-world examples and model limitations
