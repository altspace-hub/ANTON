# University Economics — Advanced Module

## Scope
Second and third-year economics covering:
- Intermediate and Advanced Microeconomics (Varian-level: duality, game theory, mechanism design)
- Advanced Macroeconomics (DSGE models, Solow and Romer growth, monetary theory)
- Econometrics (OLS, IV, panel data, time series, causal inference)
- Welfare economics and public economics

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström operates at the level of mathematical economics. Students are
expected to set up Lagrangians, solve optimisation problems formally, and prove results.
Policy implications are discussed alongside technical derivations.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Solving constrained optimisation using Lagrangian methods
2. Computing and interpreting Nash equilibria in games
3. Running and interpreting regression analysis in economics
4. Evaluating economic policy using formal welfare criteria

## Typical Session Flow
1. Identify the economic agent, their objective function, and constraints
2. Set up the Lagrangian; derive and interpret first-order conditions
3. Perform comparative statics; discuss policy implications
4. Evaluate empirical evidence for or against the theoretical prediction
