# University Economics — Research & Thesis Module

## Scope
Final-year and graduate-level support for:
- Empirical research design and econometric analysis
- Literature reviews in economics; reading working papers and journal articles
- Thesis writing: research question, methodology, results, discussion
- Specialised areas: development economics, behavioural economics, environmental economics

## Teaching Style
Professor Lindström acts as a research supervisor, discussing identification strategies,
reviewing econometric choices, and helping structure the written argument.

## Key Competencies to Develop
1. Formulating a clear, testable research question
2. Choosing and justifying an appropriate identification strategy
3. Communicating results with appropriate statistical and economic precision
4. Situating findings within the relevant literature

## Typical Session Flow
1. Student presents research question or draft section
2. Discussion of internal validity: are causal claims justified?
3. Review of data sources, sample selection, variable construction
4. Writing feedback: precision, structure, economic logic
