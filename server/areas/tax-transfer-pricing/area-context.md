# Tax & Transfer Pricing Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Tax & Transfer Pricing area covers the full spectrum of corporate tax obligations for international businesses: direct tax compliance, transfer pricing documentation, cross-border structuring, indirect tax, tax authority dispute management, and tax risk governance. It serves in-house tax directors, transfer pricing specialists, CFOs, and their advisors navigating an increasingly assertive global tax enforcement environment.

## Core Regulatory and Standards Landscape

**OECD Frameworks:**
- OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises and Tax Administrations (2022)
- OECD Model Tax Convention on Income and Capital (2017, updated 2019)
- BEPS Action Plan — 15 Actions addressing base erosion and profit shifting
- BEPS Action 8-10 — Aligning transfer pricing outcomes with value creation
- BEPS Action 13 — Transfer pricing documentation and Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR)
- BEPS Action 15 / Pillar Two — Global minimum tax (GloBE Rules), 15% effective tax rate floor
- BEPS Action 7 — Preventing artificial avoidance of permanent establishment status

**EU Directives:**
- ATAD 1 (EU 2016/1164) — Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive: interest limitation, exit taxation, GAAR, CFC rules, hybrid mismatches
- ATAD 2 (EU 2017/952) — Extension of hybrid mismatch rules to third-country arrangements
- DAC6 (EU 2018/822) — Mandatory disclosure of cross-border arrangements
- DAC7 (EU 2021/514) — Digital platform reporting
- Pillar Two Directive (EU 2022/2523) — GloBE minimum tax implementation

**Key Tax Concepts:**
- **Arm's Length Principle (ALP)** — Transactions between related parties must reflect terms that independent parties would agree on
- **Permanent Establishment (PE)** — Taxable presence created by fixed place of business, agency, or digital activity
- **Controlled Foreign Corporation (CFC)** — Rules taxing passive income of foreign subsidiaries in parent's jurisdiction
- **Thin Capitalisation / Interest Limitation** — Rules limiting deductibility of interest to curb debt-financed profit shifting
- **Substance Requirements** — Demonstrating genuine economic activity in the jurisdiction claiming treaty or regime benefits

## Analytical Principles

When working in the Tax & Transfer Pricing domain:
1. **Arm's length primacy** — Every intercompany price must be defensible by reference to comparable uncontrolled transactions
2. **Substance over form** — Tax authorities look through legal arrangements to economic reality; substance must follow function
3. **Jurisdictional specificity** — Tax rules vary materially by country; always identify the relevant jurisdiction before advising
4. **Documentation disciplines** — TP documentation must exist contemporaneously; post-hoc rationalisation is rejected by courts
5. **Proportionality** — Risk-based approach; high-value, high-risk transactions merit deeper analysis and stronger documentation
6. **Pillar Two awareness** — From 2024/2025, GloBE rules affect groups with revenue above EUR 750M; ETR computations are critical
