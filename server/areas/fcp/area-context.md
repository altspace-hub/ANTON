# FCP Area Context — Financial Crime Prevention

## Domain Landscape

Financial Crime Prevention (FCP) encompasses the full spectrum of measures that financial institutions use to detect, prevent, and report money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions violations, fraud, and other financial crimes.

## Regulatory Framework

**EU AML/CFT Architecture (2024 Reform Package):**
- AMLR — Anti-Money Laundering Regulation 2024/1624 (directly applicable, full harmonisation)
- AMLD6 — 6th Anti-Money Laundering Directive (member state transposition required)
- AMLA — Anti-Money Laundering Authority (operational from 2025, direct supervision of high-risk obliged entities)
- FUNDS Regulation — Transfer of Funds Regulation 2023/1113

**Nordic Regulatory Environment:**
- Sweden: FI (Finansinspektionen), lag (2017:630) om åtgärder mot penningtvätt och finansiering av terrorism
- Finland: FIN-FSA, laki rahanpesun ja terrorismin rahoittamisen estämisestä (444/2017)
- Denmark: Finanstilsynet, Hvidvaskloven (LBK nr 1062 af 19/05/2021)
- Norway: Finanstilsynet, hvitvaskingsloven (Lov om tiltak mot hvitvasking og terrorfinansiering)

**International Standards:**
- FATF 40 Recommendations + Guidance Notes
- Basel Committee on Banking Supervision — Sound management of risks related to money laundering and financing of terrorism
- EBA Guidelines — AML/CFT Risk Factors, Internal Governance, Outsourcing, Correspondent Banking

**Sanctions:**
- EU — Common Foreign & Security Policy restrictive measures (Council Regulations)
- US — OFAC SDN List, country-specific programs
- UN — Security Council Consolidated List
- UK — OFSI sanctions (post-Brexit)

## Key Terminology

- **BWRA** — Business-Wide Risk Assessment
- **CDD/EDD/SDD** — Customer / Enhanced / Simplified Due Diligence
- **SAR/STR** — Suspicious Activity Report / Suspicious Transaction Report
- **TM** — Transaction Monitoring
- **PEP** — Politically Exposed Person
- **UBO** — Ultimate Beneficial Owner
- **TBML** — Trade-Based Money Laundering
- **FIU** — Financial Intelligence Unit
- **Obliged entity** — any entity subject to AML/CFT requirements
- **High-risk third country** — jurisdiction with strategic deficiencies identified by FATF/EU

## Analytical Principles

When working in the FCP domain:
1. **Risk-based approach is paramount** — proportionality between controls and risk level
2. **Substance over form** — assess whether controls actually work, not just whether they exist
3. **Regulatory hierarchy matters** — distinguish AMLR (directly applicable) vs. national transpositions vs. supervisory guidance
4. **Nordic context** — Nordic financial institutions operate under EU framework but with specific national supervisory expectations and market characteristics
5. **Practical implementation** — focus on what a compliance officer can actually do with the output
