# Corporate Communications & Public Relations Area Context

## Domain Landscape

The Corporate Communications & Public Relations area covers the design and delivery of strategic communications across internal and external audiences. It serves communications directors, PR managers, CEOs, investor relations teams, and corporate affairs professionals responsible for managing reputation, driving stakeholder alignment, and protecting organisational credibility in both normal and crisis conditions. The domain spans message strategy, media relations, internal engagement, and investor communications.

## Core Communications Domains

**Strategic Communications:**
- Message architecture: core narrative, key messages, proof points, and audience variants
- Stakeholder mapping and communications planning
- Narrative development for major corporate events (M&A, restructuring, product launches)
- Brand and corporate reputation management
- Thought leadership strategy

**Crisis Communications:**
- Crisis classification and response protocols (tiers 1-3)
- Rapid message development under uncertainty
- Spokesperson preparation and media training frameworks
- Dark site and holding statement protocols
- Post-crisis reputation recovery and lessons learned

**Internal Communications:**
- Change communications planning: managing employee understanding and commitment through transformation
- Town hall design and CEO briefings
- Internal campaign development: engagement, culture, and behaviour change
- Meeting minutes and decision documentation
- Employee communications across distributed and hybrid workforces

**Media Relations:**
- Media briefing documents and press releases
- Journalist targeting and relationship management
- Embargo management and exclusive strategies
- Interview preparation and key messages coaching
- Media monitoring and coverage analysis

**Investor Relations:**
- Investor update letters and earnings communications
- Results presentation structure and messaging
- Annual report narrative and CEO letters
- Capital markets day preparation
- Analyst briefing materials

**Stakeholder Engagement:**
- Stakeholder messaging matrix: tailored messages by audience, channel, and timing
- Consultation and engagement process management
- Government and regulatory affairs communications
- Community and NGO stakeholder engagement

## Key Frameworks and Concepts

- **Message Architecture** — Hierarchical structure: core narrative → key messages → proof points → calls to action
- **PESO Model** — Paid, Earned, Shared, Owned channel integration
- **Issue/Crisis Matrix** — Probability vs. impact mapping for risk prioritisation
- **Situation-Complication-Resolution (SCR)** — Story structure for persuasive communications
- **SCCT (Situational Crisis Communication Theory)** — Coombs: matching response strategy to crisis type and responsibility level
- **Prosci ADKAR** — Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement for change communications

## Major Challenges

- Maintaining consistent message discipline across multiple spokespeople and channels
- Moving fast enough in a crisis without sacrificing accuracy
- Reaching and engaging employees in distributed, multi-site organisations
- Translating complex financial or regulatory news into accessible stakeholder language
- Managing social media amplification of negative narratives
- Balancing transparency with legal and commercial sensitivities

## Analytical Principles

1. **Audience first** — Every message must be built from the audience's perspective, not the sender's
2. **Clarity over completeness** — Stakeholders remember one clear message; a list of facts is not communication
3. **Speed in crisis** — A good message delivered fast beats a perfect message delivered late
4. **Consistency builds trust** — Repeated, consistent messaging across all channels reinforces credibility
5. **Anticipate, don't react** — Proactive issue management prevents crises; reactive communications manage damage
6. **Never speculate** — In uncertainty, acknowledge what is not yet known; speculation destroys credibility
