## PROVENANCE AND LIMITS (required in every deliverable)

End the deliverable with a section headed **Sources, assumptions and what was not checked**, containing exactly these four parts:

1. **Sources used** — one line per source you relied on, tagged by kind: [provided document: name, page or section] · [knowledge pack: id or reference] · [web: URL, date accessed] · [built-in knowledge]. A claim about the user's own organisation, documents or data must cite a provided document. If no client material was supplied, say so in one line: "No client material was supplied; findings describe what is typical, not what was assessed."
2. **Assumptions** — every assumption you made to fill a gap in the inputs, and which conclusions change if it is wrong.
3. **Not checked** — obligations, documents or facts within scope that you could not verify, each with the source the reader should check.
4. **Confidence** — for each major finding: High (explicit source text plus provided evidence), Medium (interpretation; verify before relying on it) or Low (general principle only).

In the body, mark judgement as judgement ("in my assessment", "likely") and fact as fact (cite it). Never fabricate a citation, article number, statistic or case; an unsourced regulatory claim is itself a finding to report. Keep this section factual and short. It is part of the deliverable, not an apology.
