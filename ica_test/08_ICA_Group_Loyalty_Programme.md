## 1. Programme Overview

### 1.1 ICA Kortet — The Loyalty Card
ICA Kortet is Sweden's largest retail loyalty programme with approximately 4.2 million active cardholders as of January 2025. The programme rewards customers for grocery purchases across all ICA store formats in Sweden and through ICA Online.

ICA Kortet is a co-branded programme with ICA Banken, offering an optional credit card feature (ICA Banken Mastercard) that unlocks enhanced benefits.

### 1.2 Programme Types

| Card type | Cost | Earnings rate | Extra benefits |
|-----------|------|--------------|----------------|
| ICA Kort (basic loyalty) | Free | Standard (see §3) | None |
| ICA Banken Visa/Mastercard | Free (no annual fee) | Double points on ICA groceries | Cashback, insurance, ID protection |
| ICA Banken Guldkort (Gold) | Free (income-qualifying) | Triple points | Extended insurance, lounge access |

## 2. Membership

### 2.1 Eligibility
- Any Swedish resident aged 18 or over
- Minors 15–17 may hold a junior card with parental consent; point earnings credited to parent's account
- No ICA Banken account required for basic ICA Kort

### 2.2 Registration
Customers register through:
- ICA-appen (preferred, instant digital card issued)
- ICA.se
- In-store at service desk

Registration requires:
- Full name
- Swedish personal identity number (personnummer) or date of birth
- Email address
- Mobile phone number
- Address (auto-filled from Folkbokföringen if personnummer provided)

### 2.3 Data Consent
At registration, customers are asked to opt in to:
- **Personalised offers based on purchase history** (consent rate: 71%)
- **Email communications** (consent rate: 68%)
- **Push notifications** (consent rate: 62%)

Customers may withdraw consent at any time via ICA-appen > Settings > Privacy.

## 3. Points and Rewards

### 3.1 Points Earning

| Purchase type | Points earned per SEK 1 |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Grocery purchase at ICA store (basic) | 1 point |
| Grocery purchase with ICA Banken card | 2 points |
| ICA Online purchase | 1.5 points |
| ICA Försäkring premium payment | 2 points |
| Partner purchases (see §3.4) | 0.5–1 point |

Points are credited to the member's account within 24 hours of purchase.

### 3.2 Points Redemption

| Accumulated points | Voucher issued |
|-------------------|---------------|
| 5,000 | SEK 50 voucher |
| 10,000 | SEK 100 voucher |
| 25,000 | SEK 250 voucher |

Vouchers are issued automatically when the 5,000-point threshold is reached and sent via:
- ICA-appen (digital voucher)
- Email (PDF voucher)
- Post (physical voucher, on request)

Vouchers are valid for 6 months from issue. Unclaimed vouchers are forfeited.

### 3.3 Stamkundspris (Loyalty Pricing)
In addition to points, ICA offers **Stamkundspris** — exclusive member-only prices on 80–120 selected products per week. These prices are available:
- Automatically at POS when the loyalty card is scanned
- In the ICA-appen under "Dina erbjudanden"

Stamkundspris discounts range from 15% to 50% off standard price. Items rotate weekly, selected by category managers based on category strategy and supplier funding.

### 3.4 Partner Programme
ICA Kortet earns partial points at selected partners:

| Partner | Category | Points per SEK |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Apotek Hjärtat | Pharmacy | 0.5 |
| Circle K | Fuel | 0.5 |
| ICA Banken | Financial services | Up to 2 |
| Hemnet | Property | 0.5 |
| SJ | Train travel | 0.5 |

Partners pay ICA a commercial fee per point earned at their outlets.

## 4. Personalised Offers (ICA Smart)

For opted-in members, ICA generates personalised offers using the ICA Smart AI engine:

**How personalisation works:**
1. Purchase history (up to 36 months) is analysed
2. ICA Smart clusters members into ~280 purchase behaviour segments
3. Each week, each opted-in member receives 12–20 personalised product offers
4. Offers are surfaced via ICA-appen, email, and in-store via personalised coupon print at POS

**Offer types:**
- **Category penetration:** Products in categories the member rarely buys
- **Brand switching:** Encourage trial of ICA private label vs. national brand
- **Frequency building:** Higher discount on frequently purchased items to increase visit frequency
- **Seasonal:** Holiday-relevant products based on past seasonal purchases
- **Churn prevention:** Triggered when member's purchase frequency drops > 30%

**Performance targets (FY2025):**
- Offer redemption rate: ≥ 22% (current: 19%)
- Incremental revenue per offer campaign: SEK 8–12 per active member per month
- Churn prevention effectiveness: ≥ 35% of at-risk members retained

## 5. ICA Banken Integration

ICA Kortet and ICA Banken are deeply integrated:

- ICA Banken cardholders earn 2x points at ICA stores automatically
- ICA Banken app and ICA-appen share a single login (Swedish BankID)
- Banking account balance and card spending visible within ICA-appen
- ICA-appen allows payment with ICA Banken account via ICA Pay
- Vouchers and offers visible across both apps

When a customer activates the ICA Banken Mastercard, they enter the **ICA Banken Loyalty Track**, earning:
- Additional cashback on non-ICA purchases (0.5%)
- Dedicated Gold tier at SEK 100,000+ annual ICA spend

## 6. Programme Rules

### 6.1 Account Management
- One account per individual (verified by personnummer)
- Household grouping: Up to 4 family members may link accounts to pool points (Familjekonto)
- Points are non-transferable except within a Familjekonto
- ICA may close accounts for misuse (fraudulent point accumulation, etc.)

### 6.2 Point Expiry
- Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity (no purchase recorded)
- Members receive email and push notification 60 days before expiry

### 6.3 Disputes
- Point disputes must be raised within 6 months of the purchase date
- Contact: kundservice@ica.se or via ICA-appen > Help > Loyalty Points

### 6.4 Programme Changes
- ICA reserves the right to change points rates, voucher values, and partner arrangements with 30 days' notice to members
- Material changes to the programme are communicated via email and ICA-appen
