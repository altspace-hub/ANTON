## 1. Introduction

The Risk Appetite Statement (RAS) defines the types and levels of risk that ICA Banken's Board of Directors is willing to accept in pursuit of the bank's strategic objectives. The RAS is a foundational governance document that guides all risk-taking activity across the bank.

ICA Banken's overarching risk philosophy:
> "We accept risk that is understood, controlled, and proportionate to the returns it generates. We do not accept risks that threaten our ability to serve customers, maintain regulatory compliance, or preserve the trust of ICA Group and the Swedish public."

---

## 2. Strategic Risk Context

ICA Banken's risk appetite is shaped by:
- Its role as a retail bank serving ICA's mass-market customer base
- Its position as a wholly-owned subsidiary of ICA Gruppen AB (reputational interdependency)
- Its banking licence obligations and accountability to Finansinspektionen
- Its strategic objective to grow its mortgage and savings books while maintaining cost discipline

## 3. Risk Appetite Metrics

| Metric | Minimum (hard floor) | Target | Maximum tolerance |

| CET1 capital ratio | 12.5% (regulatory minimum) | ≥ 16.0% | No defined maximum |
| Total capital ratio | 14.5% | ≥ 18.0% | No defined maximum |
| Leverage ratio | 3.0% (regulatory minimum) | ≥ 7.5% | — |
| Current CET1 (Dec 2024) | — | 16.8% | ✅ Within appetite |

Capital adequacy is monitored monthly by the CRO and reported to the Board quarterly.

### 3.2 Liquidity

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Maximum tolerance |
|--------|---------|--------|-----------------|
| Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) | 100% (regulatory) | ≥ 130% | — |
| Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) | 100% (regulatory) | ≥ 115% | — |
| Survival horizon (stressed) | 30 days | ≥ 60 days | — |
| Current LCR (Dec 2024) | — | 148% | ✅ Within appetite |

### 3.3 Credit Risk

| Metric | Appetite |
|--------|---------|
| Non-performing loan (NPL) ratio | ≤ 1.5% (current: 0.9%) |
| Loan loss provisions / net loans | ≤ 0.5% (current: 0.38%) |
| LTV (mortgage portfolio, avg.) | ≤ 65% (current: 61%) |
| Unsecured lending / total loan book | ≤ 25% (current: 26% — monitoring; remediation in 2025) |
| Single counterparty exposure | ≤ 25% of eligible capital (regulatory limit) |
| Consumer credit NPL ratio | ≤ 3.5% (current: 2.8%) |

### 3.4 Market and Interest Rate Risk

| Metric | Appetite |
|--------|---------|
| Economic value of equity (EVE) sensitivity (+200bp shock) | Change ≤ -15% of Tier 1 capital |
| Net interest income (NII) sensitivity (12M, +200bp) | Change ≤ -10% of projected NII |
| Foreign exchange (FX) open position | Minimal; FX risk not materially accepted |
| ICA Banken does not hold a trading book; market risk is primarily IRRBB |

### 3.5 Operational Risk

| Metric | Appetite |
|--------|---------|
| Annual operational risk loss (net of recovery) | ≤ SEK 50M |
| Major IT incidents (Tier 1) per year | ≤ 2 |
| Core system (T24/TSYS) unplanned downtime | ≤ 4 hours per year |
| Fraud losses (card + lending, net) | ≤ 0.15% of revenue |
| Customer data breach incidents requiring FI notification | 0 (zero tolerance) |

### 3.6 Compliance and Financial Crime Risk

| Metric | Appetite |
|--------|---------|
| Regulatory sanctions from Finansinspektionen | 0 (zero tolerance) |
| Confirmed sanctions screening failures | 0 (zero tolerance) |
| BWRA overall residual ML/TF risk rating | ≤ MEDIUM |
| STR filing timeliness (within 5 days of alert triage) | ≥ 95% compliance |
| AML training completion rate | ≥ 98% |
| KYC data quality (stale KYC > 24M) | ≤ 1.0% (currently 2.1%; remediation in progress) |

### 3.7 Reputational Risk

ICA Banken does not accept risks that would:
- Damage the ICA brand or ICA Gruppen's reputation
- Lead to public regulatory enforcement action
- Result in significant customer harm
- Generate sustained negative media coverage

Reputational risk is monitored through customer NPS, media monitoring, and regulatory intelligence.

## 4. Risk Tolerances by Category

| Risk type | Tolerance | Comment |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Strategic risk | Moderate | Willing to accept risk in mortgage growth and digital expansion |
| Credit risk | Moderate | Controlled growth within stated NPL and LTV limits |
| Liquidity risk | Low | Conservative liquidity buffer; retail deposit base |
| Operational risk | Low-moderate | Invest in resilience; accept residual operational risk within loss limit |
| Financial crime (ML/TF) | Very low | Zero tolerance for sanctions violations; accept residual AML risk ≤ MEDIUM |
| Reputational risk | Very low | ICA brand protection is a paramount concern |
| Regulatory/compliance risk | Very low | No tolerance for material regulatory breaches |
| Conduct risk | Very low | Customer harm events trigger immediate review and remediation |
| Cybersecurity risk | Very low | Critical infrastructure protection; DORA compliance |

## 5. Risk Appetite Governance

### 5.1 Monitoring and Reporting

| Report | Frequency | Audience |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Risk appetite dashboard (all metrics) | Monthly | CRO, CFO, CEO |
| Risk appetite compliance report | Quarterly | Board |
| Breach notification | Immediately | CRO → CEO → Board |

### 5.2 Breach Protocol

When a risk metric breaches its tolerance:

**Amber breach (approaching limit):**
- CRO notified immediately
- Board informed at next scheduled meeting
- Remediation plan developed within 10 business days

**Red breach (limit exceeded):**
- CEO and Board Chair notified within 24 hours
- Emergency Board meeting within 5 business days
- Immediate remediation plan; FI notification assessed

### 5.3 Annual Review

The RAS is reviewed annually by the CRO and CFO, approved by the Board. Material changes to the business or risk environment trigger an interim review.

## 6. Risk Culture

ICA Banken promotes a risk-aware culture where:
- Every employee understands their role in managing risk
- Risk concerns can be raised without fear of retaliation
- Risk management is an enabler of sustainable growth, not a barrier
- The "three lines of defence" model is understood and practised

The Board and senior management model risk-conscious behaviour through tone from the top.
