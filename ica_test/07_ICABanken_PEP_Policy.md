## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes ICA Banken's framework for identifying, classifying, and managing customers and counterparties who are Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs). PEPs are subject to enhanced due diligence (EDD) due to their elevated exposure to bribery, corruption, and abuse of entrusted power.

---

## 2. Legal Basis

- Swedish AML Act (2017:630), Chapter 3, Section 17–22
- EU 5th Anti-Money Laundering Directive (5AMLD), Article 20–22
- FATF Recommendation 12 (Politically Exposed Persons)
- EBA Guidelines on PEPs (EBA/GL/2021/02, Section 4.3)

---

## 3. Definition of PEP

### 3.1 PEP Categories

A Politically Exposed Person is an individual who holds or has held, within the last **12 months**, a prominent public function. ICA Banken classifies PEPs as follows:

**Domestic PEPs (Swedish):**
- Members of the Swedish Parliament (Riksdag)
- Members of the Swedish Government (Cabinet/ministers and state secretaries)
- Senior officials of political parties (party leaders, secretary-generals)
- Members of the Supreme Court (Högsta domstolen) and Supreme Administrative Court
- Members of the Court of Auditors (Riksrevisionen)
- Senior management of central bank (Riksbanken) — Governor, Deputy Governors
- Ambassadors and heads of diplomatic missions
- Senior military officers (Major General / Rear Admiral and above)
- Senior executives of state-owned enterprises (CEOs, board chairs of entities with >50% state ownership)
- Senior officials of municipalities and regions (Kommunstyrelseordförande, Regional Directors)

**Foreign PEPs:**
- Heads of state, heads of government, ministers, and deputy ministers
- Members of national parliaments
- Members of supreme courts, constitutional courts
- Members of courts of auditors or boards of central banks
- Ambassadors, chargés d'affaires, senior military officers
- Members of administrative, management, or supervisory bodies of state-owned enterprises

**International Organisation PEPs:**
- Senior management of major international organisations (UN, EU, IMF, World Bank, NATO, OECD, etc.)
- Members of governing bodies of such organisations

### 3.2 Close Associates and Family Members

The following persons are treated as PEPs:

**Family members:**
- Spouse or registered partner
- Children and their spouses/partners
- Parents

**Close associates:**
- Any natural person known to be in close business relationships with a PEP
- Any natural person who is the sole beneficial owner of a legal person or trust set up for the benefit of a PEP

Family members and close associates receive the same enhanced due diligence as the PEP themselves.

### 3.3 Former PEPs

Persons who have ceased to hold a PEP function are treated as PEPs for a period of **12 months** following the end of the prominent public function.

After 12 months, a risk-based assessment is made:
- If the individual's risk profile remains elevated (e.g., continuing political influence, significant wealth accumulated during tenure), PEP treatment may be extended
- Standard CDD may apply if risk assessment confirms no elevated residual risk

## 4. PEP Identification

### 4.1 Screening at Onboarding

All new customers are screened against the **Dow Jones Risk & Compliance PEP database** at the point of application. The database covers:
- 1.5+ million PEP records globally
- 500,000+ close associate and family records
- Real-time updates from government gazettes and news sources

Screening at onboarding uses:
- Full name + date of birth
- Nationality + country of residence
- Fuzzy matching at 85% confidence threshold

### 4.2 Ongoing Screening

The entire customer base is screened against the Dow Jones PEP database on a **daily batch basis**. This ensures that customers who become PEPs after account opening are identified promptly.

In addition:
- Customers are asked to self-declare PEP status at account opening and annually (at login prompt in ICA-appen)
- Adverse media monitoring may reveal PEP classification before database update

### 4.3 Self-Declaration

At account opening and annually, customers are asked:
*"Are you, or is any member of your immediate family, currently or recently (within the last 12 months) a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) — i.e., a person who holds or has held a prominent public function?"*

Self-declaration does not replace database screening. Both sources are used.

## 5. Enhanced Due Diligence for PEPs

### 5.1 EDD Requirements

All PEP-designated customers are subject to Enhanced Due Diligence:

| EDD element | Standard CDD | PEP EDD |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| Identity verification | BankID | BankID + physical ID documentation |
| Address verification | Folkbokföringen | Folkbokföringen + utility bill or similar |
| Source of funds | Declaration | Declaration + supporting evidence (payslip, tax return) |
| Source of wealth | Not required | Required — origin of accumulated wealth |
| Purpose of relationship | Declaration | Declaration + scrutiny |
| Senior management approval | Not required | **Required — CCO or CEO** |
| Ongoing monitoring | Standard TM rules | Enhanced TM rules (PEP category) |
| Periodic review | 5 years (low risk) / 3 years (medium) | **Annual** |
| Adverse media monitoring | Not standard | **Continuous** |

### 5.2 Source of Wealth Assessment

For PEP customers, ICA Banken must understand the origin of the customer's accumulated wealth (not just the source of the specific funds being deposited). This includes:

- Public salary history (cross-reference with publicly available information on compensation for the public role)
- Declared income from other sources (business interests, investments, inheritance)
- Property ownership consistent with declared wealth
- Business interests consistent with declared wealth

If the declared source of wealth cannot be adequately verified, the relationship must not be entered into.

### 5.3 Senior Management Approval

Opening or maintaining a PEP relationship requires:

| PEP type | Required approval |
|---------|-----------------|
| Domestic PEP (low-to-medium profile) | Head of Compliance + CCO |
| Domestic PEP (high-profile: minister, Riksdag member) | CCO + CEO |
| Foreign PEP | CCO + CEO |
| Family member / close associate of any PEP | Head of Compliance |

Approval must be documented in the customer file before account activation.

## 6. PEP Monitoring

### 6.1 Enhanced Transaction Monitoring

PEP customers are assigned **High-risk** customer classification in the TM system. Applicable enhanced TM rules:
- PEP-001: Any transaction >2x 12-month average — automatic alert
- PEP-002: Any international transfer — automatic review
- PEP-003: Any cash transaction > SEK 10,000 — automatic alert
- PEP-004: Any transaction to/from politically sensitive regions

### 6.2 Adverse Media Monitoring

All PEP customers are subject to continuous adverse media monitoring via Dow Jones Adverse Media database:
- Daily batch scan
- Alert triggered by: corruption, bribery, fraud, sanction, conviction, indictment keywords

### 6.3 Annual Review

All PEP relationships are reviewed annually:
- Updated source of wealth documentation if material change in wealth
- Confirmation of PEP status (still serving, ceased, decommissioned)
- Review of transaction history vs. expected profile
- MLRO sign-off on continued relationship

## 8. Exiting PEP Relationships

ICA Banken may decide not to enter into, or to exit, a PEP relationship when:
- Source of wealth cannot be adequately explained
- The risk profile is assessed as Very High and cannot be mitigated
- The PEP is from a jurisdiction with which ICA Banken has a blanket restriction
- Adverse media suggests criminal conduct

Exit process:
1. CCO documents rationale
2. CEO informed and concurs
3. Customer given 30-day notice period (statutory minimum, or shorter if crime suspected)
4. Account closed and final statement issued
5. STR filed if suspicion of ML/TF exists at point of exit

---

## 9. Non-Domestic PEPs — Enhanced Requirements

For **foreign PEPs** (including EU and non-EU):
- Physical identity verification required (face-to-face or certified copy of passport + certified address proof)
- CEO approval mandatory
- No account opened for foreign PEPs ordinarily resident outside Sweden (unless compelling reason documented)
- First transaction reviewed by MLRO before release
