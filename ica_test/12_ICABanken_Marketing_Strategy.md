## 1. Marketing Ambition

ICA Banken's marketing ambition is to be seen as **Sweden's most natural bank** — the bank that is simply part of everyday Swedish life because it is embedded in the grocery shopping experience that 4.2 million Swedes already have with ICA.

By 2026:
- Grow awareness of ICA Banken as a full-service bank from 72% to 80% among ICA loyalty members
- Increase ICA Banken product penetration among ICA Kortet holders from 20% to 30%
- Grow net promoter score (NPS) from 38 to 48
- Drive 60% of new account openings through ICA-appen (vs. 44% today)

## 2. Target Audiences

### Primary: ICA Loyalty Members Who Are Not ICA Banken Customers

**Size:** ~3.4 million ICA Kortet holders without an ICA Banken account  
**Profile:** Existing ICA shoppers, trust the ICA brand, use other banks  
**Barrier:** Low awareness of ICA Banken's full product range; inertia (existing bank relationship)  
**Message:** "You already trust ICA with your food. Why not your money too?"  
**Key offer:** Points earnings on groceries (2x vs. standard card); seamless ICA-appen integration

### Secondary: ICA Banken Current Account Holders Without Credit/Lending

**Size:** ~1 million customers with only a basic account or debit card  
**Profile:** Light users; haven't engaged with ICA Banken's full offering  
**Barrier:** Don't think of ICA Banken for credit, mortgages, or savings  
**Message:** "Your ICA bank can do more for you"  
**Key offer:** Personalised loan pre-approval in-app; ISK launch campaign

### Tertiary: Young Swedes (18–28)

**Size:** Approximately 800,000 young adults who shop regularly at ICA  
**Profile:** Digital-native; may have few or no bank products; ICA Nära/ICA Supermarket shoppers  
**Barrier:** Perceived as 'not a real bank'; prefer digital-native fintechs  
**Message:** "Banking that fits your life — no fees, no hassle, in your ICA app"  
**Key offer:** ICA Ung Konto → transition to full ICA Konto at 18; student-friendly personal loans

## 3. Brand Positioning

**ICA Banken brand promise:** "Banking made simple — because your everyday life is complicated enough."

**Key brand attributes:**
- **Trustworthy** — Backed by the ICA brand (most trusted retail brand in Sweden)
- **Simple** — No jargon, no hidden fees, no minimum balance requirements
- **Integrated** — Banking and grocery shopping in one app
- **Fair** — Competitive rates; rewards loyal ICA shoppers

**Differentiation vs. competitors:**

| Competitor | ICA Banken differentiation |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Swedbank / SEB / Handelsbanken / Nordea (Big 4) | Lower fees; ICA points; simpler digital experience; no branches needed |
| Klarna / Revolut | Full banking licence; savings products; mortgage; trusted Swedish brand |
| SBAB / Hypoteket (mortgage) | ICA loyalty bonus; full banking relationship; ICA ecosystem |
| ICA's own "do nothing" (inertia) | Tangible value (double points); easy switch; no switching cost |

## 4. Campaign Strategy

### 4.1 Always-On Channels

**ICA-appen (primary acquisition channel)**
- In-app offers: personalised credit card offer based on purchase history and current bank (estimated via creditworthiness signals)
- ICA-appen onboarding prompt: shown after 6th consecutive ICA shopping week for non-ICA Banken users
- In-app banner targeting: non-customers who have clicked ICA Banken content ≥3 times
- Push notification triggers: after large ICA purchase (>SEK 500) — savings offer; after new loyalty voucher — "Pay with ICA Banken, earn 2x points"

**Digital Paid Media**
- Google Search: "bästa sparräntan", "privatlån", "bolån", "kreditkort Sverige"
- Meta (Instagram): story format; product-benefit content; 25–45 age group
- YouTube: "Switch in 5 minutes" video tutorial
- Programmatic: retargeting of ICA.se/banken visitors; lookalike audiences built on existing ICA Banken customers

**CRM / Email**
- Monthly newsletter to opted-in ICA Kortet holders: banking tips, offer highlights, product launches
- Trigger-based: loyalty voucher email → include ICA Banken Mastercard "earn 2x points" CTA
- Post-purchase survey to new banking customers: 30-day experience survey

### 4.2 Campaign Highlights FY2025

**Campaign 1: "Sparakampanjen" (January–March)**
- Launch of competitive savings rate after Riksbank cuts
- TV (10") + digital + ICA-appen push
- Objective: 40,000 new savings accounts
- Message: "Sweden's most rewarding savings rate — and ICA points"

**Campaign 2: "Bolån launch" (April–May)**
- Digital mortgage application awareness
- YouTube 30" explainer + Google Search
- Objective: 500 new mortgage applications/month (vs. current 320/month)
- Message: "Your mortgage — sorted in 5 minutes"

**Campaign 3: "Sommarkampanjen" (June–August)**
- Travel insurance (ICA Försäkring) + Gold card with lounge access
- Partnership with ICA Group Midsommar campaign
- Message: "Travel better with ICA Banken Gold"

**Campaign 4: "Julspar" (November–December)**
- ISK fund savings launch + Christmas bonus offer (extra 0.25% savings rate for new ISK customers)
- Co-branded with ICA Group Christmas campaign
- Objective: 15,000 ISK accounts opened

## 6. Regulatory Compliance in Marketing

All ICA Banken marketing materials must comply with:
- **Consumer Credit Act (2010:1846):** APR must be displayed prominently for lending products; representative example required
- **Konsumentverket guidelines:** No misleading claims; no dark patterns in digital flow
- **Finansinspektionen conduct rules:** No undue pressure in credit marketing; suitable product matching
- **Marknadsföringslagen (2008:486):** General marketing law; no deceptive advertising

Marketing compliance review process:
1. First draft by product/marketing team
2. Legal review (1 business day SLA)
3. Compliance spot-check (for AML-sensitive communications: PEP, high-value products)
4. Approval by Head of Products + Legal
5. Published/deployed

All marketing materials archived for 3 years.
