## Executive Summary

This Business-Wide Risk Assessment (BWRA) evaluates ICA Banken AB's exposure to money laundering (ML) and terrorist financing (TF) risk across the bank's products, services, customers, delivery channels, and geographic footprint. The assessment is conducted annually in compliance with:

- The Swedish Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act (2017:630)
- FATF Recommendations (2012, updated 2023)
- EBA Guidelines on ML/TF risk factors (EBA/GL/2021/02)
- Finansinspektionen's regulations (FFFS 2017:11)

**Overall residual ML/TF risk rating: MEDIUM**

The assessment finds that ICA Banken faces a moderate inherent risk profile driven primarily by:
1. The mass-market, anonymised nature of its customer base
2. Its credit card portfolio and revolving credit products
3. Digital-only onboarding channels
4. The bank's indirect exposure to cash-intensive retail environments through the ICA store network

Mitigating controls — particularly the bank's risk-based CDD programme, transaction monitoring system, and integrated sanctions screening — reduce the overall residual risk to **MEDIUM**, which is considered acceptable within the Board-approved risk appetite.

## 1. Methodology

### 1.1 Assessment Approach

The BWRA follows the three-step risk-based approach:

**Step 1 — Inherent Risk Identification**  
Identification of ML/TF threats and vulnerabilities across all relevant risk dimensions

**Step 2 — Control Assessment**  
Evaluation of the adequacy and effectiveness of existing preventive and detective controls

**Step 3 — Residual Risk Determination**  
Residual risk = Inherent risk adjusted downward for effective controls; upward for control gaps

### 1.2 Risk Dimensions Assessed

1. Customer risk
2. Product and service risk
3. Delivery channel risk
4. Geographic risk
5. Transaction risk

### 1.3 Risk Rating Scale

| Rating | Score | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Very Low | 1 | Negligible ML/TF exposure; minimal control investment required |
| Low | 2 | Limited exposure; standard controls sufficient |
| Medium-Low | 3 | Below-average risk; enhanced monitoring in specific areas |
| Medium | 4 | Moderate exposure; active risk management required |
| Medium-High | 5 | Elevated exposure; enhanced controls required |
| High | 6 | Significant exposure; intensive controls and escalation protocols |
| Very High | 7 | Severe exposure; potential withdrawal from product/customer segment |

## 3. Product and Service Risk

### 3.1 Product Risk Matrix

| Product | Inherent ML risk | Inherent TF risk | Key risk factors |
|---------|----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Current account (ICA Konto) | Medium | Low | Layering through account-to-account transfers |
| Savings account | Low | Very Low | Limited transaction activity |
| Fixed savings | Very Low | Very Low | Static funds; long-term |
| Visa Debit card | Medium | Low | Multiple small POS transactions; foreign use |
| Mastercard Credit | Medium-High | Low | High credit limits; balance-to-cash conversion |
| Gold Mastercard | Medium-High | Low | Higher limits; international transactions |
| Personal loans | Medium | Low | Proceeds difficult to trace once disbursed |
| Mortgages | Medium-Low | Very Low | Property value inflation risk; third-party payments |
| ISK / Fund savings | Low | Very Low | Regulated investments; reported to Skatteverket |
| International transfers | Medium-High | Medium | Cross-border; some destination high-risk countries |
| ICA Pay | Low | Very Low | Low limits; linked to identified account |

### 3.2 Highest-Risk Products

**Mastercard Credit Card (inherent risk: MEDIUM-HIGH)**

Key ML vulnerabilities:
- Cards can be used for multiple small transactions below reporting thresholds (structuring)
- Balance loaded via bank transfer; cash equivalent withdrawn via ATM
- Cross-border usage creates tracing complexity
- Revolving credit can obscure source of repayment funds

Controls in place:
- ATM withdrawal daily limit: SEK 5,000
- International transaction monitoring patterns
- Unusual repayment source alerts
- Velocity monitoring on cash advance transactions

Residual risk after controls: **MEDIUM**

**Personal Loans (inherent risk: MEDIUM)**

Key ML vulnerabilities:
- Loan proceeds paid to declared bank account; actual use opaque
- Loan repayments from unexplained third-party sources
- Debt consolidation claims used to move funds

Controls in place:
- Income verification at application
- Source of repayment monitoring
- Unusual third-party repayment alerts

Residual risk after controls: **MEDIUM-LOW**

**International Transfers (inherent risk: MEDIUM-HIGH)**

Key ML/TF vulnerabilities:
- Transfers to high-risk jurisdictions
- Frequent small amounts (structuring)
- Layering into foreign accounts

Controls in place:
- Country blacklist (automatic block on FATF-listed jurisdictions)
- Transaction limits by destination country risk category
- Manual review for transfers >SEK 50,000 to medium-risk countries

Residual risk after controls: **MEDIUM**

### 3.3 Product Risk Conclusion

**Overall inherent product risk: MEDIUM**  
**Overall residual product risk: MEDIUM-LOW**

## 4. Delivery Channel Risk

### 4.1 Channel Risk Assessment

| Channel | Inherent risk | Key factors |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| In-store (staffed ICA Banken desk) | Low-Medium | Face-to-face; ID verified; lower fraud |
| ICA-appen (digital onboarding) | Medium-High | No face-to-face; impersonation/identity fraud risk |
| ICA.se (web) | Medium | Partial face-to-face controls via BankID |
| Telephone banking | Low | Transaction capability limited; BankID for auth |
| ICA Pay | Low | Linked to verified account; low limits |

### 4.2 Digital Channel Risk

ICA Banken's digital onboarding relies on **BankID** (Swedish electronic identity) for customer identification. BankID is issued by banks after in-person identity verification; therefore, digital onboarding via BankID is considered equivalent to face-to-face identification for Swedish residents.

Residual digital channel risk: **MEDIUM** (reduced from inherent MEDIUM-HIGH by BankID controls)

**Residual risk factors:**
- BankID fraud (compromised BankID credentials) — addressed through device binding and anomaly detection
- Non-Swedish residents cannot onboard digitally (manual ID process required)
- Vulnerable customer exploitation (mule accounts) — monitored via behavioural analytics

### 4.3 Channel Risk Conclusion

**Overall inherent delivery channel risk: MEDIUM**  
**Overall residual delivery channel risk: LOW-MEDIUM**

## 5. Geographic Risk

### 5.1 Domestic Operations

ICA Banken operates exclusively in Sweden. Sweden's overall ML/TF risk is assessed as **MEDIUM-LOW** based on:
- Strong AML regulatory framework (FATF-compliant)
- Active supervision by Finansinspektionen
- Effective law enforcement (FIPO, Ekobrottsmyndigheten)
- Transparency International CPI: Sweden scores 83/100 (rank 5 globally, 2023)

Domestic ML/TF threats relevant to ICA Banken:
- Organised crime (MC gangs; Middle Eastern gangs) — consumer credit exposure

## 9. Risk Appetite Statement (Summary)

The Board of ICA Banken has approved a risk appetite that:
- Tolerates a residual ML/TF risk of no greater than **MEDIUM**
- Requires immediate escalation to the Board if residual risk exceeds **MEDIUM-HIGH** in any dimension
- Accepts no relationship with FATF-blacklisted jurisdictions or UN-sanctioned entities
- Requires that all PEP relationships above Medium-High risk receive Senior Management approval

The current BWRA concludes that ICA Banken operates **within** its stated risk appetite.
