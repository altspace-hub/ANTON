## 1. Introduction

ICA Group works with approximately 3,500 Tier 1 suppliers and indirect procurement vendors globally. Our supply base is critical to delivering quality products and services to ICA's customers. We hold ourselves and our suppliers to high standards of ethical, social, and environmental conduct.

This document describes the requirements all ICA suppliers must meet as a condition of doing business with ICA Group.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:
- All suppliers of ICA private label products
- All direct-to-store suppliers of branded goods
- All logistics, technology, and service suppliers with annual ICA spend > SEK 500,000
- All Tier 2 suppliers when identified as high-risk

---

## 3. Supplier Onboarding Requirements

Before any commercial relationship may commence, suppliers must:

1. Complete the ICA Supplier Registration Portal (Leverantörsportalen) at leverantorsportalen.ica.se
2. Provide company registration documents and ownership structure
3. Submit completed Supplier Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ) covering:
   - Labour rights and employment practices
   - Environmental management system
   - Food safety certifications (where applicable)
   - Anti-bribery and anti-corruption controls
4. Accept and sign ICA's Code of Conduct
5. Provide references from at least 2 comparable retail customers
6. Pass ICA's supplier credit assessment
7. For food suppliers: provide current BRC/IFS/FSSC 22000 or equivalent food safety certification

Onboarding typically takes 4–8 weeks. ICA Procurement assigns each supplier a Supplier Relationship Manager (SRM).

## 4. Code of Conduct — Labour and Human Rights

### 4.1 Prohibition of Forced and Child Labour

Suppliers must:
- Never use forced, bonded, or trafficked labour
- Never employ workers under 15 years of age (or local minimum age if higher)
- Never employ workers under 18 in hazardous roles
- Retain employment records demonstrating age verification for all workers

### 4.2 Freedom of Association

Suppliers must:
- Respect workers' rights to form or join trade unions and bargain collectively
- Not penalise or discriminate against union members or worker representatives

### 4.3 Working Hours

Suppliers must:
- Comply with local legal limits on working hours
- Ensure regular working time does not exceed 48 hours per week (ILO standard)
- Ensure overtime is voluntary and compensated at premium rates
- Provide workers with at least one rest day in seven

### 4.4 Wages and Benefits

Suppliers must:
- Pay at minimum the applicable legal minimum wage or industry standard, whichever is higher
- Pay wages on time and in full
- Not make unauthorised deductions from wages
- Provide legally required benefits (social insurance, holiday pay, sick pay)

### 4.5 Workplace Safety

Suppliers must:
- Maintain safe and healthy working conditions compliant with local law
- Conduct regular risk assessments and implement corrective actions
- Provide adequate personal protective equipment at no cost to workers
- Record and report workplace injuries and illnesses
- Have emergency evacuation plans in place

### 4.6 Non-Discrimination and Harassment

Suppliers must:
- Not discriminate on grounds of gender, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, age, or political opinion
- Have a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and bullying
- Provide accessible grievance mechanisms for all workers

---

## 5. Code of Conduct — Environment

### 5.1 Environmental Management

Suppliers must:
- Comply with all applicable environmental laws and regulations
- Have or work toward a recognised environmental management system (ISO 14001 or equivalent)
- Monitor and report energy consumption and GHG emissions to ICA upon request

### 5.2 Hazardous Materials

Suppliers must:
- Manage and dispose of hazardous materials in accordance with applicable law
- Not use substances prohibited under REACH or equivalent regulations
- Provide ICA with Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for all relevant products

### 5.3 Water

- Suppliers in water-stressed regions must implement water efficiency measures and report water consumption annually

### 5.4 Deforestation

Suppliers of palm oil, soy, beef, timber, and cocoa must:
- Provide supply chain origin documentation
- Source from RSPO-certified (palm), RTRS/ProTerra-certified (soy), or equivalent certified supply chains
- Have a zero-deforestation commitment in place

## 6. Code of Conduct — Business Ethics

### 6.1 Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

Suppliers must:
- Never offer, promise, give, or receive bribes, kickbacks, or facilitation payments
- Have written anti-bribery policies and training for relevant employees
- Maintain accurate books and records
- Comply with the Swedish Bribery Act (Mutbrott, BrB 10:5a) and, where applicable, the UK Bribery Act 2010 and US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act

### 6.2 Conflicts of Interest

Suppliers must disclose promptly any actual or potential conflict of interest with ICA employees.

### 6.3 Gifts and Hospitality

ICA's policy: Suppliers may not offer gifts to ICA employees with a value exceeding SEK 500. Business entertainment must be reasonable and transparent.

### 6.4 Confidentiality

Suppliers must:
- Protect all ICA confidential information under the terms of the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) signed at onboarding
- Not disclose any information about ICA's business, strategies, or products to third parties

### 6.5 Competition Law

Suppliers must not engage in price-fixing, market allocation, bid rigging, or any other anti-competitive behaviour, whether with ICA or with other parties.

---

## 7. Data Protection

Suppliers processing personal data on ICA's behalf must:
- Sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) compliant with GDPR Article 28
- Process personal data only for the purposes specified in the DPA
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures
- Notify ICA of any data breaches within 24 hours of discovery
- Not appoint sub-processors without ICA's prior written consent

---

## 8. Compliance and Monitoring

### 8.1 Audits

ICA reserves the right to audit any supplier at any time, including:
- Announced audits (14 days' notice)
- Unannounced audits (no prior notice)
- Third-party audits (SMETA, SA8000, or equivalent)

Suppliers must cooperate fully with all audits and provide access to relevant records, facilities, and personnel.

### 8.2 Corrective Action Plans

If an audit identifies non-compliance:
- Minor issues: corrective action plan agreed within 30 days
- Serious issues: immediate remediation plan; ICA may suspend orders
- Critical issues (forced labour, child labour, serious safety breach): immediate suspension; contract termination if not remediated within 60 days

### 8.3 Consequences of Non-Compliance

| Severity | Examples | ICA Response |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Minor | Missing documentation, training gaps | Corrective action plan; re-audit in 6 months |
| Moderate | Unpaid overtime, minor safety violations | Corrective action plan; follow-up audit in 3 months |
| Serious | Systematic wage theft, significant environmental violation | Order suspension; intensive remediation; escalation to Procurement Director |
| Critical | Forced labour, child labour, criminal corruption, serious injury coverup | Immediate contract termination; reporting to authorities |

## 9. Reporting Concerns

Suppliers, their employees, or any other party may report concerns about breaches of this policy through:
- **ICA Ethics Hotline (EthicsPoint):** icagroup.ethicspoint.com (anonymous reporting available)
- **Email:** ethics@ica.se
- **Post:** ICA Gruppen AB, Group Legal & Ethics, 171 93 Solna

All reports are investigated. Whistleblowers are protected from retaliation under Swedish Whistleblower Act (2021:890).
