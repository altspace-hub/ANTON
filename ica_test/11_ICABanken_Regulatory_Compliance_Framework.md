## 3. Compliance Framework

### 3.1 Framework Structure

ICA Banken's compliance framework is structured around:
1. **Policy framework** — Board-approved policies covering all regulatory obligations
2. **Risk-based compliance programme** — Annual compliance plan based on regulatory risk assessment
3. **Three lines of defence** — Clear accountability at business, compliance, and audit levels
4. **Continuous monitoring** — Automated monitoring tools + compliance testing
5. **Regulatory intelligence** — Proactive tracking of regulatory change

## 4. Relationship with Finansinspektionen

### 4.1 Supervisory Relationship

ICA Banken is directly supervised by **Finansinspektionen (FI)**. The CCO is the primary point of contact for FI.

**Regular engagements:**
- Annual supervisory dialogue meeting (SREP)
- Quarterly capital and liquidity reporting
- Biannual AML supervisory assessment
- Annual ICAAP/ILAAP submission

**Ad hoc:**
- Incident notifications (operational, cyber, major AML breach)
- Approval requests (material changes, senior manager fit & proper)

### 4.2 Fit and Proper Requirements

The following individuals must be approved by FI as fit and proper:
- Board members
- CEO
- CFO/CRO
- CCO (MLRO)
- CDO
- Head of Internal Audit
- Other persons of significant influence (FFFS 2009:3)

## 6. AMLA Readiness Programme

ICA Banken has initiated its **AMLA Readiness Programme** to prepare for the EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR) and AMLA supervision framework:

**Phase 1 (2024–2025): Gap Assessment**
- Map current AML policies and procedures against draft AMLR requirements
- Identify key gaps in definitions, thresholds, and EDD requirements
- Deliver gap assessment report to CCO by Q2 2025

**Phase 2 (2025–2026): Implementation**
- Update AML/KYC policies to align with AMLR
- Upgrade CDD and TM systems for new requirements
- Implement AMLR-compliant PEP and beneficial ownership registers

**Phase 3 (2026–2027): Readiness Confirmation**
- Independent validation by external auditor
- Board approval of updated BWRA and policies
- Dry-run of AMLA reporting requirements
