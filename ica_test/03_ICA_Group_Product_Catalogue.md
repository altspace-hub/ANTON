## 1. Overview

ICA offers approximately 28,000 active SKUs across its Swedish store network. Of these, approximately 5,800 are ICA private label products. The assortment is managed centrally and tailored at store level by format and geography.

Private label penetration by value:
- ICA Basic: 8% of total grocery sales
- ICA I love eco: 5% of total grocery sales
- ICA Selection: 4% of total grocery sales
- Total private label: ~22% of total grocery sales (target: 28% by 2026)

## 5. New Product Development Process

ICA's NPD process follows a Stage-Gate model:

**Stage 1 — Insight & Ideation** (4 weeks)
- Consumer research, trend scanning, gap analysis
- Category manager initiates NPD brief

**Stage 2 — Concept Development** (6 weeks)
- Preliminary supplier shortlist
- Concept testing with consumer panel (n=200)
- Nutritional and labelling compliance review

**Stage 3 — Supplier Selection & Development** (8–12 weeks)
- RFQ issued to shortlisted suppliers
- Samples evaluated: taste, texture, nutrition, packaging
- Contract negotiation

**Stage 4 — Trial & Rollout** (4–6 weeks)
- Pilot in 20 stores (mixed formats and geographies)
- Sales and waste monitoring
- Full national rollout if KPIs met: >2.0 units/store/day in pilot

**Gate criteria for progression:**
- Consumer acceptance score ≥ 7.0/10
- Cost structure allows target margin (ICA Basic: >15%, Eco: >22%, Selection: >28%)
- Allergen, nutrition and labelling compliance confirmed
- Supplier audit (food safety & sustainability) passed

## 7. Allergen and Nutritional Labelling Policy

All ICA private label products comply with:
- EU Food Information to Consumers Regulation (EU) 1169/2011
- Swedish Livsmedelsverket guidelines
- Mandatory 14-allergen declaration on all packaging
- Nutri-Score labelling on all private label products (target A or B)
- Mandatory origin labelling for meat, fish, eggs, and honey

ICA targets to have 100% of its private label food portfolio Nutri-Score A or B rated by 2026. Current status: 68%.
