## 1. Executive Summary

ICA Banken AB is a Swedish licensed retail bank, wholly owned by ICA Gruppen AB. Founded in 2001, ICA Banken leverages the ICA Group's unparalleled reach — 1,300 stores, 4.2 million loyalty members, and daily customer interactions — to deliver retail financial services at scale. The bank operates without a traditional branch network; instead, it distributes products through the ICA store network, the ICA-appen, and digital channels.

ICA Banken holds a full banking licence issued by Finansinspektionen (Swedish Financial Supervisory Authority) and is regulated under the Swedish Banking and Finance Business Act (2004:297).

---

## 2. Mission and Positioning

**Mission:** To be Sweden's most accessible and fair bank — one that fits seamlessly into everyday shopping and life.

**Positioning:** ICA Banken does not position against traditional retail banks on prestige or full-service wealth management. Instead, it competes on:
- **Integration** with grocery shopping (earn points, pay with one app)
- **Simplicity** — straightforward products with transparent pricing
- **Accessibility** — no minimum balance requirements; products available to broad income segments
- **Trust** — backed by the ICA brand, the most trusted retail brand in Sweden (Kantar brand tracking)

## 9. Distribution Model

ICA Banken distributes exclusively through:

1. **ICA Store Network** — ~180 stores have a dedicated ICA Banken service point staffed by ICA Banken colleagues or trained ICA retailer staff. Simple products (current accounts, basic credit cards) can be opened at POS.

2. **ICA-appen** — Full digital onboarding for accounts, credit cards, and personal loans. BankID identity verification. Target: <5 minutes from application to active account.

3. **ICA.se/banken** — Web-based banking portal for account management, transfers, loan applications, and insurance.

4. **Telephone banking** — Customer service reachable at 0771-100 500, 08:00–20:00 weekdays, 10:00–17:00 weekends.

5. **Chat (ICA-appen)** — AI-assisted chat available 24/7 for FAQs; human agent available 08:00–22:00.

## 11. Strategic Priorities 2024–2026

1. **Deepen ICA integration** — Every ICA loyalty member a potential ICA Banken customer; target 30% penetration of ICA Kortet holders (current: 20%)
2. **Mortgage growth** — Target SEK 50bn mortgage book by 2026 (current: SEK 43bn); focus on urban Sweden
3. **Digital-first product UX** — All product applications fully digital; zero paper-based process by end-2025
4. **Savings and investments** — Launch ISK fund wrapper; partner with AMF or Swedbank Robur for fund distribution
5. **AI credit scoring** — Reduce credit losses by 15% through more accurate PD models by 2026
6. **AML maturity** — Complete AMLA readiness programme by Q4 2026
