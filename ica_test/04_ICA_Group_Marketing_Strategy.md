## 1. Strategic Marketing Ambition

ICA's marketing ambition is to be Sweden's most trusted, relevant, and loved retail brand — a brand that helps every Swedish family eat better, live smarter, and save money.

By 2026, ICA aims to:
- Maintain brand awareness above 98% (current: 99%)
- Achieve NPS ≥ 52 (current: 47)
- Grow active ICA-appen users to 5.5 million (current: 4.3 million)
- Drive e-commerce to 10% of grocery revenue (current: 4.2%)
- Achieve marketing ROI ≥ 3.5x across all paid channels

## 2. Target Audiences

### Primary Segments

**Segment 1 — The Swedish Family (Familjen)**  
Demographics: Households with children, ages 30–50, HHI SEK 400k–900k/year  
Size: ~1.2 million households  
Core need: Convenient, affordable, healthy meals. Limited time.  
ICA value proposition: Recipe inspiration, weekly offers, family-friendly private label  
Channel preference: ICA-appen, ICA Supermarket, e-commerce delivery

**Segment 2 — The Everyday Shopper (Vardagshandlaren)**  
Demographics: Singles and couples, ages 25–45, urban, mid-income  
Size: ~900,000 individuals  
Core need: Quick daily top-up shopping, good deals, ready-to-eat  
ICA value proposition: ICA Nära proximity, ICA Pay contactless, personalised offers  
Channel preference: ICA Nära, ICA-appen, self-checkout

**Segment 3 — The Quality Seeker (Kvalitets­sökararen)**  
Demographics: Adults 40–65, higher income, food-interested  
Size: ~450,000 individuals  
Core need: Premium produce, variety, unique products, cooking inspiration  
ICA value proposition: ICA Selection, ICA Kvantum/Maxi breadth, deli counters  
Channel preference: ICA Kvantum, Maxi ICA, loyalty programme

**Segment 4 — The Value Hunter (Priskänslige)**  
Demographics: Budget-conscious, diverse ages, lower-to-mid income  
Size: ~700,000 households  
Core need: Lowest possible spend per basket, no waste  
ICA value proposition: ICA Basic, weekly red-price offers, loyalty price (Stamkundspris)  
Channel preference: Maxi ICA, weekly leaflets, price comparison via app

## 3. Brand Platform

### Brand Positioning
**"ICA — Vi gör varje dag lite bättre."**  
(ICA — We make every day a little better.)

### Brand Architecture
- ICA (master brand) — trust, heritage, everyday relevance
- ICA Nära — closeness, community
- ICA Supermarket — balance, quality, value
- ICA Kvantum — choice, expertise
- Maxi ICA — scale, low price, destination
- ICA Banken — financial empowerment through the ICA relationship
- ICA I love eco — responsible consumption

### Brand Values
1. **Nearness** — We are part of local communities across Sweden
2. **Simplicity** — We make grocery shopping and healthy eating easy
3. **Honesty** — Transparent pricing, honest quality claims, no greenwash
4. **Inspiration** — We ignite curiosity about food and cooking
5. **Sustainability** — Doing right by people and planet

## 4. Marketing Mix

### 4.1 Channel Investment (Budget allocation FY2025)

| Channel | Budget share | Primary objective |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Digital (Search, Social, Programmatic) | 38% | Traffic, conversion, loyalty |
| TV (linear + streaming) | 22% | Brand awareness, emotional engagement |
| ICA-appen push & CRM | 18% | Retention, offers, up-sell |
| Leaflets (print + digital) | 10% | Weekly promotions, price perception |
| Out-of-home | 7% | Brand awareness, local store |
| Radio | 3% | Topicality, promotions |
| Influencer & content | 2% | Inspiration, reach among 25–35 |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

Total marketing budget FY2025: SEK 1.05 billion (Group incl. local store contribution)

### 4.2 Digital Marketing

**Search (Google & Bing)**
- Brand: always-on brand protection + Share of Voice target ≥ 85%
- Non-brand: category keywords (recipes, grocery, weekly offers)
- Shopping ads: priority for ICA Online product catalogue

**Social Media**
- Meta (Facebook + Instagram): Main brand storytelling, recipe content, family segment
  - Target: 3.2 million reached per week
- TikTok: Youth segment, recipe inspiration, trending food
  - Target: 1.8 million views per month
- YouTube: Long-form recipe videos, seasonal content
- LinkedIn: Corporate, sustainability, employer branding

**Programmatic Display**
- Retargeting: ICA-appen users, web visitors
- Prospecting: lookalike models built on loyalty card data
- Private marketplace deals with Swedish premium publishers (DN, SvD, Expressen)

**CRM / ICA-appen**
- Personalised push notifications: triggered by purchase history
- Segmented email campaigns: weekly, based on purchase clusters
- In-app banners: contextual to current shopping session
- Open rate target: 28% (industry average: 18%)

### 4.3 TV Strategy

ICA produces 3–5 major TV campaigns per year:
- **Seasonal hero campaigns** (Christmas, Midsommar, Easter) — emotional, brand-building
- **Product/category campaigns** — drive trial of private label or new ranges
- **Price/offer campaigns** — short-term sales activation

All TV is produced by ICA's long-term agency partner Forsman & Bodenfors. ICA's Christmas campaigns (Julreklamen) are culturally iconic and regularly top Swedish ad recall studies.

### 4.4 Leaflet Strategy

ICA distributes approximately 50 million printed leaflets per year through:
- Home delivery via Postnord (Maxi ICA areas)
- In-store distribution
- Digital leaflet (PDF) via ICA-appen and website

Migration to digital: target 40% of leaflet consumers on digital version by 2026 (current: 28%)

## 5. Loyalty and CRM

### ICA Kortet (Loyalty Programme)
- 4.2 million cardholders
- Points model: SEK 1 spent = 1 bonus point; 5,000 points = SEK 50 voucher
- ICA Banken customers earn double points on grocery purchases
- **Stamkundspris**: Exclusive loyalty-member prices on 80–120 SKUs per week

### Data Utilisation
ICA holds purchase history for ~3.8 million opted-in loyalty members, enabling:
- Personalised weekly offers (delivered via app, email)
- Churn prediction model (identifies at-risk members 4 weeks in advance)
- Market basket analysis for cross-sell and space planning
- Real-time personalisation engine (ICA Smart) — serves dynamic homepage per user in the app

**Data governance:** All customer data is managed under GDPR. Purchase data is retained for 3 years. Consent to personalised marketing is opt-in, current consent rate: 71% of active cardholders.

## 6. Sustainability Marketing

ICA is committed to avoiding greenwashing and applies strict internal rules:
- All environmental claims reviewed by the Sustainability team before publication
- Claims must be substantiated by supplier documentation or third-party certification
- No use of "eco-friendly" or "green" without specific qualification
- Annual Sustainability Report (GRI-aligned) published; used in B2B communications

**Cause marketing partnerships:**
- WWF: Ocean plastic reduction campaign
- Rädda Barnen (Save the Children): Food waste — "Rädda Maten" (Save the Food) campaign
- Matbanken (food bank): Surplus food donation from stores
