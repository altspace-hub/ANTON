## 1. Corporate Governance Structure

### 1.1 Legal and Regulatory Context

ICA Banken AB is a wholly-owned subsidiary of ICA Gruppen AB. As a licensed credit institution, ICA Banken is governed by:
- Swedish Companies Act (Aktiebolagslagen, 2005:551)
- Banking and Finance Business Act (2004:297)
- Finansinspektionen corporate governance requirements (FFFS 2014:1)
- EBA Guidelines on internal governance (EBA/GL/2021/05)
- Capital Requirements Directive V (CRD V), Articles 74–96

### 1.2 Governance Framework Overview

```
Board of Directors (ICA Banken AB)
    │
    ├── Audit Committee
    ├── Risk Committee
    └── Remuneration Committee
    │
    CEO (Maria Arfwidsson)
    │
    ├── CFO/CRO (Johan Berg)
    ├── CCO/MLRO (Lena Martinsson)   ← Dotted line to Board
    ├── Chief Credit Officer (Andreas Holm)
    ├── CDO (Petra Lindström)
    ├── Head of Products (Karin Söderberg)
    └── Head of Operations (Thomas Eriksson)

    Internal Audit (Henrik Sandberg) ← Independent; reports to Audit Committee
```

### 2.2 Board Responsibilities

The Board is responsible for:
- Setting and approving ICA Banken's strategy
- Approving the risk appetite and risk framework
- Overseeing the adequacy of internal control systems
- Reviewing and approving annual accounts and financial reporting
- Ensuring regulatory compliance
- Approving material policies (AML, Risk, Capital)
- Overseeing the CEO and senior management

## 3. Board Committees

### 3.1 Audit Committee

**Chair:** Anna Bjurstam  
**Members:** Anna Bjurstam, Kristina Lindquist  
**Attendees (non-members):** CFO, Internal Audit Head, External Auditor (EY)  
**Meeting frequency:** Quarterly

**Responsibilities:**
- Oversee financial reporting integrity
- Review internal audit reports; approve internal audit plan
- Review external audit findings; recommend auditor appointment
- Monitor adequacy of internal financial controls
- Review GDPR and data protection compliance
- Oversee whistleblowing reports

### 3.2 Risk Committee

**Chair:** Lars Pettersson  
**Members:** Lars Pettersson, Sara Lindgren  
**Attendees (non-members):** CRO, CCO, CDO  
**Meeting frequency:** Quarterly

**Responsibilities:**
- Oversee credit, market, liquidity, operational, and conduct risk
- Review and recommend Risk Appetite Statement to the Board
- Review BWRA (AML risk assessment) and compliance programme effectiveness
- Oversee ICAAP/ILAAP process
- Review major operational incidents
- Oversee third-party/vendor risk

### 3.3 Remuneration Committee

**Chair:** Kristina Lindquist  
**Members:** Kristina Lindquist, Erik Svensson  
**Meeting frequency:** Twice per year

**Responsibilities:**
- Review and recommend executive remuneration
- Ensure remuneration aligns with risk appetite (no incentives for excessive risk-taking)
- Review salary outcomes for identified staff (CRD V)
- Ensure compliance with CRD V remuneration rules

## 4. Internal Audit

### 4.1 Charter

Internal Audit (IA) operates under a Board-approved charter. IA:
- Is independent of all business and control functions
- Reports administratively to the CEO; functionally to the Audit Committee
- Has unrestricted access to all records, systems, and personnel

**Head of Internal Audit:** Henrik Sandberg (CIA certified)  
**Team size:** 4 auditors including Head

### 4.2 Annual Audit Plan (2025)

## 7. Three Lines of Defence

| Line | Function | Examples at ICA Banken |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| 1st line | Business units — own and manage risk | Credit, Operations, Products, IT apply controls daily |
| 2nd line | Compliance, Risk — set framework, monitor, challenge | CCO owns AML policy; CRO owns risk framework |
| 3rd line | Internal Audit — independent assurance | Annual audit plan; report to Audit Committee |

The three lines are organisationally distinct. 1st and 2nd line do not report to the same executive for their control functions.

## 9. Conflicts of Interest

ICA Banken maintains a Conflicts of Interest Policy and Register:
- All Board members and senior managers declare conflicts annually
- Transaction with ICA Group (parent) are subject to arm's length terms and documented Board approval
- Erik Svensson (ICA Group CFO) recuses from Board decisions that materially affect the ICA Group / ICA Banken balance

## 10. Subsidiary Governance (ICA Försäkring AB)

ICA Försäkring AB is a subsidiary of ICA Banken AB:
- Board: 3 members (CEO of ICA Banken + 2 independent directors)
- Separate Board meetings: quarterly
- Consolidated in ICA Banken financial reporting
- Insurance-specific regulatory reporting submitted to Finansinspektionen separately
- CCO of ICA Banken also serves as CCO of ICA Försäkring
