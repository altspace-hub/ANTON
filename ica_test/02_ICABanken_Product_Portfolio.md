## 1. Overview

ICA Banken offers a focused range of retail banking products designed for Swedish consumers. The product philosophy is built around three principles:
1. **Simple** — Easy to understand; no hidden fees
2. **Integrated** — Connected to ICA shopping and loyalty
3. **Fair** — Competitive rates; no penalty traps

## 3. Cards

### 3.1 ICA Visa Debit Card

- Included with ICA Konto
- Contactless (tap to pay)
- Apple Pay and Google Pay enabled
- Chip & PIN
- Currency conversion fee: 1.5% (waived for Euro transactions under SEK 500)
- Lost card replacement: 2–3 business days (emergency within 24h for travel)

## 6. Insurance (ICA Försäkring)

ICA Försäkring AB operates as a subsidiary of ICA Banken AB.

| Product | Coverage | Annual premium (typical) |
|---------|---------|------------------------|
| Hem­försäkring (Home insurance) | Fire, theft, liability, legal; contents up to SEK 2M | SEK 2,400–4,800 |
| Bil­försäkring (Car insurance) | Third-party, partial, comprehensive | SEK 3,500–8,500 |
| Rese­försäkring (Travel insurance) | Medical, baggage, cancellation; global coverage | SEK 1,800/year (individual) |
| Olycksfalls­försäkring (Accident insurance) | Injury, disability, death benefit | SEK 1,200/year |
| Inkomst­försäkring (Income protection) | Up to 80% of lost income; 12-month benefit | SEK 2,400/year |

**Key features:**
- All policies available digitally via ICA-appen
- Claims submitted via app with photo documentation
- ICA Kortet holders receive 10% premium discount
- ICA Banken Mastercard Gold holders: free travel insurance

**Underwriting partner:** Gjensidige Forsikring (for home and car)  
**Travel insurance underwriter:** ERV (Europäische Reiseversicherung)

## 7. Payments

### 7.1 ICA Pay
- Mobile payment integrated in ICA-appen
- Charges from ICA Konto or ICA Banken card
- Tap-to-pay at all ICA POS terminals
- Daily limit: SEK 20,000

### 7.2 International Transfers
- SEPA transfers: free, 1–2 business days
- SWIFT transfers: SEK 150 fee; 2–4 business days
- Available via ICA-appen and internet bank

### 7.3 Swish
- Integrated in ICA-appen
- Receive and send Swish payments
- Used for P2P transfers and in-app checkout
