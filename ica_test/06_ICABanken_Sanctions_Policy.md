**Version:** 3.1  
**Last updated:** January 2025

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes ICA Banken AB's framework for compliance with financial sanctions imposed by the European Union, the United Nations, the Swedish Government, and other relevant regulatory authorities. ICA Banken maintains a zero-tolerance approach to sanctions violations.

**Scope:**
- All ICA Banken employees and contractors
- All products and services
- All customers, counterparties, and transactions
- Third-party agents acting on ICA Banken's behalf

---

## 2. Sanctions Regimes

ICA Banken complies with all applicable sanctions regimes:

| Regime | Administering authority | Scope |
|--------|------------------------|-------|
| EU Consolidated Sanctions | European Commission (DG FISMA) | All EU member states; ICA Banken primary obligation |
| UN Security Council Sanctions | UN Sanctions Committee | Global; adopted into EU law |
| Swedish Government Sanctions | Utrikesdepartementet / Riksgälden | Sweden-specific designations |
| UK Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) | HM Treasury | Relevant for GBP transactions and UK counterparties |
| OFAC (US Treasury) | Office of Foreign Assets Control | Relevant for USD-denominated transactions and US nexus |

ICA Banken's primary obligation is compliance with EU and Swedish sanctions. OFAC and OFSI compliance is maintained for transactions with USD or GBP nexus.

---

## 3. Prohibited Activities

ICA Banken must not:

1. **Process transactions** involving sanctioned parties, countries, or goods
2. **Open or maintain accounts** for sanctioned individuals or entities
3. **Provide financial services** (loans, transfers, insurance) to designated persons or entities
4. **Deal in assets** owned or controlled by sanctioned persons
5. **Circumvent sanctions** — including through subsidiaries, intermediaries, or complex structures designed to obscure the sanctioned party

**Absolute prohibitions (zero-tolerance, no exceptions):**
- Any transaction with parties designated under EU Regulation 2020/1998 (Russia/Ukraine sanctions)
- Any transaction with parties on the UN Consolidated List
- Any correspondent relationship with banks in FATF-blacklisted jurisdictions (currently: Iran, DPRK, Myanmar)

## 4. Sanctioned Countries and Territories

### 4.1 Country Categories

**Category 1 — Comprehensive Sanctions (complete prohibition):**
All transactions prohibited absent a specific licence:
- Iran (EU and UN comprehensive sanctions)
- North Korea / DPRK (UN comprehensive sanctions)
- Belarus (EU targeted sanctions — significant restrictions)
- Russia (EU comprehensive sanctions since 2022)
- Myanmar (EU targeted sanctions; FATF blacklisted)
- Syria (EU and UN comprehensive sanctions)
- Cuba (limited — no ICA Banken product restriction applies domestically)

**Category 2 — Targeted/Sectoral Sanctions:**
Transactions permitted subject to enhanced screening and sector-specific prohibitions:
- Venezuela (EU targeted sanctions on individuals)
- Libya (UN arms embargo; individual designations)
- Somalia (UN arms embargo; individual designations)
- Mali (EU targeted sanctions on individuals)
- Zimbabwe (EU targeted sanctions on key individuals)
- Nicaragua (EU targeted sanctions)

**Category 3 — Enhanced Monitoring (no sanctions, but AML risk):**
No sanctions restrictions; AML enhanced monitoring applies:
- Iraq, Afghanistan, Lebanon, Nigeria, Ethiopia, Congo (FATF grey list or high risk)

### 4.2 Country List Updates

The sanctions country list is reviewed and updated:
- Weekly by the Compliance Operations team (for EU/UN/Swedish designations)
- Monthly reconciliation against OFAC and OFSI lists
- Immediately upon government designation or de-listing announcement

## 5. Sanctions Screening Programme

### 5.1 Screening System

ICA Banken uses **Dow Jones Risk & Compliance (formerly Accuity / Fircosoft)** for automated sanctions screening.

| Function | Description |
|----------|------------|
| Customer onboarding screening | Full name + DOB against all lists at account opening |
| Ongoing customer screening | Daily batch screening of all ~1.82M customers |
| Payment screening | Real-time screening of all payment instructions before execution |
| Adverse media | Daily batch screening for adverse media hits |

### 5.2 Screening Scope

Screened lists:
- EU Consolidated Sanctions List (updated 2x/week)
- UN Consolidated List
- OFAC SDN List (Specially Designated Nationals)
- OFAC Non-SDN Lists (Entity List, CAATSA)
- OFSI Consolidated List
- Swedish Government Sanctions (Riksgälden)
- HM Treasury Financial Sanctions List
- Interpol Red Notices (for internal monitoring only; not a sanctions list)
- Dow Jones PEP and Adverse Media database (supplementary)

### 5.3 Fuzzy Matching

The system applies fuzzy matching algorithms to account for:
- Name variations (e.g., transliterations from Arabic, Cyrillic, Chinese scripts)
- Common name misspellings
- Alias names
- Date-of-birth variations

**Matching threshold:** Set at 85% confidence (generates alert for review). Threshold is validated annually by the Compliance team.

### 5.4 Match Handling

| Match type | Action | Timeframe |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Confirmed sanctions match | Freeze account; block transaction; notify CCO and Board | Immediate (< 1 hour) |
| Probable match (awaiting verification) | Hold transaction; analyst review | < 4 hours |
| Possible match (false positive suspected) | Analyst review and document | < 24 hours |
| False positive confirmed | Document and dismiss; no action on account | Within SLA |

### 5.5 System Availability

The sanctions screening system must maintain:
- **Payment screening uptime: 99.95%** (equivalent to < 4.4 hours downtime per year)
- Manual backup procedure: In system outage, all international and high-risk-country payments are held until screening is restored

Uptime 2024: 99.97%

## 6. Sanctions Hits — Response Procedure

### 6.1 Confirmed Match Response

When a confirmed sanctions match is identified:

1. **Immediately:** Transaction blocked or account frozen
2. **Within 1 hour:** CCO and CEO notified
3. **Within 24 hours:** Report to Riksgälden (Swedish National Debt Office — sanctions enforcement authority) and Finansinspektionen
4. **Within 24 hours:** Assets frozen in accordance with the relevant regulation
5. **Within 5 business days:** Detailed written report to Riksgälden with supporting documentation

### 6.2 Legal Hold

All account records, transaction history, and communications related to a confirmed sanctions match are placed on legal hold. No records may be deleted or modified.

### 6.3 De-listing

If a customer is de-listed from a sanctions list:
- CCO confirms de-listing against official government notification
- Assets unfrozen within 10 business days of confirmed de-listing
- Full documentation of freeze/unfreeze maintained

## 7. Correspondent Banking Sanctions Controls

ICA Banken uses correspondent banking for SWIFT international payments. All correspondents must:
- Be domiciled in FATF-compliant jurisdictions
- Have adequate sanctions screening in their own payment chains
- Sign ICA Banken's Correspondent Banking Agreement confirming sanctions compliance

**Correspondent banks used:**
- SEB (domestic and SEPA)
- Citi (USD correspondent)
- Deutsche Bank (EUR and multicurrency)

Annual correspondent bank review confirms sanctions compliance standards.

## 8. Russia and Ukraine Sanctions (2022–present)

Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, ICA Banken implemented enhanced Russia sanctions measures:

- All existing accounts for Russian nationals reviewed (EDD completed for all 1,847 affected accounts)
- 23 accounts closed following sanctions designation review
- All transactions to Russia suspended (Category 1 prohibition)
- All Russian-issued cards declined at ICA POS terminals
- No new accounts for Russian nationals (except permanent Swedish residents with valid personnummer and pre-existing banking history)

ICA Banken's Russia/Ukraine sanctions exposure review is conducted quarterly.

## 9. Training

All employees who process payments, manage customer relationships, or make credit decisions must complete:
- Annual sanctions compliance training (30 minutes; Cornerstone LMS)
- New-hire sanctions training (within 30 days of joining)
- Event-triggered briefing when major sanctions changes occur

2024 completion rate: 97%
