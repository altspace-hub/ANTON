## 1. Executive Summary

ICA Group is one of the Nordic region's largest retail companies, operating primarily in Sweden and the Baltic states. The Group encompasses grocery retail, real estate, banking, and insurance services, making it a diversified consumer conglomerate with deep roots in Swedish society. With revenues exceeding SEK 130 billion annually, ICA Group serves millions of customers daily through physical stores, digital channels, and financial services.

ICA operates on a unique franchise-like model in which individual store owners (ICA-handlare) run their stores independently while benefiting from the collective buying power, logistics, IT infrastructure, and brand of the ICA Group. This model has been a source of competitive advantage for over a century.

## 3. Group Structure

ICA Group operates through the following primary business segments:

### 3.1 ICA Sweden
The core retail operation in Sweden. Approximately 1,300 stores across four store formats:

- **ICA Nära** – Neighbourhood convenience stores, typically 300–1,000 m²
- **ICA Supermarket** – Mid-sized supermarkets, 1,000–3,500 m²
- **ICA Kvantum** – Large-format full-service supermarkets, 3,500–8,000 m²
- **Maxi ICA Stormarknad** – Hypermarkets with extensive non-food ranges, 8,000–20,000 m²

All stores are run by approximately 1,350 independent ICA retailers (handlare) who sign franchise agreements with ICA Sweden.

### 3.2 Rimi Baltic
Retail grocery operations in Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania. Operates approximately 300 stores under the Rimi brand. Formats include Rimi Hyper, Rimi Super, and Rimi Mini. Headquartered in Riga, Latvia.

### 3.3 ICA Real Estate (ICA Fastigheter)
Manages and develops ICA's property portfolio, including retail properties, logistics centers, and offices. Portfolio valued at approximately SEK 20 billion.

### 3.4 ICA Banken
Full-service retail bank serving ICA customers. Offers accounts, cards, loans, mortgages, savings, and insurance. Wholly owned by ICA Group. (See dedicated ICA Banken documentation for detail.)

### 3.5 ICA Insurance (ICA Försäkring)
Non-life insurance products sold through ICA stores and digital channels. Includes home, car, travel, and accident insurance. Operates under ICA Banken's banking license framework for financial services.

## 5. Business Model

ICA's hybrid model combines:

1. **Wholesale & logistics** — ICA procures goods centrally and distributes to stores via regional logistics centers in Helsingborg, Västerås, Göteborg, and Sundsvall.
2. **Franchise model** — Store owners operate independently but source predominantly from ICA's central assortment. They pay fees based on turnover.
3. **Financial services** — ICA Banken monetises the customer base through retail banking, deepening loyalty and increasing lifetime value.
4. **Digital ecosystem** — ICA's app (ICA-appen) integrates shopping lists, personalised offers, recipe inspiration, loyalty points, and banking services.

## 6. Strategic Priorities 2023–2026

1. **Strengthen the ICA retailer model** — Support independent retailers with tools, training, and category expertise.
2. **Accelerate digital and e-commerce** — Grow home delivery and click-and-collect; target 10% of Swedish grocery revenue online by 2026.
3. **Sustainability leadership** — Halve Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030; expand plant-based private label range; eliminate unnecessary plastics.
4. **Financial services growth** — Expand ICA Banken customer base; launch new insurance and savings products.
5. **Baltic expansion** — Open 25 new Rimi stores in Estonia and Latvia; remodel legacy stores to Rimi Super format.
6. **Data and personalisation** — Leverage purchase data to deliver hyper-personalised offers via ICA-appen.

ICA invests approximately SEK 1.5 billion annually in technology. Key platforms:

- **ICA Online** — E-commerce platform for home delivery and click-and-collect
