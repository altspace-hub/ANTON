## 4. Store Formats and Retailer Tiers

### 4.1 Store Format Summary

| Format | Average size | # of stores | Average weekly turnover |
|--------|-------------|-------------|------------------------|
| ICA Nära | 600 m² | 480 | SEK 1.2M |
| ICA Supermarket | 2,200 m² | 490 | SEK 4.8M |
| ICA Kvantum | 5,500 m² | 190 | SEK 12.5M |
| Maxi ICA Stormarknad | 12,000 m² | 90 | SEK 35M |

### 4.2 Retailer Support Structure

Each ICA retailer (handlare) is supported by a dedicated **Retail Advisor** (butikskonsulent) who visits the store on a scheduled basis. Retail Advisors are grouped by geography:

- **Region Stockholm** — 12 Retail Advisors, ~180 stores
- **Region Götaland** — 10 Retail Advisors, ~150 stores  
- **Region Svealand** — 8 Retail Advisors, ~120 stores
- **Region Norrland** — 6 Retail Advisors, ~80 stores
