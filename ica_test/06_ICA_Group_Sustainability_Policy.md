## 1. Policy Statement

ICA Group is committed to conducting its business in a manner that is economically, socially, and environmentally sustainable. We believe that long-term business success and positive impact on people and planet are inseparable goals.

This policy applies to ICA Gruppen AB and all wholly-owned subsidiaries, and sets minimum standards that we expect our business partners and suppliers to meet.

## 2. Governance

**Sustainability Committee:** Chaired by the CSO; meets quarterly; reports to the Board

**External assurance:** Deloitte provides limited assurance on GRI-aligned sustainability disclosures  
**Framework alignment:** GRI Standards, UN SDGs (SDGs 2, 3, 8, 12, 13, 15), EU CSRD (from FY2025 reporting)

## 3. Climate and Environment

### 3.1 Emissions Targets

ICA has set science-based targets (SBT-validated) aligned with a 1.5°C pathway:

| Scope | 2019 Baseline | 2025 Target | 2030 Target | 2050 Target |
|-------|--------------|------------|------------|------------|
| Scope 1 (direct) | 48,000 tCO₂e | -30% | -50% | Net Zero |
| Scope 2 (electricity) | 82,000 tCO₂e | -60% | -90% | Net Zero |
| Scope 3 (value chain) | 3,200,000 tCO₂e | -10% | -30% | Net Zero |

**2023 actuals:**
- Scope 1: 31,200 tCO₂e (-35% vs baseline) ✅
- Scope 2 (market-based): 18,500 tCO₂e (-77% vs baseline) ✅
- Scope 3: 2,980,000 tCO₂e (-7% vs baseline) 🔄 (on track)

### 3.2 Energy

- 100% renewable electricity across all Group-owned facilities (achieved 2022)
- Solar panels installed on 85 distribution centre rooftops and 210 store rooftops
- Target: 25% reduction in energy intensity per m² of selling space by 2026 (vs 2019)
- LED lighting in 100% of stores by end-2025 (current: 91%)

### 3.3 Refrigeration

- Phase-out of HFC refrigerants by 2027 in Group-owned stores and DCs
- All new stores and DCs use natural refrigerants (CO₂ or ammonia)
- Remote monitoring of refrigeration cabinets to minimise leakage

### 3.4 Transport & Logistics

- Target: 100% of last-mile home delivery vehicles electric or zero-emission by 2027
- Target: 40% of heavy-duty DC-to-store transport on bio- or electro-fuels by 2026
- 2023 status: 22% HVO/bio-diesel in long-haul transport; 68% of urban delivery vans electric

### 3.5 Food Waste

ICA has committed to halving food waste per kg sold by 2030 (vs 2016 baseline, aligned with SDG 12.3):

| Metric | 2016 Baseline | 2023 Actual | 2030 Target |
|--------|--------------|------------|------------|
| Food waste (tonnes) | 38,500 | 28,200 | 19,250 |
| Food waste as % of revenue | 0.42% | 0.29% | 0.21% |

Key initiatives:
- Dynamic markdown pricing ("Bästföredatum" labelling)
- Food surplus donation to Stadsmissionen, Frälsningsarmén and Matbanken (3,200 tonnes donated in 2023)
- AI-driven demand forecasting reducing overstock
- "Rädda Maten" app feature: discounted near-expiry products in ICA-appen

### 3.6 Packaging

- All ICA private label packaging reusable, recyclable or compostable by 2025 (current: 82%)
- Eliminate unnecessary plastics: 120 packaging lines converted to alternative materials since 2020
- Increase recycled content in ICA private label packaging to 50% by 2026 (current: 34%)

### 3.7 Biodiversity

- ICA I love eco must meet MSC, ASC, or KRAV certification for all seafood and aquaculture products
- No deforestation in ICA's soy, palm oil, beef, or cocoa supply chains (commitment date: 2025)
- Forest-positive palm oil (RSPO Segregated) across 100% of relevant ICA private label (achieved 2023)

## 4. Products and Suppliers

### 4.1 Sustainable Sourcing Policy

All ICA private label suppliers must:
- Sign and comply with ICA's Code of Conduct (based on ILO core conventions + UN Global Compact)
- Accept ICA supplier audits (announced and unannounced) by ICA or accredited third parties
- Provide origin documentation for all raw materials
- Report GHG emissions (Scope 1 and 2) to ICA from 2025

**High-risk commodities** (enhanced due diligence required):
- Palm oil (deforestation, labour rights — Indonesia, Malaysia)
- Soy (deforestation — Brazil, Argentina)
- Cocoa (child labour — Ivory Coast, Ghana)
- Seafood (labour rights — Southeast Asia)
- Cotton (forced labour risk — Xinjiang flagged; ICA has zero sourcing from this region)

### 4.2 Supplier Audit Programme

| Audit type | Frequency | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| ICA self-assessment questionnaire | Annual | All private label suppliers |
| Social compliance audit (SMETA) | Every 3 years | Tier 1 high-risk suppliers |
| Environmental audit | Every 3 years | Top 200 suppliers by volume |
| Unannounced audit | Risk-triggered | Any supplier |

2023 audit results:
- 1,240 supplier self-assessments completed
- 118 SMETA audits conducted
- 14 suppliers placed on remediation plans
- 2 supplier relationships terminated for non-compliance

### 4.3 Nutrition

- By 2026, 80% of ICA private label food products will meet Nordic Keyhole nutritional criteria or Nutri-Score A/B
- Sugar reduction programme: 10% reduction in added sugar across ICA private label beverages and snacks by 2025
- Salt reduction: aligned with Livsmedelsverket reduction targets
- Expand plant-based private label range: target 300 plant-based private label SKUs by 2026 (current: 184)

## 5. People and Society

### 5.1 Employees

- **Equal pay:** Annual equal pay audit; ICA Sweden has achieved gender-equal pay certification
- **Gender diversity in leadership:** Target 40% women in senior leadership by 2025 (current: 37%)
- **Safety:** LTIFR (Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate) target: < 4.5 per million hours (2023 actual: 5.1 — improvement plan in place)
- **Living wage:** All Group employees paid at minimum 110% of collective agreement minimums
- **Training:** Minimum 20 hours of training per employee per year

### 5.2 Community

- ICA Retailers (handlare) are anchors of local communities across Sweden
- ICA Foundation (Stiftelse): SEK 45 million donated to child hunger and food security projects in 2023
- ICA Food Awards: Annual awards to community-focused ICA retailers
- Partnerships: Stadsmissionen, Rädda Barnen, Naturskyddsföreningen

### 5.3 Human Rights

ICA conducts Human Rights Due Diligence (HRDD) aligned with UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights:
- Annual HRDD risk mapping covering ICA's 3,500+ Tier 1 suppliers
- Supplier hotline (EthicsPoint) enabling confidential reporting of human rights concerns
- 2023: 12 human rights-related reports received; 9 resolved; 3 under investigation

## 7. Commitments and Non-Negotiables

ICA Group will never knowingly:
- Source from suppliers using forced or child labour
- Source products linked to illegal deforestation
- Use misleading environmental claims (greenwashing)
- Obstruct employees' right to organise or bargain collectively

Breach of these non-negotiables results in immediate supplier termination and, where applicable, disclosure to authorities.
