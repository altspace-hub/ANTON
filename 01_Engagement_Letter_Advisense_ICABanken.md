## 1. Background and Context

ICA Banken AB ("the Client" or "ICA Banken") has engaged Advisense AB ("Advisense") to provide advisory services in connection with the Client's Anti-Money Laundering (AML) programme maturity uplift and preparation for the EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR) and the establishment of the Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA), both expected to become effective in 2027.

This engagement builds on ICA Banken's own assessment, documented in its Business-Wide Risk Assessment (BWRA, version 5.0, approved January 2025) and its AML/KYC Policy (version 7.0), both of which identified a number of control gaps and a forward-looking need to align with the AMLR framework ahead of its transposition into Swedish law.

Advisense brings to this engagement its established Financial Crime Prevention practice, with particular depth in Swedish regulatory obligations under Lag (2017:630) om åtgärder mot penningtvätt och finansiering av terrorism, Finansinspektionen supervisory expectations, and EU-level AML policy developments.

## 2. Scope of Services

### 2.1 Phase 1 — AML Control Gap Assessment (Weeks 1–6)

Advisense will conduct a structured review of ICA Banken's existing AML framework, with particular focus on:

**2.1.1 KYC and CDD Review**
- Assessment of the Customer Due Diligence (CDD) framework against current EBA Guidelines (EBA/GL/2021/02) and draft AMLR requirements
- Review of the KYC refresh backlog (currently 38,000 customers with stale KYC > 24 months) — root cause analysis and remediation roadmap
- Assessment of EDD procedures for PEPs, non-residents, and cash-intensive business customers
- Review of beneficial ownership identification and verification procedures against AMLR Article 42 (expected)

**2.1.2 Transaction Monitoring**
- Review of the 48-rule TM ruleset in Actimize RCM for coverage, calibration, and documentation
- Assessment of alert-to-STR conversion rate (currently 1.34%) and false positive rate (86.5%) against Finansinspektionen benchmarks
- Gap assessment for crypto-asset typologies (noted as a priority gap in TM Policy §7.3)
- Review of the 4 ML models deployed in Q3 2023 — governance, validation, and explainability

**2.1.3 Sanctions Controls**
- Review of Fircosoft/Dow Jones screening configuration (name matching thresholds, list coverage, update frequency)
- Assessment of correspondent banking sanctions controls
- Review of Russia/Belarus sanctions implementation and ongoing compliance (post-2022 framework)

**2.1.4 Governance and Three Lines of Defence**
- Assessment of MLRO mandate, independence, and reporting lines
- Review of Board and Risk Committee AML reporting quality and frequency
- Assessment of first-line AML accountability (onboarding teams, operations)
- Training programme review (94% completion noted; target 100%)

**Deliverable:** Phase 1 Gap Assessment Report — structured findings with risk rating (Critical / High / Medium / Low) and preliminary recommendations. Presented to CCO and CEO within 6 weeks of commencement.

---

### 2.2 Phase 2 — AMLA Readiness Assessment (Weeks 7–12)

Advisense will map ICA Banken's current AML framework against publicly available AMLR draft text (as of Q4 2024) and AMLA supervisory expectations:

**2.2.1 AMLR Gap Analysis**
- Comparison of ICA Banken's current policies, procedures, and systems against key AMLR articles:
  - Customer due diligence (Articles 20–40)
  - Beneficial ownership (Articles 42–53)
  - Enhanced due diligence (Articles 29–34)
  - PEP requirements (Articles 35–38, enhanced vs. current 5AMLD)
  - Correspondent relationships (Articles 54–58)
  - Transaction monitoring obligations (Articles 59–68)
  - STR and FIU cooperation (Articles 69–80)

**2.2.2 Organisational Readiness**
- Assessment of compliance function capacity and capability against anticipated AMLR demands
- Review of technology infrastructure readiness (Actimize, Fircosoft, data architecture) for AMLR compliance
- Assessment of third-party/outsourcing arrangements against AMLR requirements

**2.2.3 AMLA Direct Supervision Screening**
- Assessment of whether ICA Banken meets any criteria for direct AMLA supervision (expected threshold: cross-border operations above defined thresholds; ICA Banken assessment: likely indirect supervision only)
- Preparation of briefing note for Board on AMLA implications

**Deliverable:** AMLA Readiness Report with a structured gap matrix, implementation roadmap (2025–2027), and Board presentation deck.

---

### 2.3 Phase 3 — Remediation Support (Weeks 13–24, as required)

Subject to findings in Phases 1 and 2, Advisense may be engaged to support:
- Policy and procedure drafting (AML/KYC Policy refresh, CDD Procedures update)
- TM rule development and calibration support
- KYC refresh programme design and execution oversight
- Training content development (AML specialist modules)
- Preparation for Finansinspektionen AML supervisory dialogue

Phase 3 scope and fees will be agreed separately in a Phase 3 addendum based on Phase 1–2 findings.
