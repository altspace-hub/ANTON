# Stage 3 — Vulnerabilities (Socratic script)

A **vulnerability** is a specific weakness that makes one of your threat
paths plausible. Not the threat itself, but the hole in your defences.

For each path you accepted, I'll suggest a small set of typical vulnerabilities
from the SME General pack. For each one I'll ask:
- Is this true of your business right now? (yes / no / partly)
- If "no", what evidence do you have that it's actually addressed?

Severity is 1–5. The pack has typical severities pre-set; you can override
where your context is materially different.

---

The most-cited vulnerabilities in the SME General pack:

- No multi-factor authentication on critical accounts (severity 5)
- No dual-control on outgoing payments above a threshold (severity 4)
- Payment instructions accepted by email alone (severity 4)
- Sensitive data stored without encryption at rest (severity 4)
- Backups exist but are never tested (severity 4)
- No 60-90 day cash buffer (severity 4)
- No documented leavers process (severity 3)
- No compliance / filing calendar (severity 3)
- High customer concentration (severity 3)
- Key processes undocumented (severity 3)

---

Be honest. The point isn't to look good — the point is to know where the
holes are so we can decide what to do about them in Stage 5.
