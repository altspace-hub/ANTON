# Stage 1 — Exposure Map (Socratic script)

We're going to build a picture of your business. Not the marketing picture — the
honest one. The point is to know what's exposed, so we can later see what could
go wrong and what's between you and the worst day.

I'll ask short questions. You don't need to know risk-management language.

---

**1. In one paragraph, what does your business do?**
Tell me what you sell, who you sell to, and how. Two or three sentences is fine.

**2. Who pays you, and how?**
- Mostly individuals, mostly businesses, or mixed?
- How does the money come in — card, bank transfer, cash, marketplace payouts, invoicing?
- Roughly what's your biggest payment, and how often does that happen?

**3. Who do you depend on to deliver?**
List the suppliers, platforms, or vendors you couldn't run for a week without.

**4. Who works for you?**
- Roughly how many employees and contractors?
- Anyone who handles money, customer data, or admin systems?
- Anyone who's the only person who knows how to do something important?

**5. What sensitive information do you hold?**
- Personal data (customer names, emails, IDs, payment details)?
- Employee records?
- Anything regulated (health, financial, children's data)?

**6. Where does the business live, technically?**
- One main system or many?
- Cloud, on-premise, or both?
- Anyone outside your business with admin access (IT contractor, consultant)?

**7. Where does the business live, physically?**
- One site, or many? Owned or rented?
- Stock, equipment, vehicles?

**8. What rules govern the business?**
- Are you registered with any regulator (financial, health, food, professional body)?
- Any licences that need renewing?
- Anything you've been warned about by an inspector or auditor?

**9. How does your reputation reach customers?**
- Reviews, word-of-mouth, social media, press?
- Anything bad in the last 12 months that made it into public view?

---

Once you've answered these, I'll draft a map of what we call your **exposures**
— the surfaces of the business where harm could land. You'll review the draft
and we'll edit it together. The map is the foundation; everything else builds
on it.
