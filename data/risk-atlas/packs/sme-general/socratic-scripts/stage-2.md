# Stage 2 — Threat Paths (Socratic script)

We have your exposure map. Now we walk through plausible "stories" — short
named scenarios where something actually goes wrong. We call these **threat
paths**.

I'll propose ten paths that apply to almost every business. You tell me:
**plausible**, **not plausible**, or **needs editing for our context**.

For each path I'll ask:
- Has anything like this happened to you (or a business you know)?
- If it happened tomorrow, how bad would it be?
- What's the part of YOUR business that makes it more (or less) likely?

You don't need to score yet. We're just naming the stories.

---

The ten starter paths in the SME General pack:

1. **Payment / invoice fraud** — someone tricks you into paying a fake invoice
2. **Customer or employee data breach** — sensitive data is exposed or stolen
3. **Critical supplier or platform failure** — a vendor you depend on fails
4. **Regulatory non-compliance** — missed filing, lapsed licence, employment breach
5. **Ransomware / destructive cyber attack**
6. **Key-person dependency** — one person who knows everything is unavailable
7. **Employee misconduct or insider abuse**
8. **Reputation event** — bad story goes public
9. **Physical incident** — fire, theft, accident
10. **Cash-flow shock** — late payments or a big customer loss

---

After this, I'll show you each path with the exposures and vulnerabilities
attached. You'll see the chain. We'll add or remove paths as you tell me
what fits your business.
