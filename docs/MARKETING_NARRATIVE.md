## What ANTON is, in one paragraph

We built ANTON as an AI platform with flexibility and real-life usability at its core. ANTON is **model-agnostic** — it runs any model, from the latest Claude and Mistral to local and open-source models — and it's made to suit your needs whether you're a large business, a startup, an NGO, or a private person. One week the best model might be Claude; the next week it might be GPT or Mistral or a local Llama. With ANTON you switch, because the right tool for a task changes week by week, and you should never be locked into one vendor's roadmap.

ANTON is also **smart in itself**. The more you use it, the more it learns your way of working — what good output looks like for you, how your documents fit together, the patterns inside your domain. It moves up a four-stage learning curve, from reacting to your prompts on day one to taking initiative on its own once it knows you well enough.

ANTON is **open and yours**. Your knowledge, documents, routines and information sit on your side, in a format you control. They move between models because the knowledge isn't guarded by any AI company — it's yours. If you decide tomorrow that a different model is better, your work comes with you.

And ANTON is **built for real work** — not chat. 150+ modules across 50+ professional sectors give you the structure that turns "an AI conversation" into "a piece of professional output that is reliable, repeatable, and held to a standard." No more wrestling prompt libraries. No more two sessions giving you wildly different results. ANTON tells you what the outcome should be, which approach fits, what the output looks like, what to consider, and how you and ANTON collaborate to get there.

## Why we built ANTON

Frontier AI is extraordinary at producing fluent text. It is dangerous at producing **defensible, repeatable** work.

The people who need AI most — the compliance officer drafting an SAR, the lawyer building a memo, the analyst sizing a market, the NGO writing a grant application, the small business owner trying to make sense of a regulator's letter — these people don't need a chat window. They need a workspace that:

- doesn't lock them to one model vendor;
- doesn't leak their documents to a cloud they don't control;
- gets *better* the more they use it, instead of forgetting them between sessions;
- gives them structure shaped like the real deliverable, not a wall of text.

That workspace didn't exist. So we built it. ANTON is what happens when you ask: *what would AI look like if the professional, not the model vendor, sat in the centre?*

## The four USPs

### USP 1 — Model freedom: one workspace, every model

> *"Anton is model agnostic, so it can run any model, from the latest claude and mistral to local models and open source models — it's made to suit your needs whether you are a large business, a startup, an NGO or a private person. One key feature of ANTON is that you can switch between the models and setup new ones. One week it can be ChatGPT that is best, another week it's Claude — but with ANTON you can switch and run the tasks you need with the model you prefer."*

**Why this matters.** The AI landscape changes faster than any procurement cycle. The "best model for this task" today won't be the best in three months. Buyers who lock themselves into one vendor get one vendor's roadmap; buyers who lock themselves into ANTON get *every* vendor's roadmap, automatically.

**What ANTON does.** Claude, OpenAI (incl. GPT-4o + reasoning models o3 / o4-mini), Azure OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, and Ollama — all behind one consistent interface. Switch model per session, per module, per question. The bank with an Azure tenancy and the NGO that wants free Ollama get exactly the same UX. The startup that prefers Mistral for cost gets the same outputs as the consultancy that prefers Claude for depth.

**The deeper play.** Model freedom is also a **risk-management** story for the buyer. When OpenAI changes its pricing or its safety posture or its uptime, ANTON customers are insulated — they can pivot to a different model in a single click. That isn't a feature; it's a *protection*.

> **Hero line:** *"One workspace. Every model. Switch when the world switches."*

---

### USP 2 — Self-learning: ANTON climbs a four-stage curve with you

> *"ANTON is also smart in itself and has self-learning and teaching, and the more you use ANTON it builds and learns what is your way of working, what is the right way, and how things fit together. This is done so ANTON can move up a four-grade learning curve and support you more and more — until in the end it takes its own initiative."*

**Why this matters.** Every chat tool starts over from zero every time you open it. ANTON doesn't. It remembers what you do, learns the patterns inside your domain, and gets more useful with use. AI tools that don't learn are toys; ANTON learns, so it becomes a *colleague*.

**The four stages.**

| Stage | What ANTON does | How it feels |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Reactive** | Answers what you ask, well, against rich structured prompts. | "An AI that knows my industry." |
| **2. Contextual** | Remembers your documents, your prior work, your terminology, your preferred output shape. | "An AI that knows me." |
| **3. Pattern-aware** | Detects patterns in your work — a recurring control gap, a customer profile that always returns, a trade thesis that keeps validating. Surfaces them before you ask. | "An AI that notices what I'd notice." |
| **4. Initiative-taking** | Acts proactively. Watches your inbox / your queues / your data feeds; proposes the next action, drafts the next response, flags the next risk. Asks permission, then executes. | "An AI that runs alongside me." |

**Proof point.** The Markets Pillar is the live showcase of stage 3 → 4 in production. Predictions get scored against actual market movements; calibration is tracked over time; the model that's failing gets retired and replaced. That same self-learning loop is what every pillar inherits as it matures.

> **Hero line:** *"AI tools forget you. ANTON learns you."*

### USP 3 — Your knowledge is yours: open architecture, portable, never gated

> *"ANTON is also open and where your things are truly yours, so you can setup your information how you want to work — the documents, routines, information etc. are easily interchangeable between AI models, because you have the knowledge on your side. It's not guarded by an AI company, so you are free to change."*

**Why this matters.** The most valuable thing inside an AI tool is *your knowledge graph* — your client files, your standard operating procedures, your domain shortcuts, your past work. Today, that graph lives inside whichever vendor's tool you happened to pick. If you want to leave, your graph stays behind. ANTON inverts this: the graph is **yours**, sits in your control, and moves with you to any model you choose.

**What ANTON does.** Knowledge in ANTON lives in open formats — markdown, structured JSON, signed `.anton` knowledge packs. Your documents stay on your machine (local-first; only the LLM call leaves the network). Your custom prompts, your fine-tuned module behaviour, your team's accumulated context — all of it sits in files you own. Switch from Claude to GPT to Mistral; the knowledge comes with you. Decide to host ANTON yourself instead of using the cloud version; same story. Decide to leave ANTON entirely; we'll help you export, because we built the formats to be portable.

**The deeper play.** This is the **anti-lock-in promise**. The AI industry's default business model is "your data, our moat." Ours is the opposite: your data, your moat. We make our money because the product is *better*, not because you can't leave.

> **Hero line:** *"Your knowledge. Your data. Your file. Your choice."*

### USP 4 — Built for real work: structured, repeatable, reliable

> *"ANTON is also built for real work, and real tasks. ANTON has a huge library of modules from 50+ different sectors and work areas. These modules support making work reliable, repeatable and to a high standard. It removes the hassles of prompt libraries and how different sessions get totally different results — here you are supported with what the task outcome should be, which approach, what the output should be, what to take into consideration, and how you and ANTON will collaborate."*

**Why this matters.** A chat tool gives you a blank text box and asks you to imagine the right prompt. Every user runs the same task differently. Every session produces a different result. There is no standard of work and no learning curve — every prompt is artisanal, every output is bespoke. That's fine for play; it's catastrophic for professional work.

**What ANTON does.** Every module starts with the **problem statement, not the model**. A module knows:

- **What the outcome should be** — a board pack, a gap assessment, a SAR narrative, a contract review, a market analysis. The module is shaped like the deliverable, not like a chat log.
- **Which approach fits** — the right reasoning depth, the right knowledge sources, the right framework anchor (regulation, methodology, professional standard).
- **What the output should be** — Markdown plus a structured JSON payload, optionally exported as DOCX, XLSX, PDF, PPTX, Mermaid, board deck, plain-language summary, devil's-advocate review, regulator's-eye review. Same input, many export formats. No re-running.
- **What to consider** — built-in checklists, integrity rules, sanity checks. The module surfaces what an experienced practitioner would surface.
- **How you and ANTON collaborate** — review gates, approval steps, reviewer feedback loops. ANTON proposes; you decide. The audit trail captures everything.

**The library.** 150+ modules across 50+ professional domains:

- **Compliance & financial crime** — AMLR, GDPR, DORA, NYDFS Part 500, BSA / AML federal, Swiss AMLA, UK ECCTA, MAS 626, OFAC / OFSI, and more
- **Legal** — Counsel's Desk with 22 expert personas, 8 modes (research / drafting / negotiation / litigation / opinion / memo / argument / strategy), citation tracking
- **Risk** — Risk Atlas with seven-stage deterministic threat-path methodology, 25 industry packs
- **Markets & finance** — self-learning trading research, ANTON 100 indices, predictions with calibration
- **Healthcare, education, NGO, creative, procurement, civic engagement, CRM** — and 40+ more sectors

> **Hero line:** *"Stop prompting. Start delivering."*

A workspace where the AI is **powerful but in service**, the knowledge is **yours and portable**, the work is **shaped like a deliverable**, and the system **gets better as you use it**. That is what ANTON is, and that is why people who have to be right about their work choose it.

| If you're a... | ANTON gives you... |
|---|---|
| **Compliance officer** | Audit-defensible outputs, deterministic risk scoring, a regulator-ready evidence trail, and a workspace that learns your firm's risk appetite over time. |
| **Lawyer** | Counsel's Desk with live citation tracking, 22 expert personas, 8 modes, and outputs that survive the partner's red pen. |
| **Analyst** | Markets Pillar with predictions you can score, calibration over time, and a workspace that flags patterns before you ask. |
| **Consultant** | One toolkit you can take to every client, every domain. Your accumulated knowledge becomes a saleable asset (via the upcoming marketplace), not a sunk cost. |

| **Private person** | A real AI workspace on your own machine for your own life — financial decisions, family planning, household compliance — without sending it all to a chatbot. |

## The companion picture — the suite + FutureChain

ANTON the workspace is the centre. Around it sits:

**The ANTON Suite** — four mobile apps that extend ANTON into the field.
- **Companion** (teal) — your desktop ANTON, on a phone. Approvals, voice, capture.
- **Business** (blue) — take FutureChain payments on any phone. No backend. A bakery, a barber, a market stall can take crypto in 60 seconds.
- **Pay** (sunrise) — the customer-side scanner. Scan a `futurechain:pay` QR, pay in FTC, get a receipt.
- **Communications** (navy + teal) — chat, social events, portals visitor surface. Your personal-identity ANTON.

**FutureChain** — our own payment + identity rail. ISO 20022 PACS.008 messaging, a tax engine across 27+ jurisdictions, Ed25519 throughout, a mesh dialer that talks ANTON-to-ANTON without going through anybody's tunnel. **The substrate under USPs 1 and 3.** When the marketplace launches and expertise becomes an income stream, FutureChain is how value moves between ANTONs.

## The vision in one frame

We're building toward a six-layer arc — each layer independently valuable, each making the next more powerful:

1. **Individual ANTON** — the workspace · *done*
2. **Intelligent ANTON** — self-learning, prediction tracking · *mostly done*
3. **Network** — encrypted ANTON-to-ANTON messaging · *built*
4. **Collaborative Intelligence** — ANTON-to-ANTON problem solving · *in progress*
5. **Marketplace** — knowledge packs trade between ANTONs · *next*
6. **Economy** — FutureChain settles the value · *building in parallel*

We're past layer three. Layers four, five and six are why we built layers one, two and three.
