## RECITALS

(A) The Controller wishes to use the openEXPERT / ANTON software platform for AI-assisted compliance, legal, and document analysis work.

(B) The Controller will provide personal data to the Processor for processing in connection with the platform's services.

(C) The parties wish to set out their obligations as regards such processing in accordance with Article 28 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("**GDPR**") and, where applicable, the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("**UK GDPR**").

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "**Applicable Data Protection Law**" means the GDPR, UK GDPR, and any national implementing legislation, as amended or replaced from time to time.
- "**Data Subject**" has the meaning given in the GDPR.
- "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the GDPR, and includes any personal data processed under this Agreement.
- "**Processing**" has the meaning given in the GDPR.
- "**Sub-Processor**" means any third party appointed by the Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF PROCESSING

2.1 The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including for transfers of Personal Data to a third country or international organisation, unless required to do so by applicable law.

2.2 **Subject matter:** AI-assisted document analysis, compliance advice generation, and regulatory research.

2.3 **Duration:** For the duration of the subscription agreement between the parties, plus any legally required retention period thereafter.

2.4 **Nature and purpose:** Processing of documents, communications, and queries submitted by the Controller's authorised users through the openEXPERT platform.

2.5 **Types of Personal Data:** [May include names, professional roles, email addresses, and any personal data contained in documents uploaded by Controller users.]

2.6 **Categories of Data Subjects:** The Controller's employees, clients, counterparties, and any individuals whose personal data appears in documents processed through the platform.

---

## 3. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

The Processor shall:

3.1 Process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Controller and for no other purpose, unless required to do so by applicable law.

3.2 Ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

3.3 Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including as appropriate:
- (a) pseudonymisation and encryption of Personal Data;
- (b) ability to ensure ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of systems;
- (c) ability to restore availability of Personal Data in a timely manner following an incident;
- (d) a process for regularly testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organisational measures.

3.4 Not engage Sub-Processors without prior specific or general written authorisation of the Controller. Where general authorisation has been given, the Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of Sub-Processors, giving the Controller the opportunity to object.

3.5 Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, breach notification, DPIAs, prior consultation), taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to the Processor.

3.6 Delete or return all Personal Data to the Controller after the end of the provision of services, and delete existing copies unless applicable law requires storage.

3.7 Make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and allow and contribute to audits and inspections conducted by the Controller or its mandated auditor.

3.8 Immediately inform the Controller if it believes an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

5.1 Where Personal Data is transferred outside the UK or EEA, the Processor shall ensure that such transfer is subject to appropriate safeguards, including:
- EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914) or their UK equivalents (IDTA);
- An adequacy decision; or
- Another lawful transfer mechanism under Applicable Data Protection Law.

5.2 With respect to Anthropic PBC (Claude AI API):
- Transfers to the US are governed by the EU/UK Standard Contractual Clauses entered into between the Processor and Anthropic.
- Prompts and documents are transmitted to the Claude API for processing. No Personal Data is retained by Anthropic for training purposes under the API terms of service (enterprise tier).

## 6. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

6.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.

6.2 Such notification shall include, to the extent then available:
- (a) a description of the nature of the breach, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;
- (b) the name and contact details of the data protection officer or other contact point;
- (c) the likely consequences of the breach;
- (d) measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

6.3 The Controller is solely responsible for notifying supervisory authorities and Data Subjects as required by applicable law.

---

## 7. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

7.1 The Processor shall, taking into account the nature of the processing, assist the Controller in responding to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Chapter III GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection).

7.2 The Processor shall promptly notify the Controller of any request received directly from a Data Subject, without responding to such request unless instructed to do so.

---

## 8. DELETION AND RETURN

8.1 On termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Processor shall:
- (a) at the choice of the Controller, delete or return all Personal Data and copies thereof; and
- (b) delete all Personal Data unless applicable law requires storage.

8.2 The default retention policy for uploaded documents and session data is 90 days from last access, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

## 9. AUDIT RIGHTS

9.1 The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

9.2 The Processor shall allow and contribute to audits and inspections conducted by the Controller or a mandated auditor, provided that:
- (a) reasonable notice of not less than 14 days is given;
- (b) audits are conducted during business hours and do not unreasonably disrupt operations;
- (c) the auditor is bound by confidentiality obligations equivalent to those in this Agreement.

---

## 10. LIABILITY

10.1 Each party's liability under this Agreement is subject to the limitations set out in the main subscription agreement between the parties.

10.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either party's liability for:
- (a) death or personal injury caused by negligence;
- (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
- (c) any liability that cannot be excluded by applicable law.
