The 30-second pitch

One company, four product surfaces, six layers of vision.

ANTON is a local-first AI workspace for professionals — compliance officers, lawyers, analysts, advisors, and 50+ other domains. It runs on the user's own machine, so documents never leave; it speaks to every frontier LLM (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Mistral, Ollama); and every output is audit-defensible because the architecture is deterministic where it has to be and AI-augmented where that's safe.

Around the workspace sits the ANTON suite: a Companion app that drives your desktop ANTON from your phone; a Business app that turns any phone into a FutureChain merchant POS; a Pay app for customers; and a Communications app for the social layer. Underneath sits FutureChain, our own payment + identity rail with ISO 20022 PACS.008 messaging and a tax engine that covers 27+ jurisdictions.

The point of the whole stack is the six-layer vision: individual tool → intelligent tool → network → collaborative intelligence → marketplace → economy. Each layer is independently useful. Each makes the next more powerful.

01 · Why ANTON exists

The problem with frontier AI for regulated work.

Frontier AI is extraordinary at producing fluent text. It is dangerous at producing defensible work.

Professionals whose deliverables are read by a regulator, an auditor, a partner, or a court need three things general AI tools refuse to give them:

Problem 1

A defensible record

What model ran, what prompt, what knowledge sources, what was the deterministic vs AI-rationalised answer, who reviewed it. None of the major chat tools produce a paper trail that survives an examination.

Problem 2

Local control of documents

Most compliance teams cannot lawfully paste a client file into a cloud chatbot. ANTON runs on the professional's own machine; only the LLM API call leaves the network, and the LLM provider is the user's choice.

Problem 3

Structure that matches the work

A real piece of professional output is not a "chat response." It is a gap assessment, a board pack, a risk register, a tabular review of 47 contracts. ANTON ships 150+ modules and 40+ output formats shaped like the actual deliverable.

Most "AI for professionals" tools are wrappers that ask the user to trust the AI. ANTON's posture is the opposite: the AI is a powerful intern; the workspace is the professional. The deterministic engine (Risk Atlas, Tabular Review, gap scoring) never lets the model decide a residual score or a compliance verdict — the model writes the rationale around the deterministic output. That is what makes ANTON outputs survive review.

04 · FutureChain

The payment + identity rail under the suite.

FutureChain is ANTON's own payment + identity rail. It is the technology that closes the six-layer vision: when ANTON instances pay each other for expertise, route real-money payments, and run the merchant POS that funds the network, FutureChain is the substrate underneath.

PACS.008 builder

Native ISO 20022 payment-message construction. Builder + canonicalisation + hash. Lets a payment instruction be a structured, signable artifact rather than a wire-format string.

Wallet

Ed25519 keypair + address derivation + signing. Phone-resident; private keys never leave the device.

RPC

submit_pacs008_batch and query endpoints. The chain interface.

Tax engine

FIFO / LIFO / average / specific-ID / share-pooling cost basis · holding-period rules · wealth-tax · loss-offset · K4 reporting · CSV exports. 27+ jurisdictions: SE · DE · FR · US · ES · NG · JP · SG · AE · CH · PT · CY · MT · BE · PL · CA · KR · IL · BR · KE · AU · ZA · IT · GB · IE · NL.

Reference encoder

ADR-004 versioned remittance envelope (v1+v2). The payload format that travels in a QR or a SEPA reference field.

Mesh dialer

ANTON-native E2E relay protocol — Noise IK handshake + WireGuard cipher suite. Replaces reliance on Cloudflare Tunnel. v0.1 scope = household + NGO + self-hosting SME.

Identity

Contact hashes derived from raw Ed25519 pubkeys; trust scoring; AAP (ANTON Agent Protocol) for inter-instance discovery.

Payment Gateway

Other local AI agents (Claude Desktop, Cursor, MCP clients) can transact through ANTON's wallet — gateway can be opened/closed, per-agent permissions, budget limits, approval thresholds, audit trail.

ANTON Companion · teal

The remote control for a desktop ANTON. Capacitor-wrapped, paired to a desktop ANTON instance via an Ed25519 enrollment ritual (60s-TTL QR + 6-digit out-of-band confirmation code + biometric re-confirm on critical approvals). Multi-instance: a consultant can have their personal ANTON and three client ANTONs paired.

  • Approvals — the enterprise wedge. Severity-sorted inbox; biometric re-confirm on critical/high; signed-envelope responses (Ed25519 sig + replay-protected nonce). The MLRO approves a SAR submission from the lift.
  • Voice mode — Telegram-style hold-to-talk overlay, live captions, immediate barge-in on tap.
  • Capture — camera / library / share-target → resize to 2048px → POST to ANTON. Photograph a contract, ANTON reviews it.
  • Push — APNs / FCM / web-push. Payload carries only event id + severity + opaque title + deep link, never confidential content.

ANTON Business · blue

Phone-only merchant POS for FutureChain payments. No backend. The phone is the entire merchant. Bilateral KYC + sweep agreement with an exchange partner handles FTC → SEK conversion off-app. A bakery, a barber, a market stall can take crypto in 60 seconds with no servers.

  • Simple mode: keypad → QR with ADR-004 v1 remittance reference.
  • Extended mode: cart with item catalog → QR.
  • Refunds: build + sign PACS.008 to customer.
  • Kvitto (receipt) rendering on-device as PDF, emailed via Resend / Postmark.
  • 7-year retention export (PDF + SIE) for Swedish Bokföringslagen.
  • All 38 language catalogues shipping complete.

ANTON Pay · sunrise

The customer-side counterpart to Business. Consumer scans a merchant's futurechain:pay QR and pays in FTC. QR scanning via the qr-scanner npm package (pure web). Includes a paste-a-URI fallback. Settlement is bilateral merchant ↔ exchange partner — Pay only records the customer's local receipt in IndexedDB.

ANTON Communications · navy + teal

Separate consumer app — the social / personal layer. Chat with friends. Social events. Portals visitor surface. Wallet shell. Paired to hosted ANTON for the consumer surface.

The Companion App is the user's professional surface; Comm is the consumer surface. Two intentionally separate identities — forcing a single identity creates a "your boss can find you via your party-planner profile" leak.

Design principles

  1. Light theme is the default as of v0.7.5. Three themes: light (default), dark (original ANTON look), corporate (blue-tinted enterprise).
  2. Teal = action. Never use teal for decoration; if it's teal, it's clickable or it's the brand.
  3. Progressive disclosure. Defaults should let someone click "Run" and get an excellent result.
  4. Clear labels over icon-only. Our users are 55-year-old MLROs, not 22-year-old founders. Labels win.
