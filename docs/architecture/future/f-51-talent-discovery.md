ANTON's Talent surface combines **external recruitment** (job-ad bias audit, candidate flow, dual-model decisioning) with **internal mobility** (opt-out aspiration profiles, manager-blind matching). Compliance hooks target EU AI Act Annex III + EU Pay Transparency Directive enforcement points.

## Beehive (multi-agent deliberation) — relationship

Beehive is the multi-agent deliberation surface used to generate the dual-model bias audit (and other multi-perspective tasks). Tables: `beehive_sessions`, `beehive_rounds`, `beehive_participants`, `beehive_contributions`, `beehive_message_log`, `beehive_outputs`, `beehive_human_injections`, `beehive_shared_atoms`. Status: 🟢 — primitives wired; full deliberation surface still maturing.

## Compliance hooks

| Regulation | Enforcement point in code |
|---|---|
| EU AI Act Annex III (high-risk recruitment AI) | mandatory `talent_human_decisions` row before any candidate-affecting publish/contact action |
| EU Pay Transparency Directive | blocked fields enforced at form layer; `talent_compliance_rules` evaluates job-ad text for disallowed range claims |
| GDPR (consent for internal mobility data) | opt-in only via `talent_aspiration_profiles`; deletion request handled by service-level cascade |

## `.anton career-profile` bundle

Per `32-anton-bundle-format.md` (type 44): a `career-profile` bundle carries the user's aspiration profile + skill gaps + (optionally) a sanitised CV. Travels via AAP between consenting peers (e.g. talent agencies). Signed; opt-in only.
