Reference

Glossary

Every ANTON term in one place, in plain language. Where a term has its own help page, the definition links to it.

AA — C

TermMeaning
AAP — ANTON Agent ProtocolThe protocol two ANTON instances use to talk to each other and exchange signed .anton bundles. See Agent Protocol & portals.
ADR-004The architecture decision that defines how the payment reference field is encoded — a version-tagged envelope (v1 merchant-bearing, v2 operational).
.anton bundleA portable, signed package of ANTON content — a knowledge pack, evidence pack, Risk Atlas industry pack, contact bundle or portal.
AppetiteStage 7 of the Risk Atlas — where a residual risk score sits on a 5×5 grid: within, at the boundary, outside, or unacceptable.
AreaA domain grouping of expert modules in the Work pillar — e.g. FCP, legal, audit, healthcare. ANTON has around 59.
Atom / knowledge atomA small, verified fact (e.g. a specific regulatory clause) that ANTON can inject into a prompt to reduce hallucination, or extract from output for reuse.
Behaviour profileThe empirical picture of a user's payment habits — median amount, known merchants, active hours, velocity — derived from local history. Feeds the fraud engine.
CapacitorThe framework that wraps the ANTON phone web apps into native Android (and iOS) apps.
CheckpointA pending decision an ANTON Local instance hands to a paired phone for human approval. See ANTON Companion.
Contact hashA privacy-preserving address in the form ANTON-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, derived from an Ed25519 public key. Safe to share; reveals no email or phone number.
Creditor partyThe merchant side of an ISO 20022 payment record — name, address, country. ANTON Business embeds it into the QR.

DD — M

TermMeaning
Debtor partyThe payer side of an ISO 20022 payment record — the customer's identity, from their saved payment details.
Ed25519The digital-signature algorithm that anchors identity across the whole suite — every app and instance has an Ed25519 key pair.
FCP — Financial Crime PreventionThe largest expert area in ANTON Local — anti-money-laundering, sanctions, fraud, and related compliance modules.
Fraud engineA pure, deterministic, on-device check (9 rules) in ANTON Pay and Comm that flags an unusual payment before you confirm. Advisory — never blocks. See the signals.
FTC — FutureChain tokenThe currency unit used across the ANTON payment apps.
futurechain:payThe QR-encodable URI that carries a payment request from a merchant to a customer. See The payment flow.
Inherent riskStage 4 of the Risk Atlas — risk before controls, computed deterministically as max(exposure, threat, vulnerability).
Knowledge packAn importable bundle of regulatory or domain knowledge in the .anton format.
Knowledge sourceWhere a module gets reference material — Claude's Knowledge, Online References, Local Folders, or Combined. See Knowledge & prompts.
KvittoA merchant receipt in ANTON Business, kept in a gap-free numbered ledger.
Mesh / ANTON MeshThe end-to-end encrypted transport that lets a phone reach an instance through a blind relay. See ANTON Mesh & relay.
Micro-FTCThe smallest FTC unit; amounts are stored as integer micro-FTC to avoid rounding errors.
MigrationA versioned SQL change to the PostgreSQL schema. ANTON Local has 161+.
ModuleA single pre-tuned expert task in the Work pillar — pick it, configure it, run it. ANTON has around 260.
Money profileA user's self-declared financial baseline (income source, monthly spend, typical payment). Feeds the fraud engine.

NN — Z

TermMeaning
Noise IKThe cryptographic handshake pattern the mesh relay uses — gives forward secrecy and authenticates the instance.
PACS.008The ISO 20022 credit-transfer message format. ANTON assembles PACS.008 drafts today; real signing is pending the Rust core.
PairingThe Ed25519 ritual that makes a phone trusted by an ANTON Local instance. See Pairing & enrollment.
PillarA top-level "mode of intelligence" in ANTON Local. There are 12. See The 12 pillars.
PortalA user-built ANTON web space that is also a machine-readable endpoint. See Portals.
Prompt layersThe 7 layers ANTON assembles into every LLM request — from system foundation to transparency. See Knowledge & prompts.
RelayThe WebSocket broker that forwards encrypted traffic between phones and instances, and hosts the portal registry. It can never read the traffic.
RendererA post-hoc transformer that turns a module's output into a file or visual — DOCX, Mermaid chart, heatmap, board deck. See Output & export.
Residual riskStage 6 of the Risk Atlas — risk after controls: inherent − reduction, clamped to 1–5. Computed deterministically.
Risk AtlasANTON's seven-stage, audit-defensible risk engine. See Risk Atlas.
Secure storeThe tiered on-device store for private keys — native Keystore/Keychain, else encrypted IndexedDB, else memory.
Specialized AgentA standing AI persona with its own prompt, knowledge, routing and connectors. See Specialized Agents.
SSE — Server-Sent EventsThe streaming channel ANTON Local uses to deliver LLM responses token by token.
Thinking levelHow much reasoning a run gets — quick, think, think_hard, investigate, plan_first.
Transparency logThe append-only, signed record of portal registrations that lets anyone verify the registry has not tampered with an entry.
WassupThe ephemeral status feed in ANTON Comm — posts to your contacts that expire after 24 hours.
