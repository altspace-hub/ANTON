ANTON Business — the merchant app

ANTON Business is a phone-only, no-backend tool for small and medium merchants — retailers, restaurants, event sellers, consultants — to accept FutureChain payments by showing a QR code. It is the merchant counterpart to ANTON Pay: Business makes the code, Pay scans it.

1What ANTON Business is for

ANTON Business lives at src/business/ and ships as a Capacitor Android app built with Vite and React, plus an installable PWA. It does one job: turn a sale into a futurechain:pay QR code a customer can scan, and keep a clean, gap-free record of every sale.

It is built for merchants who are not crypto-savvy. There is no node to run, no backend to host, no wallet jargon on the main screens. A shopkeeper opens the app, taps in an amount, and shows the screen to the customer. Behind that simplicity sits a proper ISO 20022 payment record and a bookkeeping-grade ledger.

2How to think about it

🧭The mental model

ANTON Business is a code generator with a cash register attached. It never receives money and it never talks to a payment network. Its job is to describe a sale precisely — amount, items, VAT, your merchant identity — pack that description into a QR, and write a receipt. The customer's app (Pay or Comm) reads the code; the actual money moves bilaterally between you and Safello, off-app. Business is the counter, not the till.

Two ideas explain almost every screen:

  • You create codes; you don't collect payments. The QR is a request. Settlement happens on the FutureChain rail between the merchant and Safello — the app's job ends when the code is shown and the receipt is written.
  • The QR is a contract, and you write it. Everything the customer sees on their Review screen — amount, your business name, the purpose, the expiry — comes from the code you generated. Getting the cart and the VAT right in Business is what makes the customer's review honest.
💡Good to know

ANTON Business has no login and no account. Your business identity is stored on the phone after onboarding. Like the other payment apps, treat the device as the system of record — and see §7 for exporting the receipt ledger so your books survive a lost phone.

3Onboarding — registering the business

Onboarding collects the details that go into every QR's creditor party and every receipt. It is a one-time form.

  1. Open the app

    The Welcome screen explains that Business turns a sale into a scannable payment code, all on the phone.

  2. Enter your legal details

    Legal business name, organisation number, and registered address. These become the ISO 20022 creditor party in every code you generate.

  3. Set your VAT status & country

    Whether the business is VAT-registered, and the country of registration. This drives how VAT appears on receipts and in Extended-mode carts.

  4. Confirm the receive address

    The Safello-arranged FutureChain receive address that customer payments settle to. Every QR carries it as the to field.

  5. You're open for business

    The Home screen offers the two sale modes. Settings — ported from ANTON Comm — cover wallet, appearance with 8 accent colours, backup and language.

How it works Merchant identity & the creditor party +

src/business/services/merchant.ts holds the onboarding profile — legal name, org number, address, VAT status, country, receive address — in the phone's local store. secure-store.ts guards anything sensitive.

payment-party.ts turns that profile into the ISO 20022 creditor party — the cn/cc/cct/cst/cpc fields (name, country, city, street, postcode) — which qr.ts then folds into every futurechain:pay code so the payer's app can build a complete PACS.008 record from a single scan.

4Simple & Extended sale modes

Business has two ways to describe a sale. They produce the same kind of QR — the difference is how much detail goes into it.

ModeHow you enter the saleBest for
SimpleTap an amount on a keypad. One number, show the QR.A market stall, a tip jar, a flat-price service — anywhere the amount is the whole story.
ExtendedBuild a cart from a pre-set item catalogue, each line carrying its own VAT rate and optional discount.A café or shop with a fixed menu, where the customer — and your books — need an itemised breakdown.
🧭Which mode to reach for

Simple mode is faster, but Extended mode is what makes your receipt meaningful. If a sale needs to show what was bought and at which VAT rate — for the customer's expense claim or your own bookkeeping — use Extended even though it is a few more taps. Simple mode is for when the amount genuinely is the entire transaction.

How it works The item catalogue & cart +

Extended mode draws on a merchant-defined catalogue: src/business/services/items.ts stores each catalogue item — name, price, VAT rate. cart.ts is the pure cart model: it adds and removes lines, applies per-item discounts, and rolls the lines up into a total with a correct per-rate VAT breakdown.

That cart total — or the Simple-mode keypad amount — is what qr.ts encodes. The two modes converge here: by the time a QR is built, a Simple sale and an Extended sale are just an amount plus an optional item count and VAT detail on the ADR-004 reference.

5Taking a payment in Extended mode

This is the fuller of the two flows. It is the same every time.

  1. Open the Sell tab, choose Extended

    The cart screen opens with your item catalogue along the bottom.

  2. Build the cart

    Tap each item the customer is buying. Adjust quantities, apply a discount on a line if needed. The running total and the per-rate VAT breakdown update live.

  3. Generate the QR

    Tap Charge. Business builds a futurechain:pay code — see §6 — with a 15-minute expiry, and renders it full-screen with a live countdown.

  4. Show the screen to the customer

    They scan it with ANTON Pay or Comm, review the amount and your business name, and confirm on their device.

  5. Mark as paid & keep the kvitto

    Once the customer confirms, tap Mark as paid. The sale becomes a numbered kvitto in the receipt ledger — see §7.

📌Example — a café order

A customer orders two coffees and a sandwich. The barista opens Sell → Extended, taps Coffee × 2 and Sandwich × 1 from the catalogue. The cart shows 12.50 FTC, incl. 25% VAT 2.50 FTC. Tap Charge, and a QR appears with a 15-minute countdown. The customer scans it with ANTON Pay, sees "12.50 FTC · Café Lindgren · Restaurant", and confirms. The barista taps Mark as paid — kvitto K-000412 is written, with the two coffees, the sandwich and the VAT line intact.

⚠Settlement is bilateral, off-app

"Mark as paid" records that the customer confirmed on their side — it does not mean the app moved money. Actual settlement is bilateral between the merchant and Safello on the FutureChain rail. For low-value, in-person sales this is fine; for a large or remote sale, confirm the funds have arrived through your Safello arrangement before handing over goods. See The payment flow.

6The futurechain:pay QR code

The QR is the whole point of the app. It is a futurechain:pay URI — a compact, structured description of the sale. The customer-side decoding is covered on The payment flow; here is what Business packs into it.

FieldWhat it carries
toThe merchant's Safello-arranged FutureChain receive address.
amountThe total in micro-FTC (1 FTC = 1,000,000 micro-FTC).
refThe ADR-004 v1 reference — an 8-char merchant id, a 12-char order id, the purpose, and optional item count / VAT / discount.
cn/cc/cct/cst/cpcThe ISO 20022 creditor party — your business name, country, city, street and postcode.
expA unix timestamp 15 minutes ahead — long enough for a queue, short enough that a stale code can't be re-used.
vThe schema version — 1.
💡Why the creditor party rides in the QR

Carrying your ISO 20022 creditor details inside the code means the customer's app can assemble a complete PACS.008 payment record from a single scan — no lookup, no network call to identify you. It is what lets a private payer end up with a proper, structured receipt of who they paid, not just an address. Getting your onboarding details right (§3) is what makes that record accurate.

How it works Building the URI with the FutureChain SDK +

src/business/services/qr.ts is a pure function with no I/O. It assembles the futurechain:pay URI, converting the FTC amount to micro-FTC, and builds the ref field via the @futurechain/sdk package's reference.encodeV1() — the ADR-004 v1 schema. The 15-minute window is the exported QR_EXPIRY_SECONDS constant.

The reference SDK module is BUILT — encode/decode round-trips deterministically. The PACS.008 builder it feeds into on the customer side is still STUB, blocked on the FutureChain Rust core (iso20022_pacs008.rs) being vendored — so the QR is fully real today, but the customer's record stays an unsigned draft until that lands. See Build status.

7The kvitto receipt ledger

Every completed sale becomes a kvitto — a receipt — and the receipts form a ledger that is built to satisfy bookkeeping law.

Gap-free numbering

Receipts are numbered K-000001, K-000002, … with no gaps, ever — the sequence the Swedish Bokföringslagen 5 kap. requires. A missing number would itself be a bookkeeping red flag, so the app simply never produces one.

Full detail & export

Each kvitto carries the line items, the per-rate VAT breakdown, the total, a status, and a timestamp. The whole ledger exports to PDF (human-readable receipts) or JSON (for an accountant or accounting software).

★A habit worth keeping

Export the kvitto ledger regularly — weekly, or at least monthly — and store the file off the phone. The ledger is gap-free and bookkeeping-grade, but it lives on one device with no backend behind it. An export you already have is worth more than a perfect ledger you can no longer reach. Treat it as part of closing the day's till.

How it works The receipt ledger & exporters +

src/business/services/receipts.ts owns the kvitto ledger. It issues the next gap-free K- number, records the line items, VAT breakdown, total and status for each sale, and persists the ledger through db.ts on the device.

Export is split out: pdf.ts renders human-readable receipt PDFs, and kvitto-export.ts produces the JSON form for accounting hand-off. backup.ts and backup-format.ts handle a full-app backup so the merchant profile, catalogue and ledger can be restored onto a new phone.

8Best practices

✓ Do
  • Enter your legal details exactly at onboarding — they become the QR's creditor party.
  • Use Extended mode whenever a sale needs an itemised, VAT-correct receipt.
  • Export the kvitto ledger to PDF or JSON regularly, and store it off the phone.
  • For a large or remote sale, confirm Safello settlement before releasing goods.
  • Show the customer a fresh code if the 15-minute countdown has run low.
✕ Don't
  • Don't treat "Mark as paid" as proof of settlement — it records the customer's confirm only.
  • Don't expect the app to move or receive money; it generates codes and keeps receipts.
  • Don't rely on the phone as your only copy of the books — export.
  • Don't reuse a stale QR after its 15-minute expiry; generate a new one.
  • Don't skip Extended mode for itemised sales just to save a few taps.
📌Example — closing the day

At the end of a market day a stall holder has 37 sales — kvitto K-000376 through K-000412, an unbroken run. From the Receipts tab she taps Export → JSON and AirDrops the file to her laptop, then Export → PDF for her own folder. The gap-free numbering means her accountant can see at a glance that nothing is missing. The phone could be lost tomorrow; the day's books are already safe.
