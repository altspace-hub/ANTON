More pillars — School, Life, Pathfinder, Community, Procure, Civic, Grow

Beyond Work, Markets, Portals and Missions, ANTON Local has seven more pillars. Each is a different mode of intelligence for a different setting — a classroom, a household, a research moment, a conversation, a purchase, a public service, a sales pipeline. This page tours all seven.

Pillars are top-level modes of intelligence, each with its own UI, services and data model, selected from the App Mode toggle. The four flagship pillars have their own help pages — Work, Markets, Portals and Missions. The seven below complete the set.

1School

School is ANTON's educational pillar — a learning interface designed so a student can work with AI while a teacher keeps oversight. It is not a chat window handed to a child; it is a classroom-shaped environment with assignment queues, progress tracking and a teacher's view over what is happening.

The defining principle is supervised learning, in the human sense: the student gets help, the teacher sees the work, and the AI's role is bounded by what the teacher allows. It belongs to the same family of thinking as Missions — capable automation with a responsible human in the loop.

2Life

Life is the personal-life pillar. Where Work serves the professional, Life serves the person — with modules built for everyday financial and consumer situations, with particular care for settings where good advice is hard to come by.

Its modules cover microfinance and bottom-of-the-pyramid (BoP) finance, consumer protection, workers' rights and mobile money. These are the same kind of pre-tuned expert modules as in the Work pillar — they simply address personal rather than professional problems, and many are written for the Global South.

💡Life and Work share the module model

If you have read the Work pillar page, you already know how Life works: pick the module whose problem statement matches your situation, fill in your context, choose a format and run. Several Life-style areas — microfinance, mobile money, workers' rights, personal finance (BoP) — exist as domain areas under server/areas/ just like the professional ones.

3Pathfinder

Pathfinder is the mode-aware research assistant — a "smart action bar". Instead of asking you to find the right pillar and the right module, Pathfinder reads what you type in plain language, works out your intent, and routes you to the right place. It is the front door for when you know your problem but not where ANTON keeps the answer.

It keeps a search history and calibrates over time, so its routing gets better the more you use it. Think of Pathfinder as a knowledgeable receptionist for the whole of ANTON Local: describe what you need, and it takes you there.

4Community

Community is the networking pillar inside ANTON Local — end-to-end encrypted ANTON-to-ANTON messaging. It lets your instance communicate securely with other instances using privacy-preserving contact hashes and a trust score, with group chats and announcement channels on top.

It is the in-workspace counterpart to the standalone ANTON Comm app, and it is ANTON's Layer 3 — "Network": the messaging fabric that later pillars of collaboration are built on. The key idea is that you are reachable without being exposed — a contact hash is a shareable address that reveals nothing about you.

5Procure

Procure is the procurement pillar. It runs a purchase as a structured cycle: define what you need, gather options, score vendors against weighted criteria, negotiate, and track the resulting contract. It turns "we should buy something" into an auditable process.

The heart of Procure is criteria scoring — vendors are not chosen on a hunch but evaluated against explicit, weighted criteria, so the decision can be explained and defended. That deterministic-scoring instinct is shared with the Risk Atlas.

6Civic

Civic is the pillar for dealing with public authorities and public services. It manages civic engagements — checking whether you are eligible for something, preparing and submitting documents, finding the right authority, and drawing on civic knowledge packs that encode how a given process works.

Its purpose is to make an opaque, bureaucratic process legible: an eligibility check tells you, before you invest effort, whether a path is even open to you, and the document-submission flow keeps track of what has been sent and what is outstanding.

7Grow

Grow is the CRM-style pillar — for managing relationships and a sales pipeline. It holds contacts, moves opportunities through pipeline stages, surfaces AI-detected signals about where to focus, and produces briefings before you walk into a meeting.

Where a traditional CRM is a passive database, Grow is meant to be active: the signals and briefings mean ANTON is reading the pipeline with you, not just storing it. Missions can even feed Grow — an outreach Mission and the Grow pipeline are designed to work together.

How it works Procure, Civic and Grow — migrations & services +

Three of these pillars were added as dedicated data models in consecutive migrations: 091_procure_pillar.sql defines the procurement cycle, vendor and criteria tables; 092_civic_pillar.sql defines civic engagements, eligibility and document submissions; 093_grow_pillar.sql defines contacts, pipeline stages and opportunities.

Each is backed by its own service layer: server/services/procure-service.ts (with procure-benchmarks.ts, procure-rfq-templates.ts and procure-vendor-directory.ts), server/services/civic-service.ts (with civic-eligibility.ts, civic-knowledge-pack.ts and civic-process-library.ts), and server/services/grow-service.ts. Grow also connects to Missions through mission-grow-bridge.ts.

How it works Community — the encryption services +

Community's end-to-end encryption is handled by a small set of services: server/services/community-crypto.ts provides the cryptographic primitives, community-e2e.ts implements the end-to-end encrypted messaging layer, and community-signing-service.ts handles message signing so recipients can verify authorship.

Identity rests on Ed25519 keys — the same primitive used across the whole ANTON suite — and the shareable address is a privacy-preserving contact hash in the ANTON-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX format. The relay (see ANTON Mesh & relay) routes only ciphertext and can never read a message. Community is the in-workspace sibling of the standalone ANTON Comm app, which uses the same identity model.

8How to think, use and work well with them

🧭The mental model — one product, seven lenses

Don't think of these as seven separate apps. Think of ANTON Local as a single workspace you can point at a situation by choosing a pillar. The pillar doesn't change what ANTON knows — it changes the shape of the help: a classroom shape, a personal-life shape, a pipeline shape. Choosing a pillar is choosing the right lens. When in doubt about which lens fits, that is exactly what Pathfinder is for.

Using them — a worked path through Pathfinder. Suppose you're a small-business owner and you type into Pathfinder: "a supplier sent me a contract and I think the price is wrong." Here is how the pillars fit together:

  1. Start in Pathfinder

    Type the situation in plain words. Pathfinder reads the intent and offers routes rather than making you guess the pillar.

  2. It routes you to Procure

    The contract-and-price intent points at Procure. You open a procurement cycle and score the supplier against weighted criteria.

  3. Check your own footing in Life

    If the question is really a personal-finance one, Pathfinder may instead route to a Life module — same pick-the-module flow as Work.

  4. Reach the other party via Community

    Need to discuss it securely with a peer or adviser? Community carries the conversation end-to-end encrypted.

  5. Record the relationship in Grow

    If this supplier matters going forward, log them in Grow so the next interaction starts with a briefing.

📌Example — a single question, the right lens

A teacher wants to set a research task and watch how a class engages with AI. They don't browse menus — they switch to School, because School is the lens for "a classroom, with my oversight." The assignment goes into the queue, students work with bounded AI help, and the teacher's view shows progress. The same teacher, that evening, switches to Civic to run an eligibility check for a school grant. One workspace, two lenses, two situations handled — no new tool learned. That is the whole point of pillars.

⚠Some pillars are younger than others

School, Procure, Civic and Grow are real, working pillars with their own data models — but they are less mature than Work or the Risk Atlas. Treat their output with the same professional judgement you'd apply anywhere, and check Build status for the honest, current picture of each.

✓ Do
  • Use Pathfinder when you know the problem but not the pillar — that's its job.
  • Pick the pillar that matches the setting, not just the topic.
  • Treat Community contact hashes as your shareable, privacy-safe address.
  • Score vendors in Procure against explicit weighted criteria, not a hunch.
  • Run a Civic eligibility check before investing effort in a submission.
✕ Don't
  • Don't treat the pillars as seven unrelated apps — it's one workspace, many lenses.
  • Don't hand a child a raw chat window when School gives a bounded, supervised one.
  • Don't assume School / Procure / Civic / Grow are as mature as Work — check status.
  • Don't use Grow as a passive address book — the signals and briefings are the value.
  • Don't fight to find a module by hand when Pathfinder will route you in one sentence.
★The whole-product habit

Learn the App Mode toggle early, and learn to reach for Pathfinder when you're unsure. The professionals who get the most from ANTON Local are not the ones who memorise every pillar — they are the ones who, faced with a situation, instinctively reach for the right lens.
