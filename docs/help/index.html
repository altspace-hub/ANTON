◆The five apps

A
ANTON Local
The desktop workspace — 12 pillars, ~260 expert modules, the Risk Atlas, Markets, Portals, Missions. The brain of the suite.
▲
ANTON Companion
Your ANTON Local instance, on your phone — approvals, push, voice, capture. Paired with an Ed25519 ritual.
●
ANTON Comm
Standalone E2E messaging, the Wassup feed, events, a FutureChain wallet with tax engine — no ANTON Local needed.
⟪
ANTON Business
The merchant app — generate payment QR codes, simple or itemised, with a gap-free receipt ledger.
☀
ANTON Pay
The customer app — scan a merchant QR, review the ISO 20022 detail, run a 9-rule fraud check, pay.
