5. Fees

The network fee is fixed by protocol rule at 0.1% of the amount, capped at 0.1 FTC and floored at 250 satoshi. Your device computes that fee and signs it into the payment before it is sent, so it cannot be changed afterwards; a node can accept the payment or reject it, but cannot recompute the fee. The fee is paid to whoever settles the block. It is not revenue we collect, and there is no fee auction or priority lane.

Because the Apps are self-custody, your funds and your identity keys remain yours whatever happens to our infrastructure, and you may point an app at a different node, including one you run yourself.

8. Public blockchain data

Payments on the FutureChain network are public and permanent. The amount, the fee, the sender and recipient addresses, the time and the settling block are visible to anyone, for ever, and cannot be edited or withdrawn.

A payment also carries a structured ISO 20022 message containing the payer's name and any reference you attach. That message is not encrypted — the protocol field it travels in is misleadingly named — and it is read by the node that screens your payment. Today that node discards it after screening, so it is not retrievable afterwards by anyone. Treat anything you write into a payment reference as readable by whoever handles the payment, and never put a secret in one.

9. Compliance screening

Payments are screened before they settle. A payment that fails screening is rejected rather than settled, and we may decline to relay or submit it. The screening decision is recorded with the payment. We may be required to retain or disclose information in response to a lawful request.
