ANTON · FutureChain

Delete your data

Version 1.0 · Draft for counsel · 6 September 2026

Applies to ANTON Comm, ANTON Pay, ANTON Business and ANTON Companion.

Before publishing: confirm the request mailbox in §5 is monitored and that the controller identity matches the one filled into the Privacy Policy. This page must be reachable without installing any app and without signing in — that is a Google Play requirement for the account-deletion URL, not a stylistic preference.

The ANTON apps are built so that deletion is something you carry out yourself, on your own device, without asking us. This page explains what each app deletes, what survives deletion, and how to ask us to remove the little that is not in your hands.

1. The short version

None of the four apps has an account on our servers in the ordinary sense — there is no username, no password, and no profile we hold about you. What identifies you is a keypair your phone generates and keeps. Deleting it on the device is the deletion.

Two things are exceptions, and we would rather name them than let you discover them:

  • ANTON Comm registers a push token with our relay so that a message can wake your phone. That is a durable record on our infrastructure. Signing out deletes it.
  • Payments confirmed on the FutureChain ledger cannot be deleted by anyone, ourselves included. That is what a ledger is. See §4.

2. Deleting in the app

AppWhereWhat it removes
ANTON Comm Settings → Sign out Your identity key (including the copy held in the Android Keystore), every message, contact, group and draft on the device, and the push registration held on our relay. Confirmation is required, and without your recovery phrase the action cannot be undone.
ANTON Pay Settings → Reset app The wallet key, the recovery phrase, locally held transaction history, and your tax and profile settings. Your on-chain balance is not affected — it belongs to the key, so restoring the recovery phrase reaches it again. Resetting without a backup is permanent loss of those funds.
ANTON Business Settings → Reset Merchant configuration, the watch-only wallet address, the item catalogue, and the receipt and Z-report ledger stored on the till. Read §3 first if you trade in Sweden.
ANTON Companion Settings → Unpair this device The device certificate and session token, and the device’s entry on the ANTON instance you paired with. Content created through Companion lives on that instance, which you or your organisation operate — delete it there.

Uninstalling the app afterwards clears anything the operating system still holds for it, including the hardware-backed key material in the Android Keystore.

Uninstalling on its own is enough for Pay, Business and Companion — they hold nothing on our servers. For Comm, sign out before uninstalling if you can: the push registration is deleted by a request your phone signs with the identity key, so once the app is gone that request can no longer be made. If you have already uninstalled, use §5 and we will remove it for you.

3. What deletion does not reach

Your own record-keeping obligations

If you use ANTON Business as a till, Swedish bookkeeping law (bokföringslagen 5 kap. and SKVFS 2021:17) requires you to retain receipt and journal data for seven years. Deleting the app does not discharge that duty, and we hold no copy to fall back on. Export your SIE 4 file and receipt archive before you reset.

Copies held by the people you dealt with

A message you sent is on the recipient’s phone. A receipt you issued is in the customer’s wallet. Deleting your copy does not delete theirs, and we have no way to reach into someone else’s device.

Undelivered messages in transit

When a Comm message cannot be delivered immediately, the relay holds the encrypted envelope in memory for up to seven days (or 100 messages per recipient, whichever comes first) and then discards it. It is never written to disk, and we cannot read it — we hold no key for it. It disappears on its own; nothing needs to be requested.

4. The ledger

A payment confirmed on the FutureChain ledger is permanent and public. Neither you nor we can edit or erase it. This is inherent to how a ledger works, not a policy we could choose to change.

What the ledger holds is an address, an amount and a time — not your name. The link between an address and a person lives on your device, and deleting the app breaks it on our side of the world. It does not break it for anyone who already knows the address is yours.

We think this is a limitation you are entitled to know about before you use the apps rather than when you ask us to delete something. The Privacy Policy states it in the same terms.

5. Asking us to delete something

Write to info@futurechain.solutions with the subject Deletion request. Tell us which app, and — if the request concerns a Comm push registration — the routing identifier shown under Settings → About, so we can find the right row.

We never need your recovery phrase or any private key, and we will never ask for one. Anybody who does is trying to take your funds or your identity. Do not put a recovery phrase in an email, to us or to anyone.

We answer deletion requests within 30 days, as Art. 12(3) GDPR requires. If a request concerns data we do not hold, we will say so plainly and point at where it does live rather than simply closing the request.

You may also complain to your supervisory authority. In Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

6. Deleting the app is not deleting your money

This has its own heading because it is the mistake that costs people the most. In Pay and Business your funds are held by a key, not by an account with us. If you delete the app without having written down the recovery phrase, the key is gone and no one — not you, not us, not the ledger — can restore access to those funds. There is no password reset in self-custody. Back up the phrase first, then delete.
