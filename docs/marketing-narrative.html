Why we built ANTON

Frontier AI is extraordinary at fluent text. It's dangerous at defensible, repeatable work.

The people who need AI most — the compliance officer drafting an SAR, the lawyer building a memo, the analyst sizing a market, the NGO writing a grant application, the small-business owner trying to make sense of a regulator's letter — these people don't need a chat window. They need a workspace that:

  • doesn't lock them to one model vendor,
  • doesn't leak their documents to a cloud they don't control,
  • gets better the more they use it, instead of forgetting them between sessions,
  • gives them structure shaped like the real deliverable — not a wall of text.

That workspace didn't exist. So we built it. ANTON is what happens when you ask: what would AI look like if the professional, not the model vendor, sat in the centre?

1 Model Freedom

One workspace. Every model. Switch when the world switches.

ANTON is model-agnostic, so it can run any model — from the latest Claude and Mistral to local and open-source models — and it's made to suit your needs whether you're a large business, a startup, an NGO or a private person. One week it can be ChatGPT that's best, another week it's Claude. With ANTON you switch, and run the tasks you need with the model you prefer. — Founder note

Why this matters

The AI landscape changes faster than any procurement cycle. The "best model for this task" today won't be the best in three months. Buyers who lock themselves into one vendor get one vendor's roadmap; buyers who lock themselves into ANTON get every vendor's roadmap, automatically.

What ANTON does

Claude, OpenAI (including GPT-4o + reasoning models o3 / o4-mini), Azure OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, and Ollama — all behind one consistent interface. Switch model per session, per module, per question. The bank with an Azure tenancy and the NGO that wants free Ollama get the same UX. The startup that prefers Mistral for cost gets the same outputs as the consultancy that prefers Claude for depth.

The deeper play

Model freedom is also a risk-management story for the buyer. When OpenAI changes its pricing or its safety posture or its uptime, ANTON customers are insulated — they can pivot to a different model in a single click. That isn't a feature; it's a protection.

"One workspace. Every model. Switch when the world switches."
2 Self-learning

AI tools forget you. ANTON learns you.

ANTON is smart in itself, and has self-learning and teaching. The more you use ANTON, it builds and learns your way of working — what is the right way, how things fit together. ANTON moves up a four-grade learning curve and supports you more and more, until in the end it takes its own initiative. — Founder note

Why this matters

Every chat tool starts over from zero every time you open it. ANTON doesn't. It remembers what you do, learns the patterns inside your domain, and gets more useful with use. AI tools that don't learn are toys; ANTON learns, so it becomes a colleague.

3 Your knowledge. Yours.

Open architecture. Portable. Never gated.

ANTON is open and your things are truly yours. You can set up your information how you want to work — the documents, routines, information are easily interchangeable between AI models, because you have the knowledge on your side. It's not guarded by an AI company, so you are free to change. — Founder note

Why this matters

The most valuable thing inside an AI tool is your knowledge graph — your client files, your standard operating procedures, your domain shortcuts, your past work. Today, that graph lives inside whichever vendor's tool you happened to pick. If you want to leave, your graph stays behind. ANTON inverts this: the graph is yours, sits in your control, and moves with you to any model you choose.

What ANTON does

Knowledge in ANTON lives in open formats — markdown, structured JSON, signed .anton knowledge packs. Your documents stay on your machine (local-first; only the LLM call leaves the network). Your custom prompts, your fine-tuned module behaviour, your team's accumulated context — all of it sits in files you own. Switch from Claude to GPT to Mistral; the knowledge comes with you. Decide to host ANTON yourself instead of using the cloud version; same story. Decide to leave ANTON entirely; we'll help you export, because we built the formats to be portable.

The deeper play

This is the anti-lock-in promise. The AI industry's default business model is "your data, our moat." Ours is the opposite: your data, your moat. We make our money because the product is better, not because you can't leave.

"Your knowledge. Your data. Your file. Your choice."
4 Built for real work

Stop prompting. Start delivering.

ANTON is built for real work and real tasks. It has a huge library of modules from 50+ different sectors and work areas. These modules make work reliable, repeatable and to a high standard. No more wrestling prompt libraries. No more sessions giving totally different results. Here you are supported with what the outcome should be, which approach, what the output should look like, what to consider, and how you and ANTON collaborate. — Founder note

Why this matters

A chat tool gives you a blank text box and asks you to imagine the right prompt. Every user runs the same task differently. Every session produces a different result. There is no standard of work, and no learning curve — every prompt is artisanal, every output is bespoke. That's fine for play; it's catastrophic for professional work.

What ANTON does

Every module starts with the problem statement, not the model. A module knows:

  • What the outcome should be — a board pack, a gap assessment, a SAR narrative, a contract review, a market analysis. The module is shaped like the deliverable, not like a chat log.
  • Which approach fits — the right reasoning depth, the right knowledge sources, the right framework anchor (regulation, methodology, professional standard).
  • What the output should be — Markdown plus a structured JSON payload, optionally exported as DOCX, XLSX, PDF, PPTX, Mermaid, board deck, plain-language summary, devil's-advocate review, regulator's-eye review. Same input, many export formats. No re-running.
  • What to consider — built-in checklists, integrity rules, sanity checks. The module surfaces what an experienced practitioner would surface.
  • How you and ANTON collaborate — review gates, approval steps, reviewer feedback loops. ANTON proposes; you decide. The audit trail captures everything.

The library

150+ modules across 50+ professional domains:

  • Compliance & financial crime — AMLR, GDPR, DORA, NYDFS Part 500, BSA / AML federal, Swiss AMLA, UK ECCTA, MAS 626, OFAC / OFSI, and more
  • Legal — Counsel's Desk with 22 expert personas, 8 modes (research / drafting / negotiation / litigation / opinion / memo / argument / strategy), citation tracking
  • Risk — Risk Atlas with seven-stage deterministic threat-path methodology, 25 industry packs
  • Markets & finance — self-learning trading research, ANTON 100 indices, predictions with calibration
  • Healthcare, education, NGO, creative, procurement, civic engagement, CRM — and 40+ more sectors
