What FutureChain is

A blockchain that speaks bank.

Built in Stockholm by people who spent fourteen years inside the financial system. Three architectural decisions distinguish it from every other chain.

01

Pre-mempool compliance

The Heimdall engine screens every transaction before it enters the mempool. Sanctions, AML, jurisdiction — all checked at the gate. Failed transactions never become permanent.

02

Native ISO 20022

Banks submit full PACS.008 messages — the same format SWIFT moved to in 2025. Hashes go on-chain (800 bytes); encrypted full messages live off-chain. Travel-Rule data preserved end to end.

03

Two-tier storage · GDPR-safe

99.5% storage reduction on-chain. Personal data remains erasable off-chain — solving the "right to erasure" conflict that makes most blockchains legally radioactive in Europe.
