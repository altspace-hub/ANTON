# ANTON capabilities — extracted from whitepapers + capability deck

## Big positioning ideas
- **APCI** — Artificial Professional Context Intelligence (deliberate contrast to AGI). The thesis: intelligence isn't the bottleneck — context is. Professional context compounds; intelligence commoditises.
- **"We gave the AI a proper professional education, so you don't have to be an AI expert to get expert results."**
- **"Not here to replace your colleagues. Here to become one."**
- **Three pillars: Work · School · Life** — same architectural foundation serves a compliance officer, a student, a citizen.
- **Powered by Claude + Mistral + GPT + Gemini + local Ollama** (model-portable).
- **Open source · Apache 2.0 · github.com/altspace-hub/ANTON**
- **Built by Daniel Bardun & FutureChain AB.**

## Key concrete numbers (use sparingly, they're impressive)
- 400+ expert modules across 50+ professional domains (capability deck) / 245+ modules across 57 expert areas (WP2)
- 40+ expert personas
- 30+ output formats (consulting-grade deliverable templates)
- 7-layer prompt system
- Six knowledge-source modes (AI-only → full integration)
- Six-dimensional Trust Score
- 13.4:1 ROI example: 37.5h saved/month at €2.81 cost
- 4 Discovery depth levels: Lite (15-30m), Standard (1-2h), Professional (3-4h), Expert (full day)

## The seven-layer prompt system & APCI architecture
- **Knowledge atoms** — discrete units (facts, insights, conclusions, recommendations) with confidence score, source trail, temporal validity, tracked relationships
- **Knowledge graph** — entities (clients, regulations, controls, risks, people, systems) with typed relationships
- **Retrieval system** — domain-aware ranking
- **Feedback loop** — every correction sharpens it
- **Pattern detection** — temporal correlations, entity convergences, cascade sequences, coverage gaps
- **Intelligence dashboard** — makes accumulated knowledge visible
- **Prompt assembly** — context becomes intelligence

## Earned Autonomy — the AI Orchestrator
- Four phases of trust earned through demonstrated competence (not configuration)
- Immutable safety limits regardless of trust level
- Reasoning trails — full auditability
- Pattern engine
