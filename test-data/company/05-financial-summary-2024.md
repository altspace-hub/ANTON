## 6. EU VAT OSS Position — 2024

### Overview
KitchenBox EU AB is registered for the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) scheme since July 2021 (registration number EU372009841). B2C sales shipped to non-Swedish EU customers are subject to the destination country's VAT rate. B2B cross-border sales to VAT-registered businesses are zero-rated (reverse charge).

VAT is declared and paid quarterly via the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket) OSS portal.

### OSS Compliance Notes
- Distance selling thresholds no longer apply (EU-wide threshold abolished July 2021)
- All B2C EU cross-border sales subject to destination VAT via OSS
- B2B sales to VAT-registered customers: invoice ex-VAT, buyer applies reverse charge (verified via VIES before invoicing)
- Swedish domestic sales taxed at SE standard rate 25% via regular SE VAT return (momsdeklaration)
- No separate VAT registrations in any other EU member state
