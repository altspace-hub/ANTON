## Procurement Policy Notes

- **Supplier concentration risk**: SUP-001 (Güter Stahl) = 28% of direct procurement. Board aware; alternative German knife supplier being evaluated (Q2 2025).
- **FX risk**: JPY/USD exposure from SUP-002 (Tanaka). Hedged via quarterly FX forwards via Handelsbanken. SEK exposure from SUP-007 (NordicStore) — not hedged (domestic functional currency).
- **Single-source SKUs**: KB-KN-004 (Tanaka), KB-CW-004 (Fonte Royale), KB-GT-001 (Holzwerk), KB-GT-003 (Präzision) — each has only one supplier. Continuity risk reviewed annually.
- **REACH / PFOA compliance**: All cookware and bakeware suppliers provide annual PFOA-free declarations. Stored in ANTON knowledge base (Compliance folder).
- **Payment runs**: Every 15th and last day of month. Jonas Weber approves. Maria Santos counter-signs for amounts >€25,000.
