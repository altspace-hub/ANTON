## Business Model

KitchenBox EU is a direct-to-consumer (D2C) e-commerce retailer specialising in high-quality kitchen equipment. The company sources products from manufacturers in Germany, Italy, Portugal, and Japan, and sells exclusively online to consumers and small hospitality businesses across the EU.

**Revenue streams:**
- 92% B2C (individual consumers)
- 8% B2B (restaurants, catering businesses, culinary schools)

**Geographic split (2024 revenue):**
- Sweden: 28%
- Germany: 24%
- Netherlands: 16%
- France: 12%
- Denmark: 7%
- Finland: 6%
- Belgium: 5%
- Other EU: 2%
